- Slide 1: Some days make him want silence. - Zuv sits in a parked car after a bad workday, office bag and laptop beside him, shoulders tired, city/office lines fading behind the windshield.
- Slide 2: Then he remembers her waiting. - His phone lights up with Aachu's warm message: ghar aa jao? The car is still, but the air starts changing around him.
- Slide 3: Chai. Drama. One long story. - Aachu waiting at home with chai, expressive face, cozy lived-in little chaos, and one long story already loaded before he enters.
