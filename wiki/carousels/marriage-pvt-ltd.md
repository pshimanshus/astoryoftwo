## Slide Flow

- Slide 1: Every marriage is a two-person company
with too many departments. - Wide doorway view inside a lived home. Aachu and Zuv cross in opposite directions while already handling multiple household tasks: Aachu carries a small shopping bag and keys; Zuv holds a plain budget notebook while looking for something. They exchange one familiar knowing glance. The home is the office; no literal corporate props.
- Slide 2: FINANCE
One says, “Budget.”
The other says, “Sale mein tha.”
Both have receipts. - Over-the-shoulder dining-table view. Zuv points to one number line in a blank budget notebook. Aachu calmly slides a sale receipt beside it, then taps his own unnecessary expense on a second receipt. His raised eyebrow drops while she suppresses a smile.
- Slide 3: LOST & FOUND
“Kahan hai?”
One points.
Five minutes later, roles reverse. - Deep-focus living-room scene. Aachu searches beside a console for her keys while Zuv silently points to them in plain sight. In the background his phone rests on the sofa arm he has just checked, and Aachu has already noticed it. The visual promises the immediate role reversal.
- Slide 4: TRAVEL
One packed the charger.
The other remembered the passport. - Low wide view over an open suitcase. Aachu holds up a neatly coiled charger triumphantly. At the doorway Zuv raises the passport she forgot. Their eyes meet across the suitcase with equal sheepish satisfaction.
- Slide 5: LEGAL
Both have screenshots.
Neither has the full context. - Tight overhead and over-shoulder hybrid view at the table. Two phones enter from opposite sides, each held by its owner and angled toward the other. Their free index fingers meet near the center as both present harmless contradictory evidence. Faces remain partially visible with amused, stubborn expressions. Phone screens show only soft abstract message shapes, never readable UI or invented text.
- Slide 6: IT SUPPORT
Wi-Fi nahi chal raha?
One checks the router.
The other finds the loose plug. - Low close view near the media console. Zuv crouches beside the router checking its buttons while Aachu follows the cable and reconnects the loose wall plug. Router lights switch on; he looks up and she gives a tiny satisfied shrug.
- Slide 7: EMERGENCY
Midnight hunger.
No departments.
Full team activation. - High-angle midnight kitchen climax. Aachu opens the cupboard and passes ingredients while Zuv sets a pan on the stove and reaches for plates. Their eye-lines are task-focused, their body distance has contracted, and all objects move through a clear shared workflow.
- Slide 8: HEAD OFFICE
Nobody is in charge.
Both think they are. - Symmetrical medium view during post-cooking cleanup. Aachu wipes the counter while simultaneously pointing Zuv toward the plates; Zuv carries the plates while simultaneously pointing her toward the loose cupboard door. They notice their mirrored instructions and begin laughing.
- Slide 9: No fixed roles.
Just two people covering
each other’s shift. - Quiet floor-level release in the kitchen. Aachu and Zuv sit shoulder-to-shoulder sharing the midnight food from the two plates. The earlier keys, charger and two face-down phones appear naturally at the periphery as subtle callbacks. Their posture is relaxed and familiar; the departments have disappeared.
