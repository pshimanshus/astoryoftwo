## Slide Flow

- Slide 1: we talk about healthy love,
but we still learn it mid-fight. - After a small argument in a lived-in room, Aachu and Zuv sit on opposite edges of the same sofa or floor rug. Two cups sit untouched between them. Their bodies are turned away, but their feet or hands still angle toward each other. Clean upper-middle wall/paper space for text.
- Slide 2: we talk about patience,
but sometimes our tone arrives first. - Kitchen counter moment: one partner sets a spoon or cup down a little too firmly while the other freezes mid-action. No shouting, no cruelty; the visible tension is in hands, posture, and the too-loud object.
- Slide 3: we talk about listening,
but sometimes we wait for our turn. - Over-shoulder scene at a small table: one person is speaking softly while the other holds a breath, fingers curled around a napkin or phone, clearly preparing a reply instead of fully receiving. Keep faces gentle and human.
- Slide 4: we talk about giving space,
but still leave the door half open. - Doorway view: one partner sits alone on the bed or floor needing space, while the other is visible only as a quiet hand, shoulder, or shadow near a half-open door. The door gap becomes the relationship receipt.
- Slide 5: we talk about saying sorry,
but some apologies come as tea. - Object-led repair: a warm cup of tea is placed beside the other person's hand after silence. The cup, steam, lowered eyes, and small distance do the apology. Avoid making tea a generic prop; make it the apology object.
- Slide 6: we talk about forever,
but some nights we only repair today. - Late-night floor or bed-edge scene: both partners are tired, sitting closer now, maybe folding a blanket, sharing water, or picking up the same small mess. The big word forever becomes one ordinary repaired night.
- Slide 7: maybe love is noticing the pattern,
and choosing softness sooner. - Morning-after payoff: the room is calm, the half-open door is now open, the two cups are together, and Aachu and Zuv share a small ordinary touch or glance. No grand pose. The visual says they came back softer.
