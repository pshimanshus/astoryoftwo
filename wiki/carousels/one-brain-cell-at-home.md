## Slide Flow

- Slide 1: Some couples share one brain cell. - A cozy Indian apartment living-room scene in warm evening light: Aachu and Zuv stand near a soft sofa like two very serious detectives, one cushion already lifted, both leaning in with focused eyes while the phone and remote are visible in the scene but not yet noticed. Their body language says this is a shared investigation, not one person being mocked.
- Slide 2: Phone kaha hai? - Aachu searches beside the sofa with exaggerated seriousness while her phone with a tiny heart sticker is clearly in the same hand she is using to gesture. Zuv watches from beside her, amused but not announcing the answer yet, letting the comedy breathe.
- Slide 3: It was in her hand. - Zuv gently points with one finger toward the phone already in Aachu hand. Aachu freezes for one beat, half guilty and half laughing, with the sofa cushions now slightly messy. The joke lands as affectionate recognition, never insult.
- Slide 4: Remote kaha hai? - The reversal: Zuv now searches for the remote with the same grave seriousness while the remote is plainly in his own hand. Aachu watches him with delighted deadpan expression, enjoying that the one brain cell has changed owners.
- Slide 5: It was in his hand. - Aachu points at the remote in Zuv hand. Zuv looks down at it, caught, with a small helpless grin. The frame mirrors slide 3 so the audience feels the equal reversal and the no-judgement contract between them.
- Slide 6: Sofa still got checked. - Even after both reveals, Aachu and Zuv keep checking the sofa together. Two cushions are lifted, one cushion rests on the floor, the phone and remote are already visible and safe, and both are still invested in the useless investigation as if it matters.
- Slide 7: Home is where no one acts smart. - Final warm sofa scene: Aachu and Zuv collapse into the sofa together, laughing softly among one or two messy cushions. The phone and remote are safe between them or on the table. The scene feels tender because no one had to perform competence; both are allowed to be fully themselves.
