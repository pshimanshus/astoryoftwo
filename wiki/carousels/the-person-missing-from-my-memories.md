## Slide Flow

A phone between them displays a restrained non-legible 3-by-4 photo grid: ten distinct thumbnails visibly show Aachu alone in different clothes and places; none visibly contains Zuv seated beside her. Aachu's scrolling finger stops and she turns from the phone to study Zuv's face while he still studies the grid. An open envelope nearby contains four small prints, each also showing Aachu alone. The phone remains a small physical object, never an enlarged interface. Keep both upper bodies and four owned hands readable, with generous clean upper-middle paper for exact text.
- Slide 2: Not because you weren’t there. - Closer three-quarter overhead view in the same present-day space and wardrobe. Aachu taps one solo thumbnail of herself beside moving cartons. The selected image remains visible at the centre while its rectangular edge dissolves organically into a larger warm-watercolor apartment-doorway memory around the seated couple: two suitcases and Zuv entering with his own suitcase become visible outside the original phone-photo crop. Zuv leans closer in the present. The transition must feel like a storybook page revealing the complete lived scene, never a split screen, collage, portal graphic, floating gallery, or app mockup.
- Slide 3: You were there when I chose courage. - Full wide cinematic memory at the threshold of a modest unfamiliar apartment in a new city. Aachu stands just inside with new keys in her palm, excited and visibly uncertain. Half-open cartons and her suitcase locate the move. Behind her, Zuv enters carrying his own suitcase, not carrying hers: her courageous decision has changed both their lives and he has come along rather than merely helping her settle. She looks back to him; he gives a small grounded go-on expression; her key hand moves toward the lock. The door opening is the visible consequence.
- Slide 4: You made me visible from places no one could see. - A specific creative-work scene in a small improvised studio. Aachu works in the lit recording and creation area beside an unfinished illustrated page and notes. A small camera preview facing Zuv visibly frames only Aachu. Zuv stands outside that preview behind the camera and soft light, adjusts the light, watches her framing, and gives a restrained timing cue; Aachu looks toward him rather than directly into the lens. The full illustration contains both people while the recorded rectangle contains only her. Keep equipment sparse and make their shared labour legible without UI, social-media chrome, or a generic applause pose.
- Slide 5: You held the world outside for a minute. - Late-night ordinary city pause after a difficult day. Their car is safely parked at a quiet broad roadside under warm streetlights. Aachu stands beside the passenger side folded with real laughter, fully turned toward Zuv. Several feet away Zuv makes a playful gesture while taking her photograph; a small natural camera preview contains Aachu laughing alone while the full illustration shows Zuv causing and recording the moment. Her closed work tote and face-down phone remain inside the open passenger door, physically outside this breathable minute. No food, chai, scenic overlook, grand date, traffic danger, or tourist landmark.
- Slide 6: You never made love the reason I stayed behind. - Departure threshold outside their building at early morning, with a waiting cab safely pulled to the curb. Aachu faces the open rear door with one travel suitcase and backpack, leaving alone for an opportunity. Zuv stands behind and slightly to the side after tightening one loose backpack strap; his hand has already released it. Their hands separate in the lower centre while her feet and suitcase remain decisively oriented toward the cab. She looks back at him; he holds the sadness without blocking her path. The visible consequence is that the way forward stays open.
- Slide 7: Sometimes the person missing from your memories is the person who made them possible. - Return to slide 1's exact true top-angle room, wardrobe, cushion, phone, and envelope, now slightly wider and warmer. The same ten solo thumbnails and four solo prints remain visible nearby. The phone is propped against the cushion facing outward with an unmistakable countdown light but no readable UI. Aachu pulls Zuv beside her into the new live camera composition; she looks at him and he looks back, surprised and warm, neither posing. For the first time the active camera frame visibly contains both people. Preserve generous clean upper 42 percent for the long exact text and keep the prints secondary, never a scrapbook collage.
