## Slide Flow

- Slide 1: You were there.
I was here. - High dreamy overhead public square or open court on warm ivory paper. Zuv is a small steady figure on one side, Aachu is a small expressive figure far away on the opposite side. Their locations clearly prove the text: he is there, she is here. Long soft shadows and faint walking paths show distance without sadness. No decorative hearts, no ribbons, no red string, no UI, no copied dark screenshot styling.
- Slide 2: You lived your life.
I lived mine. - Warm elevated sidewalk view. Aachu and Zuv move through the same city on separate routes, living their own ordinary lives. Aachu carries expressive movement, denim, light top, red scarf or red bag; Zuv walks calmly in navy/black and tan with watch. Their shadows nearly align but do not meet. The scene proves parallel lives, not romance yet. No symbolic charms or tied-thread motifs.
- Slide 3: Until the day
our paths crossed. - Soft crosswalk from above. Aachu and Zuv step into the same crossing from opposite sides. Their shadows meet first, then their eye-lines begin to notice each other. A scarf edge or bag strap may move naturally with Aachu, but nothing symbolic is attached to them. The crosswalk itself is the story evidence: paths finally crossing.
- Slide 4: Two people.
One moment. - Airy transit or elevator interior with both bodies close enough for the public world to narrow. Their hands pause near the same rail or pole because the carriage shifts; they nearly touch or lightly brush. Aachu has small jewelry and a red scarf/bag edge, Zuv has a watch and dark/navy sleeve. The moment is quiet and real: two people, one pause. No heart ribbon, no tied fingers, no thread charm.
- Slide 5: Years apart.
Seconds to fall. - Escalator or station stair scene. Aachu leans into Zuv in a quiet side-hug while the escalator carries them upward. Zuv steadies her shoulder or bag with active gentle care. Other commuters are soft silhouettes. The scene compresses years of separate life into sudden closeness. The fall is emotional, not literal; keep bodies natural and flattering.
- Slide 6: Different stories.
Same ending. - Final waist-level walking scene on one shared path. Aachu and Zuv are seen from mid-back or waist down to naturally joined hands, walking the same direction. Aachu has red scarf or red bag edge, denim, light top; Zuv has dark/navy sleeve, tan trousers, watch. No string, no ribbon, no charm tied to fingers. The ordinary handhold and shared direction prove the ending.
