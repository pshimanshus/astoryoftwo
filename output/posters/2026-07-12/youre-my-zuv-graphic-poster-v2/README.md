# ZUV — contemporary graphic wall poster

This is the redesigned poster version of the high-performing “You’re my Zuv”
illustration. Unlike the first framed conversion, this version uses a complete
poster-design system: dominant display type, a limited high-contrast palette,
screen-print texture, a graphic sun, simplified mountains, and an integrated
border.

## Print file

- Format: 50 × 70 cm portrait
- Pixels: 5906 × 8268
- Resolution metadata: 300 DPI
- Exact text: `ZUV` and `MY LIFE, IN ONE WORD.`
- Palette: midnight indigo, saffron, warm cream, coral
- Suggested paper: 200–250 gsm matte or textured fine-art stock

## Identity gate

- The approved rear-view pose from `zuv-one-word/source-art/slide-02-source.png`
  was supplied as the identity/composition reference.
- His curly black hair, her long wavy black hair, white/ivory clothing, body
  proportions, seated pose, and joined hands remain the identity anchors.
- Neither face is visible or invented, eliminating facial drift.

## Generation record

Created with the built-in image-generation workflow as an original contemporary
graphic poster. The design borrows general poster principles from the supplied
reference shop but does not copy any individual product or artist’s composition.
