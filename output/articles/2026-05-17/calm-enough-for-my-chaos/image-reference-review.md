# Image Reference Review

## Decision

Use all seven carousel images because each one carries a distinct story job.
Do not group them at the end. Place each image immediately after the section it
emotionally proves.

## Image Placement Plan

1. `slide-01.png`
   - Placement: Hero image after the opening line and subtitle.
   - Story job: Establish the central hook: he did not marry peace.
   - Alt text: A hand-drawn illustration of Anchal as a bright chaotic spark beside a calm smiling Himanshu, with the text: He didn't marry peace.

2. `slide-02.png`
   - Placement: After the paragraph about saying "mujhe kuch nahi hua" while clearly crying.
   - Story job: First proof that the humor comes from recognizable emotional contradiction.
   - Alt text: Anchal says mujhe kuch nahi hua while clearly crying, as Himanshu calmly holds a handkerchief and water.

3. `slide-03.png`
   - Placement: After the section "The shoes were never about shoes."
   - Story job: Show love as being known in the middle of a dramatic exit.
   - Alt text: Anchal threatens to leave while barefoot, and Himanshu quietly holds her shoes.

4. `slide-04.png`
   - Placement: After the breakfast/morning moods section.
   - Story job: Move from one dramatic moment into daily married rhythm.
   - Alt text: Three tiny moods of Anchal appear before breakfast while Himanshu waits calmly with chai and snacks.

5. `slide-05.png`
   - Placement: After the section explaining that his calm is active care.
   - Story job: Reframe Himanshu as warm, amused, and steady rather than passive.
   - Alt text: Himanshu smiles warmly among small signs of Anchal's chaos, with the text: And somehow, he still smiles like this is normal.

6. `slide-06.png`
   - Placement: Before the emotional safety section.
   - Story job: Slow the essay down from comedy into tenderness.
   - Alt text: Anchal and Himanshu sit softly together on terracotta steps, with the text: Maybe love is not finding calm.

7. `slide-07.png`
   - Placement: Final image after the closing thesis.
   - Story job: Leave readers with the save/share line.
   - Alt text: A small spark and a steady diya flame sit side by side, with the text: Maybe it's finding someone calm enough for your chaos.
