# Outline

## Opening Hook

Open with "He didn't marry peace." Turn it from a joke into a confession about real married love.

Image: `slide-01.png`

## Emotional Problem

People imagine love as calm, aesthetic, and perfectly regulated. Real couple love is full of tiny contradictions, hunger, tears, moods, and dramatic exits.

## Deeper Turn

The point is not that one person is difficult and the other person suffers. His calm is active love: noticing, waiting, preparing, learning, and choosing softness.

Image: `slide-05.png`

## Emotional Safety

Peace can sometimes mean silence or avoidance. Emotional safety means being allowed to have weather without being shamed for it.

Image: `slide-06.png`

## Final Payoff

She is the spark. He is the steady flame. Love is not finding someone without chaos. It is finding someone calm enough for your chaos.
