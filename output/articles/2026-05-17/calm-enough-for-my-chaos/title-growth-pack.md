# Title And Growth Pack

## Recommended Title

Calm Enough For My Chaos

## Subject Line Candidates

1. Calm Enough For My Chaos
2. He Didn't Marry Peace
3. The Kind Of Love That Holds Your Shoes
4. He Married The Whole Weather Report
5. Maybe Love Is Not Finding Calm

## Preview Text

A small essay on dramatic love, quiet care, and the person who learns your weather.

## Suggested Slug

calm-enough-for-my-chaos

## Reader Comment Prompt

Which one are you in love: the chaos, the calm, or somehow both?

## Substack Note

Maybe love is not finding calm. Maybe it is finding someone calm enough for your chaos.

## Alternate Notes

- Send this to the person who knows where the shoes are.
- The best kind of love does not make your feelings smaller. It gives them somewhere safe to land.
- He did not marry peace. He married the whole weather report. And somehow, that became home.
