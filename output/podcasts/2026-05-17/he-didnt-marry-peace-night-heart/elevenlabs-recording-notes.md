# ElevenLabs Recording Notes

## File To Record

Use `script.md`.

## Recommended Voice Direction

Use a warm, intimate, late-night narrator voice.

Avoid:

- announcer energy
- motivational speaker tone
- overly dramatic romance narration
- fast social-media voiceover pacing

Aim for:

- soft smile during funny lines
- slower, more reflective final third
- conversational breaths
- gentle pauses after short standalone lines

## Suggested Voice Settings

These are starting points, not hard rules:

- Stability: medium-high
- Similarity: medium
- Style exaggeration: low to medium
- Speaker boost: on, if available

## Recording Notes For Cleanup

After generation, listen for:

- Hinglish pronunciation of `mujhe kuch nahi hua`
- whether "He didn't marry peace" feels intimate rather than comedic only
- enough pause after "He stays. He smiles. He learns the forecast."
- final line should feel soft, not triumphant

## Output Naming

Recommended final audio file:

`he-didnt-marry-peace-night-heart-v1.mp3`
