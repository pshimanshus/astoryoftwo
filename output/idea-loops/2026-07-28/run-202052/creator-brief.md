# Creator concept brief

Selected candidate: `run-202052-i1-b-museum-of-almost`

Working title: **The Museum of Almost**

## Concrete moment

During a clear-out, one partner puts a worn folder in the discard pile. The other partner rescues it. Inside are not achievements or romantic souvenirs, but unfinished attempts from different eras: crossed-out applications, rough sketches, abandoned plans, and first drafts. The owner feels embarrassed and tries to close the folder. The keeper quietly arranges the pieces by date. After seeing a life rather than a pile of failures, the owner adds today's unfinished draft.

## Why a cold viewer recognizes it

Many people have versions of themselves they consider unsuccessful or embarrassing. The visible contradiction is immediate: one person throws the folder away while the person who loves them treats it as worth saving.

## Format and visible proof

Strongest format: illustrated carousel.

The folder physically moves from drawer to discard pile and back. Each revealed artifact changes the emotional meaning. The chronology exposes time passing, and the final added draft proves the discovery changed the owner. The scene should remain readable with all text hidden.

## One-person send reason
