# She Was Not High Maintenance

Aachu's little needs, soft impracticality, and full-alive green-dress energy are not high-maintenance when Zuv notices early and turns the tiny discomfort into gentle care.

## Emotional Arc

universal high-maintenance anti-label -> Aachu fully alive on the balcony -> bare-feet green-dress proof -> Zuv notices before she asks -> care-without-shrinking thesis

## Slide Flow

- 1: She was not high-maintenance. - A universal relationship hook on the Ayatana balcony: Aachu in her green dress, barefoot and expressive, looking fully alive rather than difficult.
- 2: She was just fully alive. - Aachu moving through the balcony in green, hair and posture carrying soft main-character energy; the tiny practical chaos is loved, not judged.
- 3: Bare feet. Green dress. Tiny crisis. - Concrete proof beat: bare feet near the chair, green dress gathered carefully, a small shoe/sandal detail visible as the tiny crisis without making the shoe the whole premise.
- 4: He noticed before she asked. - Zuv notices the tiny discomfort and kneels with calm warmth to help with the footwear before Aachu has to turn it into a request; his posture is attentive, not performative.
- 5: Maybe love is care without shrinking. - Aachu and Zuv standing close after the moment is handled, the balcony soft behind them, with the feeling that care made her more herself, not smaller.

## Recommended Caption

some people call it high-maintenance.

but sometimes it is just being soft, dressed up, barefoot,
and wanting the moment to stay beautiful for one more second.

and sometimes love is the person who notices the tiny discomfort
before you have to turn it into a request.

maybe love is not asking someone to be easier.
maybe it is care without shrinking.
