# Main Kar Lungi

Aachu's 'main kar lungi' is not a request for distance; it is pride asking to stay whole, and Zuv's love is the quiet care that helps without making her feel small.

- 2: Translation: don't go far. - A simple public pathway scene where Aachu's body language says she can handle it, but her glance back says stay close; Zuv keeps the exact respectful distance.
