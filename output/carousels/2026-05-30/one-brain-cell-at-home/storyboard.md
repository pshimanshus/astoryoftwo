# One Brain Cell At Home - Storyboard

## Slide 01: Some couples share one brain cell.

Role: universal hook
Scene: A cozy Indian apartment living-room scene in warm evening light: Aachu and Zuv stand near a soft sofa like two very serious detectives, one cushion already lifted, both leaning in with focused eyes while the phone and remote are visible in the scene but not yet noticed. Their body language says this is a shared investigation, not one person being mocked.
Action: Both start the search together like a tiny search party.
Reaction: Their faces are over-serious in a recognizably silly domestic way.
Objects/hands: One cushion is lifted; the missing phone and remote are visible as planted comic receipts.

## Slide 02: Phone kaha hai?

Role: Aachu-specific setup
Scene: Aachu searches beside the sofa with exaggerated seriousness while her phone with a tiny heart sticker is clearly in the same hand she is using to gesture. Zuv watches from beside her, amused but not announcing the answer yet, letting the comedy breathe.
Action: Aachu asks for the missing phone while searching the sofa.
Reaction: Zuv notices the contradiction but holds the moment with a soft smile.
Objects/hands: Phone remains clearly in Aachu hand while the other hand lifts a cushion.

## Slide 03: It was in her hand.

Role: comic reveal
Scene: Zuv gently points with one finger toward the phone already in Aachu hand. Aachu freezes for one beat, half guilty and half laughing, with the sofa cushions now slightly messy. The joke lands as affectionate recognition, never insult.
Action: Zuv points out the phone with gentle timing.
Reaction: Aachu freezes, then begins to laugh at herself.
Objects/hands: The phone becomes the center of the reveal while the cushion remains displaced.

## Slide 04: Remote kaha hai?

Role: reversal setup
Scene: The reversal: Zuv now searches for the remote with the same grave seriousness while the remote is plainly in his own hand. Aachu watches him with delighted deadpan expression, enjoying that the one brain cell has changed owners.
Action: Zuv begins his own missing-remote search.
Reaction: Aachu sees the remote in his hand and holds the laugh.
Objects/hands: Remote is visible in Zuv hand while he checks under a cushion.

## Slide 05: It was in his hand.

Role: reversal reveal
Scene: Aachu points at the remote in Zuv hand. Zuv looks down at it, caught, with a small helpless grin. The frame mirrors slide 3 so the audience feels the equal reversal and the no-judgement contract between them.
Action: Aachu returns the gentle reveal by pointing at the remote.
Reaction: Zuv sees it and smiles with caught-in-the-act softness.
Objects/hands: Remote is centered as the reversal object.

## Slide 06: Sofa still got checked.

Role: escalation proof
Scene: Even after both reveals, Aachu and Zuv keep checking the sofa together. Two cushions are lifted, one cushion rests on the floor, the phone and remote are already visible and safe, and both are still invested in the useless investigation as if it matters.
Action: They continue the sofa search after the mystery is solved.
Reaction: Both are now smiling because the uselessness is the point.
Objects/hands: Cushions move; phone and remote stay safe as comic evidence.

## Slide 07: Home is where no one acts smart.

Role: earned thesis
Scene: Final warm sofa scene: Aachu and Zuv collapse into the sofa together, laughing softly among one or two messy cushions. The phone and remote are safe between them or on the table. The scene feels tender because no one had to perform competence; both are allowed to be fully themselves.
Action: They settle together after the nonsense.
Reaction: Both laugh with equal ownership of the joke.
Objects/hands: Phone and remote rest safely nearby as receipts of the story.
