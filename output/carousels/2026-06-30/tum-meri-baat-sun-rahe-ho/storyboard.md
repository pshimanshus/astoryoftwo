# Tum Meri Baat Sun Rahe Ho

Status: prompt and visual plan repaired after failed generation review.

## Locked Copy

1. Our romance is very simple.
2. She asks,  
   “tum meri baat sun rahe ho?”
3. I say,  
   “haan haan, bilkul.”
4. Then she asks,  
   “maine kya bola?”
5. And suddenly,  
   my entire life flashes before my eyes.
6. But still, every day,  
   we choose this nonsense.
7. Because love is not always peaceful.  
   Sometimes love is just getting caught beautifully.

Shot ladder:

6. Soft aftermath: phone face-down, sheepish smile, tension melts.
7. Playful table action: Aachu lightly taps/flicks him with a folded napkin, both smiling.
