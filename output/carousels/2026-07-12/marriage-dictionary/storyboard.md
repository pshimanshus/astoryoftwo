## Visual Story

The visual arc is one home-language progression:

1. The house introduces the private language through everyday objects.
2. Both partners are caught in the `bas 5 minute` lie.
3. Leaving gets interrupted by one more household task.
4. The cupboard/shelf joke proves shared home geography.
5. Food becomes a missing-you signal.
6. A covered plate after a tiny fight becomes the emotional receipt.
7. Home lands as wordless understanding through simultaneous small care actions.
