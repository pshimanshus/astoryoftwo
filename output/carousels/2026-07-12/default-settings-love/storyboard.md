# Default Settings Love

Forever is not staged as a grand vow here. It is staged as the quiet way one
person becomes the default inside ordinary adult life.

The reference strip's concept is lifelong love, but this version avoids the
old Q/A copy, older-couple echo, and repeated pretty montage. The visual system
is small infrastructure: keys, phone muscle memory, bedside rhythm, paperwork,
and home entry rituals.

1. **LOVE DOESN'T ALWAYS FEEL BIG.**  
   Object-only overhead. Two keyrings, two cups, a folded blank paper, and two
   hands entering the frame.

2. **SOMETIMES IT BECOMES A DEFAULT.**  
   Wide hallway. Aachu and Zuv move through a leaving-home ritual they already
   know by heart.

3. **THE FIRST NAME YOU TYPE.**  
   Close over-shoulder phone moment. The screen stays blank/no readable UI; the
   action is muscle memory, not phone-interface content.

4. **THE EXTRA KEY YOU STOP HIDING.**  
   Detail shot. Both hands at a key hook, trust becoming shared access.

5. **THE SIDE OF THE BED THAT KNOWS YOU.**  
   Morning bedroom rhythm. Shared charger, lived-in pillows, hair tying, folded
   blanket or cup movement. Mutual rhythm, not one partner saving the other.

6. **THE PERSON LIFE CALLS FIRST.**  
   Proof slide. A calm paperwork/clinic desk, blank form lines, one pen, close
   hands. Adult love without melodrama.

7. **FOREVER, IN SMALL SETTINGS.**  
   Distant evening doorway. Two keys, two pairs of shoes, warm home light. The
   last line makes the whole deck sendable.

Proof-first slide: slide 6, because it carries the strongest emotional turn and
the highest risk of becoming too dramatic, too generic, or too form/text heavy.
