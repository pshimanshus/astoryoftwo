# Versions Of Us

Concept: Love is not loving one frozen version of someone. It is staying
curious and tender as life keeps editing both people.

Visual principle: make the carousel feel like an archive of changed versions.
Objects are receipts, not decoration. The older-couple echo appears only in the
final slide.

1. **LOVE IS NOT LOVING ONE VERSION.**  
   Drawer/table archive: old movie ticket, key, bracelet, messy note, tiny
   photo strip. Aachu and Zuv only partly visible at the edge.

2. **THE FIRST VERSION IS EASY.**  
   Early-date detail: two cups, nervous hands, Aachu mid-laugh, Zuv trying to
   look calm. Close, intimate, slightly funny.

3. **THEN LIFE STARTS EDITING YOU.**  
   Moving boxes, changed plans, crossed-out calendar, new city/routine hints.
   Both in motion.

4. **SOME DREAMS GET REPLACED.**  
   One old plan packed away without tragedy. Aachu folds an outfit or old note
   while Zuv tapes a new label on a box.

5. **THE RIGHT PERSON LEARNS THE UPDATE.**  
   Mutual care in one frame: he knows her new thing; she adjusts something for
   him. Two small actions, no speech, no savior pose.

6. **STILL CHOOSING. STILL LEARNING.**  
   Young couple walking away from the archive/table/room; older couple far in
   the background doing the same gesture. Subtle future echo only here.

Proof risk: slide 5 is the riskiest because it must show mutual updating
without becoming generic care or Zuv-handler framing.
