# Versions Of Us - Message Repair V2

## Diagnosis

The current proof set has the right archive mood, but the emotional argument is incomplete.
It says people change, but it does not name the fear underneath that change:

> What if you only loved the version of me you met first?

The repaired deck needs to make one clearer claim:

Love is not preserving the first version of a person. It is learning the person life keeps making next, without asking the old one to come back.

## Repaired Slide Spine

### Slide 1 - Cover
Copy: `LOVE IS NOT LOVING ONE VERSION.`

Visual: A drawer or table of personal objects from different eras: a small photo strip, bracelet, key, folded note, old ticket, half-visible calendar page. Aachu and Zuv appear only as partial edges or hands, as if the archive belongs to them.

Message job: Establish the thesis. This is not a nostalgia deck; it is about refusing to freeze someone.

### Slide 2 - First Version
Copy: `THE FIRST ONE IS THE EASIEST TO LOVE.`

Visual: Early-date detail scene: two cups, nervous hands, Aachu mid-laugh, Zuv trying to look calm, a tiny almost-spilled spoon or napkin moment. Close, specific, human.

Message job: Show why the beginning feels simple. The first version arrives with less history, fewer compromises, and more mystery.

### Slide 3 - Life Intervenes
Copy: `THEN LIFE STARTS EDITING YOU.`

Visual: Moving boxes, crossed-out calendar, half-packed shelves, a new city/routine hint, one old plan still visible but not centered. Both are in motion.

Message job: Make change feel external and inevitable, not a relationship failure.

### Slide 4 - The Loss Inside Change
Copy: `SOME DREAMS DON'T COME WITH YOU.`

Visual: Aachu folds or sets aside an old outfit, note, or dream-object while Zuv labels a new box nearby. The object is treated gently, not thrown away.

Message job: Add the ache. Change is not just growth; some imagined futures quietly end.

### Slide 5 - The Missing Emotional Receipt
Copy: `THE RIGHT PERSON NEVER ASKS FOR THE OLD YOU BACK.`

Visual: Mutual care in one frame. An old photo or old-object sits on the table, but Zuv is making space for Aachu's current need/routine instead of pointing back to it. Aachu simultaneously adjusts something for him. No rescue pose.

Message job: Say the thing the first proof implied but never delivered. Staying means not making someone audition as their old self.

### Slide 6 - Payoff
Copy: `THEY KEEP LEARNING WHO YOU BECOME.`

Visual: Young Aachu and Zuv leaving the archive/table/room together. In the far background, an older couple echoes the same small gesture, subtle and quiet. A blank page or empty drawer space suggests future versions still coming.

Message job: Land the promise without using "forever" or "always." The final feeling is active curiosity, not static devotion.

## Visual Rule For Regeneration

- Keep the archive-of-versions feeling.
- No Q/A copy structure.
- No repeated older couple motif across the deck.
- No gallery, beach, or bookstore unless the object is doing story work.
- Every slide should contain one story object that proves the message.
- Text must be exact and legible.
- Preserve Aachu and Zuv identity from the selected reference bundle.
- Use warm ivory/off-white paper, muted watercolor-and-ink, tiny `@a.storyof.two` top-right.
