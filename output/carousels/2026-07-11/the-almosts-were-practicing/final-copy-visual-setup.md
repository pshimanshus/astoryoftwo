# The Almosts Were Practicing

## Final Copy

1. We didn’t miss each other.  
   We were just not ready yet.

2. Same city.  
   Different timing.

3. You were becoming you.  
   I was becoming me.

4. Wrong turns.  
   Late buses.  
   Streets we crossed alone.

5. Maybe love doesn’t arrive late.  
   Maybe it waits for both people.

6. When we finally met,  
   it didn’t feel sudden.

7. It felt like everything  
   had been walking there.

## Visual Setup

Core visual system: separate lives across time slowly becoming one shared path.

Rules locked for the final set:

- Warm A Story watercolor-and-ink street/path aesthetic.
- 1080x1440 Instagram carousel post frames.
- Exact handwritten slide text baked into each image.
- Tiny `@a.storyof.two` brandmark in the top-right.
- No extra signs, labels, UI arrows, carousel dots, logos, or random lettering.
- Before slide 7, Aachu and Zuv do not make direct romantic eye contact.
- Outfits change across the sequence so the story reads as time passing, not one repeated afternoon.
- Slide 6 uses the restaurant almost-meet context: Aachu eating, Zuv entering, recognition not yet forced.
- Slide 7 is the first connected frame: shared direction, hand-hold, no dramatic pose.

## Slide Visual Roles

1. Crosswalk near-miss: separate paths, shadows almost crossing.
2. Same city timing: she exits a quiet cafe/bookstore area, he arrives elsewhere.
3. Becoming: two self-contained personal-growth lanes on a promenade.
4. Rhythm: wrong turns, bus stop, forked streets, separate movement.
5. Turn: open plaza waiting-space, shadows converging.
6. Almost meet: restaurant/burger context, she focused on food, he entering.
7. Payoff: walking together down one shared path.
