# Things I Want To Do With You

Visual system: **Little Futures, Mostly Faceless**.

The deck borrows the reference's quiet romantic wish-list rhythm, but translates it into A Story of Two watercolor scenes where hands, objects, posture, and small saved details carry the emotion.

## Slide Flow

1. **Things I want to do with you:**  
   Hands interlocked upward against meadow/sky. Faces hidden. This opens the wish-list without copying the screenshot.

2. **Spend a whole Sunday in bed...**  
   Top-down bed coffee, croissants, soft sheets, hands only. Domestic quiet does the work.

3. **Order one of everything...**  
   Candlelit dessert table, spoons meeting, hands almost touching. Partial silhouettes only if needed.

4. **I want to hold hands during drives...**  
   Clean inside-car crop, not a dashboard illustration. Hands interlocked over a simple center console, road softly visible, small tote/store cue only.

5. **I want to cook dinners with you...**  
   Balcony/garden dinner from behind. Zuv serves or reaches so the scene has active care.

6. **My love language is thinking...**  
   Zuv in a shop holding a small object because he knows Aachu would love it. Aachu is implied by his noticing.

7. **And quietly collecting those moments...**  
   Warm room, saved notes/photos/receipts/object pile, shared hands. The list becomes a private archive.

Generate slide 04 first. It best tests the faceless identity system, hand anatomy, ordinary-romance proof, text placement, and A Story paper tone.
