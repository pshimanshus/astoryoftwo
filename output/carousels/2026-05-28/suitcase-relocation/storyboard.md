## Emotional Arc

## Slide Flow

- 1: Some couples don't pack. They relocate the house. - Wide bedroom packing-room scene: Aachu and Zuv stand on opposite sides of an open suitcase, both proudly surrounded by absurd but believable trip piles; clothes, pouch, cables, shoes, and toiletries are visible while both look convinced this is normal.
- 2: She packed "just options." - Aachu kneels beside the suitcase holding three outfit options against herself, with more folded outfits and a dupatta nearby; Zuv watches from the floor with an amused side-eye, hands paused over his own packing pile.
- 3: He packed every charger except the one they needed. - Zuv sits seriously with a tangle of real phone chargers, adapters, earphones, and a power bank, looking confident; Aachu leans in with a judging look while the one needed phone cable is clearly missing from the pile.
- 4: They sat on the suitcase. - Both Aachu and Zuv sit on top of the bulging suitcase together, knees up and hands gripping the edges, trying to zip it with full teamwork while the suitcase pushes back.
- 5: Still forgot toothbrushes. - Hotel bathroom arrival scene: Aachu and Zuv stare at an empty toothbrush cup in silence while the huge suitcase sits open behind them, packed with everything except toothbrushes.
- 6: Nobody blamed each other. Only the zip. - Close-medium bedroom or hotel-floor scene: both point at the stubborn suitcase zip with offended innocent faces, standing shoulder to shoulder so the blame lands on the zip instead of either person.
- 7: Maybe love is two overpackers blaming the zip. - Soft final floor scene: Aachu and Zuv sit beside the half-closed suitcase, tired and laughing, one hand from each still resting near the zip; the mess feels shared, affectionate, and chosen.
