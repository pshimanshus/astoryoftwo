# Kitchen Forgiveness Glitch - Visual Storyboard

Status: LOCKED 29/30 STORY VERSION

## Scene 1

Tiny hook text: **She was still angry.**

Wide ordinary kitchen. Aachu is alone, scrubbing an already-clean cup or counter
spot too hard. The room is normal; her hand pressure is not.

Feeling: after-fight energy living in a chore.

## Scene 2

Thought bubble: **Aayega toh maaf kar dungi.**

Close/medium shot. Her face has softened slightly, but her hand still grips the
cloth/cup too tightly.

Feeling: forgiveness has started in her head, but not in her body.

## Scene 3

No copy.

Without realizing it, she sets his cup/glass/chai space ready on the counter.
She still looks guarded. The softness is only in the action.

Feeling: her hands have forgiven him first.

## Scene 4

No copy.

Doorway cut. Zuv enters. Aachu freezes near the prepared cup before turning.
He is normal and unaware.

Feeling: the room changes. The audience knows something he does not.

## Scene 5

Speech bubble: **AB AAYE HO?**

She turns fast and shouts with the cloth still in hand. Zuv freezes at the
doorway with keys or bag in hand. Familiar, theatrical, not cruel.

Feeling: mouth failed the forgiveness plan.

## Scene 6

Tiny reveal text: **Cup ready tha.**

Aftermath freeze. Zuv notices the cup/glass already waiting for him. Aachu looks
away, still annoyed, exposed by her own care.

Feeling: the payoff is visual. The cup tells the truth she will not say.
