# Plate Stack Marriage Test

Status: storyboard repaired after creator correction on 2026-05-28. Copy is not locked.

## Baseline

This is a story conveyed through carousel slides, not a line carousel.

The deck should read as a small domestic scene:

`dinner -> both done -> his normal kitchen instinct -> silent plate handoff -> she reads it -> she stacks hers too -> phone + kitchen walk`

No best husband. No best wife. No couple-goals moral. No chore lecture.

Important repair: Zuv does **not** ask, "will you keep this?" The move is more
ordinary than that. He simply hands/slides his plate to Aachu because she is
also done eating. Aachu accepts the move, stacks her own plate on top, tells him
to keep both, then goes to her phone.

## Scene 1

Context text: **When you're finally married...**

Wide home dinner table. Both are eating. The kitchen is visible in the back.
Their plates are close to finished, but nobody is performing a joke yet.

Feeling: normal after-dinner laziness.

## Scene 2

Thought bubble: **rakh deta hoon.**

Zuv is done. He touches/lifts his plate and looks once toward the kitchen. Aachu
is also nearly done or has just finished, so her plate is visibly ready too.

Feeling: his first instinct is normal and boring.

## Scene 3

Tiny thought beat: **wait...**

He notices Aachu's plate is done too. His body stops halfway between getting up
and staying seated. His plate is in his hand; his eyes move from kitchen to her
plate.

Feeling: the lazy married-life shortcut arrives without a lightbulb or speech.

## Scene 4

No copy.

He casually hands/slides his own plate toward Aachu, like this is a very normal
handover between two people who are both done. He does not ask. He does not act
clever.

Feeling: the move is visible, but still casual enough to feel real.

## Scene 5

No copy.

Aachu has his plate now. Her own empty plate is right beside her. She looks at
his plate, then at him. Deadpan. Her phone is near her hand, waiting.

Feeling: she has understood the entire shortcut before he has finished acting it.

## Scene 6

Tiny speech bubble: **dono rakh do.**

She calmly stacks her own plate on top of his plate and gives the stack back /
pushes the stack into his hands. One hand is already reaching for her phone.
Zuv freezes: not angry, not noble, just outplayed.

Feeling: she does not fight the shortcut; she redirects it.

## Scene 7

No copy.

He walks to the kitchen carrying both plates. She is already on her phone at the
table like nothing happened.

Feeling: normal consequence. No moral needed.

## Copy Rule

Approved tiny copy candidates only:

- `When you're finally married...`
- `rakh deta hoon.`
- `wait...`
- `dono rakh do.`

Everything else is deleted unless the visual fails without it.
