# Storyboard

## Slide 1

**Copy:** Some people don’t tell stories.

**Job:** Universal hook

**Visual:** Aachu mid-story in a warm open room, seated but almost standing from excitement, hands lifted mid-air, eyebrows alive, one clear speech swoosh beginning from her. Zuv is present but quieter at frame edge, already looking at her instead of away.

**Swipe reason:** The viewer recognizes the storyteller archetype immediately.

## Slide 2

**Copy:** They perform the whole incident.

**Job:** Aachu-specific reveal

**Visual:** Aachu reenacts the incident with full body language: one hand points to an imaginary person, the other acts out the scene. Tiny motion marks and two small reaction sparks around her. Zuv’s posture stays relaxed and amused, watching the performance rather than interrupting.

**Swipe reason:** Escalates from telling to performing.

## Slide 3

**Copy:** With side plots nobody asked for.

**Job:** Comic escalation

**Visual:** Aachu’s main story branches into three tiny illustrated mini-vignettes floating around her: a tiny road/auto moment, a tiny phone-call bubble, a tiny food or bag detail. Zuv remains in the main frame, listening, not lost inside the mini-scenes.

**Swipe reason:** Adds the most taggable joke beat.

## Slide 4

**Copy:** And a timeline only they understand.

**Job:** Pattern proof

**Visual:** Aachu confidently explains a winding dotted timeline across the air with arrows that loop and double back. She is certain. Zuv is quietly amused, following the line with his eyes and a soft smile, one elbow on knee.

**Swipe reason:** Turns chaos into a recognizable relationship rhythm.

## Slide 5

**Copy:** But he listens like it matters.

**Job:** Gold Zuv-role slide

**Visual:** The frame calms down. Zuv leans in, phone face-down beside him, shoulders angled toward Aachu, soft attentive smile. Aachu is still expressive but smaller in motion now, feeling received. Lots of warm negative space around them.

**Swipe reason:** This is the emotional reversal: his listening makes her performance safe.

## Slide 6

**Copy:** Maybe love is not asking for the short version.

**Job:** Tender turn

**Visual:** A quieter two-shot: the mini-scenes fade into soft pale outlines behind them. Zuv’s hand rests near the phone, still untouched. Aachu’s expression softens from performance into being known. The room feels still.

**Swipe reason:** Bridges the joke into a love thesis.

## Slide 7

**Copy:** Maybe it’s someone who stays for the full story.

**Job:** Save/share thesis

**Visual:** Final close, gentle frame: Aachu still talking with one small hand gesture, Zuv beside her by choice, close enough to show commitment but not posed. A single long story-thread line curls around both of them into a tiny heart-like loop, subtle and low-contrast.

**Swipe reason:** The final line becomes sendable because the proof trail earned it.
