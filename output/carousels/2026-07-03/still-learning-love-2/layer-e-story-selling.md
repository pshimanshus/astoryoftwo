## Emotional Machine

A cup lands too firmly on the kitchen counter before patience arrives. At the table, one listens while also preparing a reply. One asks for space, but the bedroom door stays half open. The apology does not come as a speech; it comes as a warm cup of tea placed beside a quiet hand. Later that night, they do not solve forever, they only repair today. By morning, the door is open, the cups are together, and they choose softness sooner than last time. -> The public image becomes a private receipt. -> The pretty part is only proof; the relationship is the subject.
