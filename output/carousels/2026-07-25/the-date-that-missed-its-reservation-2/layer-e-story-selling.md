## Emotional Machine

Rain, traffic, and wrong turns make the intended restaurant across the divided road frustratingly close but inconvenient to reach. Their irritation is really about having missed ordinary days together. Zuv gives Aachu a minute without creating distance. They turn the failed reservation into roadside chai beside their safely parked car. The final beat is a nonlinear callback to before departure: entirely outside the apartment in the corridor, the front door fully closed, Aachu visibly turns back from the lift direction, and both test the exterior handle together. Car only. No scooter or helmets. Exactly two visible people. -> The public image becomes a private receipt. -> The pretty part is only proof; the relationship is the subject.
