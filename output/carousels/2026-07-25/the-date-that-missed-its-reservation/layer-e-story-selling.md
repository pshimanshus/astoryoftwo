## Emotional Machine

If the private meaning is absent, the scene stays decorative. -> Before leaving for their date, Aachu and Zuv close the apartment door from the outside. Zuv checks the exterior handle twice; Aachu rolls her eyes, takes two steps toward the lift, then visibly returns and checks the same fully closed exterior handle with him. They travel by car, park safely on the quieter service lane across a divided road to avoid traffic, and can see the intended restaurant nearby but inconvenient to reach. By the third reroute they are irritated. When Aachu says don't talk to me, Zuv gives her a minute without leaving. He realizes the disappointment is about missing ordinary time together. They choose roadside chai; Aachu steals a sip from his glass while he protects the biscuits. The plan fails, but the date does not. -> The public image becomes a private receipt. -> The pretty part is only proof; the relationship is the subject.
