# Road Hi Galat Hai

Locked 8-slide visual lane restored from creator feedback.

## Slide Flow

### Slide 1

On-image text:
```
left?
seedha?
```

Visual: High aerial watercolor road scene: a big flyover rises ahead while one tiny confusing left cut runs alongside it. A small car is near the split. The road geometry must be instantly readable on a phone: left cut beside the flyover, not hidden. Place the two text labels near the two choices, not centered: left? beside the service-lane cut and seedha? beside the flyover/straight path.

### Slide 2

On-image text:
```
shit.
left hi tha!
```

Visual: Rear exterior road view: the car is already climbing onto the flyover. The missed tiny left cut is clearly visible behind and below them, separated by a divider. The viewer should immediately understand they missed the turn. Keep the couple mostly implied through the car, not a full couple pose.

### Slide 3

On-image text:
```
I told you.
Always listen to me.
I am always right.
```

Visual: Interior car scene. Aachu is in the passenger seat turned toward Zuv in full playful see-I-told-you mode, one hand lifted mid-lecture, expressive and dramatic but lovable. Zuv is driving, quiet, eyes on road, calm and not annoyed. Dashboard and windshield hint at the flyover outside. Avoid making her mean or making him morally superior.

### Slide 4

On-image text:
```
where?
seedha.
```

Visual: Driver POV through windshield or high 3/4 road scene: another equally confusing flyover/service-lane situation, but this time a tiny right cut peels away from the straight path. Zuv slows down. Aachu confidently says seedha. Put where? near Zuv/driver side and seedha. near Aachu/passenger side or straight lane.

### Slide 5

On-image text:
```
not you.
not me.
this.
```

Visual: The car has gone straight and the right cut has been missed. Both are now looking at the road/map/turn together. Their fingers point outward toward the confusing road/map, not at each other. The visual must show the shift: no one is blaming each other, both are blaming the impossible road design. Place not you. near one partner, not me. near the other, and this. near the confusing road/map/turn.

### Slide 6

On-image text:
```
+17 minutes
```

Visual: Map-like road loop, tiny car taking an absurd detour around flyovers and service lanes. The route should loop comically without becoming a phone UI screenshot. Treat it like a hand-drawn map in watercolor ink with one tiny car and a ridiculous long loop. Text +17 minutes is written on the road/route itself.

### Slide 7

On-image text:
```
wrong turn.
same team.
```

Visual: They finally reach the destination near the parked car. One is annoyed, one is laughing, both carrying the same stupid story now. Their body language is together: close enough to feel like a team, not posed. A faint flyover/road in the background reminds us what happened.

### Slide 8

On-image text:
```
Tag the person you have jointly blamed a flyover, Google Maps, or one impossible service lane with.
```

Visual: Tiny parked car in the lower frame. Aachu and Zuv stand beside it, both looking back at the road/flyover like it personally betrayed them. Large clean warm paper space for the long CTA text; keep the road/flyover visible but not busy. This should feel like an illustrated shared complaint, not a poster.
