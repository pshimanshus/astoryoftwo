# Some Couples Come With Private Captions

Status: Fresh current-flow package, proof required before full generation.

## Visual System

Private Caption Frames Across Many Moments. The deck borrows only the mechanic:
small labels that reveal how two people privately read the same scene. It does
not copy Friends frames, actors, sets, or blocking.

## Slide Flow

1. `some couples come with private captions`
   - City crossing after coffee. Public hook.

2. `being dramatic / taking it seriously`
   - Home doorway with half-packed tote. Her drama becomes a real mission to him.

3. `stressed / listening first`
   - Rainy window. His active role is phone-down listening.

4. `excited / happy she's smiling`
   - Night street-food stall. Her joy becomes his joy.

5. `doesn't like it / agrees`
   - Grocery aisle. Tiny alliance, same-team comedy.

6. `acting tough / knows he's soft`
   - Winter market. The private caption goes both ways.

7. `bad joke / favorite sound`
   - Parked car or roadside stop. Ordinary intimacy.

8. `the right person captions you kindly`
   - Rooftop blue hour. Universal thesis.
