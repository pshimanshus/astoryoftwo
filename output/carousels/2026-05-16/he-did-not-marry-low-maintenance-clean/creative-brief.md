# Clean Creative Brief

title: He Did Not Marry Low Maintenance
channel: @a.storyof.two
date: 2026-05-16
mode: identity-only concept-led carousel

## Hard Reset

The previous package failed because it treated the identity image as a story
photo and then filled the missing story with generic template beats. This
rebuild does not do that.

There are no story photos for this carousel. The only image reference is
`identity_images/aachu_zuv.png`, and it must be used only for Aachu and Zuv face
consistency. Do not copy the photo background, pose, clothing, lighting, or
event context unless a slide explicitly asks for it.

## Creative Thesis

Zuv did not marry someone easy, quiet, polished, or "low maintenance." He
married Aachu's expressive, dramatic, lovable emotional weather. The love is not
that she becomes smaller. The love is that he stays gentle without making her
feel like too much.

## Voice

Affectionate, desi, lightly funny, never insulting. The joke is not "wife is a
problem." The joke is "this is exactly the chaos he chose."

## Visual System

- Warm off-white paper background.
- Soft hand-drawn flat illustration.
- Imperfect black outlines, matte muted colors.
- One clear visual beat per slide.
- Aachu: expressive face, dramatic-but-soft body language.
- Zuv: calm posture, warm eyes, steady presence.
- Text is short, handwritten, integrated into the illustration.
- Brandmark `@a.storyof.two` sits tiny at bottom-right.

## Slide Spine

1. Anti-ideal: he did not marry low maintenance.
2. Specific Aachu truth: "mujhe kuch nahi hua" while visibly emotional.
3. Daily-life proof: one small plan becomes many backup plans.
4. Rhythm: drama, snack, silence, smile.
5. Payoff: the chaos becomes home.

## Non-Negotiables

- Use identity image only for face consistency.
- No fake "source photo" cues.
- No copy-card layout.
- No stock Indian couple.
- No cluttered suitcase gag unless it is drawn simply.
- No cruel or tired wife-husband humor.
- No final-ready claim until model-native images exist in `final/`.
