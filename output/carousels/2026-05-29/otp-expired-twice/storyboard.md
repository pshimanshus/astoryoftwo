# OTP Expired Twice - Storyboard

Status: handoff ready, final images pending

Human truth: Some couples are not calm under tiny pressure; they become a two-person panic system that somehow works.

## Slide 01 - 30 seconds.

Story job: hook

Visual: Cozy home evening, low table or sofa edge. Aachu holds her phone close to the viewer with a large harmless app verification timer reading 30 seconds. Zuv sits beside her with his own phone showing a tiny OTP message. Both realize the countdown has turned a normal task into a mission.

Emotion: tiny household pressure, funny alertness, shared focus

## Slide 02 - jaldi bolo.

Story job: setup

Visual: Zuv reads the OTP from his phone too calmly while Aachu types into her phone too fast. Both phones are visible, with the countdown still implied on her screen.

Emotion: comic urgency, too-calm reading versus too-fast typing

## Slide 03 - 8 tha ya 3?

Story job: comic escalation

Visual: The digit confusion lands physically. Zuv's finger partly covers one digit on his OTP phone. Aachu's thumb freezes mid-type over her phone. Both eye-lines snap to the screens at the same time.

Emotion: sudden doubt, comic freeze, shared micro-panic

## Slide 04 - expired.

Story job: failure beat

Visual: Aachu's phone shows one large simple expired state. Both stare at the screen in silence like the timer personally betrayed them.

Emotion: silent betrayal, tiny domestic defeat

## Slide 05 - resend. breathe.

Story job: repair and teamwork

Visual: They physically reset. Aachu and Zuv move closer side by side, both phones angled toward each other on the table, hands nearly overlapping as they prepare the resend. The retry looks like a tiny mission-control operation.

Emotion: reset, teamwork, shared seriousness over a silly thing

## Slide 06 - submitted.

Story job: tiny victory

Visual: The app screen shows a simple submitted checkmark. Both release the tension at once with a tiny celebration: eye contact, shared exhale, small relieved smile, shoulders dropping.

Emotion: tiny triumph, relieved eye contact, ordinary task made dramatic

## Slide 07 - two people panicking correctly.

Story job: earned payoff

Visual: Both phones are face-down after the crisis. Aachu and Zuv lean back together laughing softly, like they survived something far bigger than a normal OTP. The cozy home scene feels complete and ordinary again.

Emotion: affectionate laughter, ordinary relief, shared ridiculousness
