# Main Kar Lungi

Aachu's 'main kar lungi' is not a request for distance; it is pride asking to stay whole, and Zuv's love is the quiet care that helps without making her feel small.

## Slide Flow

- 1: Main kar lungi. - Outdoor public threshold after a walk: Aachu steps ahead with chin-up independence and a confident hand gesture, while Zuv stays half a pace behind, attentive but not hovering.
- 2: Translation: don't go far. - A simple public pathway scene where Aachu's body language says she can handle it, but her glance back says stay close; Zuv keeps the exact respectful distance.
- 3: She wanted to do it herself. - Busy outdoor old-city market lane at golden hour: Aachu leads through the crowd with chin-up pride, managing the moment herself while Zuv stays half a step behind and slightly to the traffic side, matching her pace without touching or hovering.
- 4: He helped like it was nothing. - Same public crowd pressure, but Zuv quietly creates private clearance: he steps diagonally toward the traffic side and subtly holds space with one calm hand gesture so Aachu can pass first and keep the win.
- 5: Maybe love is care without making a scene. - Open courtyard at dusk: they walk together after the moment, her independence intact and his care invisible enough to feel natural.
