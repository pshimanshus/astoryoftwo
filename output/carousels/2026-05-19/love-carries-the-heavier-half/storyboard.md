## Recommended Caption

50-50 sounds fair until someone has to ask for the same small thing twice.

some days your half is heavier.
some days mine is.

and sometimes the fight is not about paani.
it is about wanting to be noticed before you have to make a whole case out of it.

maybe love is not keeping the perfect score.
maybe it is seeing when your person has less left,
and carrying the heavier half for today.
