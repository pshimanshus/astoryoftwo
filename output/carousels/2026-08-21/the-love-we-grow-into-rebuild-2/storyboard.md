# The Love We Grow Into Rebuild

Growing together does not mean never getting irritated. It means learning the difference between asking for space and asking someone to leave, then finding a small way back to each other.

## Recommended Caption

I do not want us to become the couple that never gets irritated. I want us to become the couple that knows the difference between space and leaving — and keeps finding a small way back. Sometimes, that way is chai.
