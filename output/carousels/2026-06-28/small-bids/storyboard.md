# Small Bids

Love often asks for attention in very small ways, and closeness is built by turning toward those tiny bids before they become quiet distance.

## Emotional Arc

tiny signals -> small asks -> original phone bid -> physical invitation -> role flip -> ten-second turning choice -> missed-bid ache -> closeness built in small moments

## Slide Flow

- 1: the smallest moments can make or break connection - Wide warm living-room scene in one airy frame: Aachu and Zuv are in the same room but not fully turned toward each other yet. Tiny signals around the room act as visual evidence: a phone glowing softly near a sofa cushion, an empty cushion with a slight handprint, a half-finished cup, a small glance across the room, and one person almost missing the other. Keep it simple, not a collage.
- 2: sometimes love asks in very small ways - Medium-detail sofa-side scene: Aachu sits with a phone in one hand, the other hand lightly patting the empty cushion beside her. Her body gives a tiny sigh and a small sideways glance toward Zuv. Show the phone, cushion pat, sigh, and glance through posture and object placement, not through UI or speech bubbles.
- 3: look at this - Over-shoulder close scene from behind Aachu as she holds her phone out toward Zuv to show him something. The phone screen is visible only as soft light or a tiny illustrated image shape with no readable UI. Zuv is turning from what he was doing, not fully there yet. Focus on her hand offering the phone and the hopeful eye-line.
- 4: come sit with me - Close detail shot of Aachu hand patting the empty sofa cushion beside her. The cushion compresses slightly under her palm. Zuv is entering as a cropped knee, hand, or shoulder at the edge, drawn toward the seat. Keep faces optional; the hand and empty cushion carry the scene.
- 5: you won't believe what happened today - Role-reversal medium scene: Zuv is now the one starting an animated story, leaning forward with one hand mid-gesture and a gentle excited face. Aachu is beside him, half in her phone for one beat, eyes not yet lifted. Keep it affectionate, not accusatory; the reversal is recognizable and funny-soft.
- 6: turning toward each other is a choice you make in ten seconds - The decisive ten-second choice: a phone lies face-down on the table in the foreground. One partner is physically turning their torso and shoulders toward the other; the other partner looks up and meets their eyes. Hands relax away from the phone. The scene must clearly show attention moving from screen to person.
- 7: it's never the big fights. it's the small "look at this" you didn't look at. - Quiet aftermath scene with no dramatic fight: the room is calm, the phone rests dim on a lap or table, and a tiny missed glance creates a small distance between them on the sofa. Use negative space to show that the hurt is small but real. No yelling, no argument pose, no melodrama.
- 8: closeness isn't built in big moments. it's built in the small ones you almost missed. - Final soft payoff: the once-empty cushion is now filled, the phone is face-down and away, and Aachu and Zuv sit turned toward each other in a relaxed close posture. One hand rests near the other, eyes attentive, room signals settled. Keep it warm, save-worthy, and specific to the small-bids story.
