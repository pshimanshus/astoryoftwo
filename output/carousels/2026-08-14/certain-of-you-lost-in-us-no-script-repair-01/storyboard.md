## Recommended Caption

I thought certainty would feel like knowing what came next.

But choosing you answered only the most important question. Life kept changing the scene, moving the furniture, and leaving whole pages blank.

Some days, love gave us no line to say and no mark to stand on.

I am learning that losing the script is not the same as choosing the wrong scene partner.

Commitment answered who. We are still learning how. 🤍
