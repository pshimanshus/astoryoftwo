## Recommended Caption

I think I expected certainty to arrive as a map.

But choosing you only answered one question—the most important one. The rest of our life keeps unfolding in rooms we have never entered before.

Some days we know exactly what we are building. Some days we sit in the middle of it, tired and mapless.

I am learning that being lost together is not the same as being wrong for each other.

Commitment answered who. We are still learning how. 🤍
