# More Than the Hurt

A repairable hurt becomes a physical obstruction: the chair representing Zuv's absence grows larger than him. The sequence does not erase the event; it moves the obstruction while leaving the cold dinner and two downturned memories in the room.

## Setup / Payoff Ledger

- Empty chair: missed presence → occluding hurt → occupied accountability → rotated out of their sightline.
- Phone and keys: held on arrival → set aside → remain inactive through conversation.
- Memory frames: three upright memories implied → all three down → one restored → two remain down.
- Dinner: cold at the rupture → still visible at the ending, preventing emotional erasure.

## Recommended Caption

I did not need the hurt to become smaller.

I needed it to stop becoming your whole face.

That took honesty. An apology that did not hurry me. And time.

Looking at you again was not forgetting what happened. It was remembering that what happened and who you are were not the same sentence.

This is for the hurts that can be repaired—not the ones that keep asking you to survive them.
