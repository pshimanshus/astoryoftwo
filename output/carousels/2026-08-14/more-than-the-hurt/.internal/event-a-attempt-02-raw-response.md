After Aachu waits through a missed dinner and Zuv arrives too late, she withdraws from visible memories overnight, but his quiet morning accountability leads her to reopen one small piece of their shared space and begin a cautious conversation with him.

Within the complete sequence, it reads as **temporary memory-darkening**, not an irreversible ending. The fact that two frames stay down keeps the reversal emotionally credible and incomplete.
