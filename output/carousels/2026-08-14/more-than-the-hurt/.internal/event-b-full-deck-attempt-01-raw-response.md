## Sequence verdict

**REPAIR.** Slide 1 establishes the missed dinner well, and slide 2 preserves the chair obstruction, but the causal sequence breaks afterward:

- Slide 3 never turns the three memories face-down.
- Slide 4 changes the table, room geometry, wardrobe, hand blocking, and emotional temperature.
- Slide 5 presents a static easel frame instead of the decisive partial lift.
- Slide 6 introduces the two downturned frames only at the end, changes the opening axis, relocates props, and resolves the couple too warmly.

The visible sequence therefore reads closer to “missed dinner → phone put away → Aachu arranges frames → the couple reconcile” than the locked “absence becomes obstruction → hurt darkens memory → accountability waits → one memory partially reopens → sober engagement begins.”
