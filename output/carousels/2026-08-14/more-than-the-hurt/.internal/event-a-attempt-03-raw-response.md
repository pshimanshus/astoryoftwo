Card 1

- Who/where: Aachu sits at a two-place dining table at night; Zuv has just entered through the doorway.
- Visible action: He stops with phone and keys in hand while she remains beside an untouched meal.
- Relationship/emotional state: Disappointment and distance. The burned-low candle, cold second plate, dominant empty chair, lack of contact, and Aachu’s gaze toward the vacancy imply a long wait or missed shared moment.
- Probable before/next: She prepared and waited; he arrived late. He must now either explain himself or remain outside the emotional space.
- Proof: Separate hands, crossed gazes rather than eye contact, the chair dividing them, and the diagonal wide view make absence the central fact.
- Uncertainties: The cause of his lateness and whether the phone caused the hurt are not established.

Card 2

- Who/where: Both remain in the dining room moments later.
- Visible action: Zuv deliberately places his phone face-down beside the keys.
- Relationship/emotional state: A tentative offer of attention, not reconciliation. His open free hand and mutual orientation suggest willingness, while the empty chair and its slats still preserve the barrier.
- Probable before/next: He has recognized the tension; next comes either an attempt to engage or a continued refusal from Aachu.
- Proof: The phone changes from active possession to inactive object; both look toward each other, but neither touches the chair or crosses the divide. The compressed camera view makes the obstruction feel stronger.
- Uncertainties: It is unclear whether putting the phone down is an apology, surrender, or merely practical.

Card 3

- Who/where: Aachu is alone in a dim hallway later that night.
- Visible action: She deliberately turns the last of three small frames face-down.
- Relationship/emotional state: Severe withdrawal and an attempt not to look at emotionally charged memories. Her attached hand and gaze make the act intentional rather than accidental.
- Probable before/next: No repair occurred at dinner; she retreats and symbolically closes off. The untouched, unbroken frames leave open the possibility of later reversal.
- Proof: All three frames ending face-down creates a strong visual shutdown; the isolated wide shot and absent partner intensify it.
- Uncertainties: The frames’ contents are never visible, so their connection to the couple is inferred. “The last of three” carries some breakup/finality coding despite the objects remaining intact and in place.

Card 4

- Who/where: Both return to the dining room the following morning; Aachu stands at the threshold while Zuv sits in the formerly empty chair.
- Visible action: He waits rather than approaching her.
- Relationship/emotional state: Accountability, caution, and unresolved distance. His open hands and upward gaze suggest availability without pressure; her threshold position shows she has not yet re-entered emotionally.
- Probable before/next: He remained or returned to face what happened; she must decide whether to enter the exchange.
- Proof: Occupying the once-empty chair directly answers Card 1. The persistent cold meal, dead candle, and inactive phone and keys keep the previous night present.
- Uncertainties: His motive and how long he has waited remain unstated.

Card 5

- Who/where: Aachu and Zuv are seated together in the morning dining room.
- Visible action: Aachu actively lifts one frame from face-down toward upright while choosing direct eye contact with Zuv.
- Relationship/emotional state: A voluntary, measured reopening. The unfinished frame movement conveys choice in progress; separate hands and the absence of touch prevent it from reading as instant forgiveness.
- Probable before/next: She has accepted the invitation to engage; next she may complete the rotation and begin speaking.
- Proof: Her attached hand supports the frame at forty-five degrees with its bottom edge still planted, making the state change mechanically legible. The unobstructed eye contact and fully visible hands rule out a mere posed-gaze reading.
- Uncertainties: The frame’s exact meaning remains inferred, and the outcome is intentionally not guaranteed.

Card 6

- Who/where: Both sit at adjacent sides of the same table in clear morning light.
- Visible action: They begin a restrained exchange while maintaining physical boundaries.
- Relationship/emotional state: Guarded reconnection, not complete repair. One frame upright while two remain down communicates partial progress.
- Probable before/next: Card 5’s choice has become sustained engagement; continued conversation and gradual repair are likely.
- Proof: The once-dividing chair has moved beside the table, their sightline is clear, hands remain open and separate, and the matched wide camera releases the obstruction established in Card 1.
- Uncertainties: Actual speech is inferred from gaze and seating because there is no unmistakable mouth or conversational gesture.

One-sentence sequence: A missed dinner leaves a chair-sized rupture between them; he puts distraction aside, she shuts away their memories, he returns to occupy the absence without forcing contact, she voluntarily begins reopening one memory and their sightline, and they start a cautious conversation with only partial repair achieved.
