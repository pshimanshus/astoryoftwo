## Recommended Caption

Some days, love is not the absence of anger.

It is the quiet fact that one difficult day does not get to narrate the whole relationship.

We were both tired. We both made it worse. And somewhere inside all that noise, we still reached for the same light.

A bad day was never the whole truth about us. 🤍
