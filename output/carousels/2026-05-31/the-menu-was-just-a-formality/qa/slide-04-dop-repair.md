# Slide 04 DOP Repair

Status: REPAIR LOCKED

Creator correction:

The proof failed because it tried to show everything: Pizza Bakery sign, couple
faces, menu, pizza, coffee, restaurant interior, and story text. The image
generation also did not reliably load identity/reference priority, and the
result did not feel premium enough.

## New DOP Principle

One slide, one visual verb:

> He places the hazelnut cold coffee before she asks.

Everything else is atmosphere.

## Correct Shot

Use a quiet medium-close table shot, not a wide restaurant tableau.

- Foreground: Aachu's open menu, lowered slightly, with her fingers still on it.
- Main action: Zuv's hand slides a tall hazelnut cold coffee into frame from
  the right, placing it exactly near her hand.
- Aachu: visible in soft three-quarter profile or partial face, caught-smiling
  because she has been understood.
- Zuv: only a soft partial profile, shoulder, forearm, or hand is needed; his
  action matters more than showing his full face.
- Background: blurred warm wood and one soft lamp or stripe-wall cue from
  Pizza Bakery. No marquee sign. No pizza. No readable menu text.

## Frame Grammar

- The glass is the receipt.
- The hand movement is the care.
- The lowered menu is the joke.
- Her caught smile is the emotional turn.
- Pizza Bakery is only a warm hint.

## Text

Repaired on-image text:

```text
He ordered before
the verdict.
```

The coffee itself proves what he ordered. Place the text in clean upper-middle
ivory space, away from faces, hand, and glass.

## Hard Blocks

- No pizza on slide 4.
- No Pizza Bakery sign on slide 4.
- No full table spread.
- No restaurant advertisement composition.
- No giant coffee product shot without the hand/action.
- No wide scene where the story action becomes tiny.
- No yellow/parchment paper.
- No generic Aachu/Zuv faces if faces are visible.

## Decision

Slide 4 should feel like a tiny cinematic insert from a date: the exact second
the usual order arrives before she admits it was always going to be the usual.
