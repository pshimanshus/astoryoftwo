# Storyboard V4 — The Route He Planned, The City She Found

status: LAYER_C_SELECTED_ROUTE

This replaces the cafe/doorway system. The carousel is now one continuous
evening mini-film. He has a route. She keeps finding the real story.

## Visual Grammar

- His world: map lines, phone calendar grids, notebook boxes, watch, calm gaze,
  blue-grey structure.
- Her world: keys, red accent, movement, wind, open hand, sudden detours,
  warm street light.
- Their world: thresholds, roads, reflections, rain, small pauses, the route
  becoming a memory.
- No zodiac symbols. No quote card. No repeated table scene. No cafe-table or
  two-people-sitting-across-a-round-table composition.

## Slide 1 — The Map Before The Night

Text:

```text
Capricorn man
Aries woman

he plans the future.
she starts it.
```

Scene:
Balcony or apartment landing, but not the old static doorway. Zuv is standing
with a folded city map/notebook route and phone calendar. Aachu is already at
the stairwell/exit, keys lifted, looking back with a grin. The city waits below.

Camera:
Wide 4:5 establishing frame. Zuv left with route lines; Aachu right at the
stairwell; open city negative space above for text.

Logic:
He brought the plan. She has already begun the evening.

## Slide 2 — The First Detour

Text:

```text
he says,
"let's think about it."

she says,
"too late."
```

Scene:
Auto-rickshaw stand or tiny ticket window on a city street, not a cafe. Zuv
pauses with one gentle "wait, think" hand, holding the route card. Aachu is
already leaning toward the driver/window, paying or pointing at the next stop,
phone in hand with abstract blocks only.

Camera:
Diagonal street composition with auto/ticket-window edge creating depth. Text
on blank wall/awning above. Faces visible.

Logic:
His caution arrives one beat after her decision.

## Slide 3 — The Route Gets Edited

Text:

```text
he makes the plan.
she becomes the plot twist.
```

Scene:
They stand by a wall map, street sign shape, or parked auto. Zuv shows the neat
route. Aachu draws one tiny red detour arrow across it or places a red sticker.

Camera:
Medium two-shot with the map between them. Text in upper sky/wall. Both faces
visible, her hand visibly changing the route.

Logic:
The plan is visible. Her plot twist is visible.

## Slide 4 — Under The Awning

Text:

```text
he worries in silence.
she fights out loud.
```

Scene:
Rain or sudden wind under a shop awning. Zuv quietly checks the route/sky,
worried but contained. Aachu speaks animatedly to the situation, one open hand
in the air, not angry at him. They are close under one shelter.

Camera:
Soft two-shot under awning; rain outside, dry space around them. Text on the
awning/wall negative space.

Logic:
They handle stress differently, but stay on the same side.

## Slide 5 — Crossing

Text:

```text
he brings stability.
she brings spark.
```

Scene:
Crossing a small street. Zuv steadies her by lightly guiding at the elbow or
fixing her sleeve as she laughs mid-step. She has motion; he has care.

Camera:
Romantic street-level medium-wide. Street lights blur behind them. Text in sky
or building wall negative space.

Logic:
He grounds her movement. She lights up his structure.

## Slide 6 — The Wrong Turn That Was Right

Text:

```text
he saves screenshots.
she follows instinct.
```

Scene:
Zuv holds phone with abstract saved map cards. Aachu points down a side lane
glowing with tiny lights. He looks from the phone to her direction, choosing to
follow.

Camera:
Over-shoulder or side two-shot, lane depth ahead. Text in upper-left night sky.

Logic:
His screenshots say one thing. Her instinct sees another.

## Slide 7 — The Street Light Dance

Text:

```text
he acts calm.
but he loves the chaos.
```

Scene:
A small night market / quiet lane with fairy lights. Aachu twirls or dances
with keys/red scarf/dupatta accent. Zuv stands or sits nearby, route notebook
closed now, watching with a private smile.

Camera:
Medium-wide, Aachu in motion, Zuv in stillness, both faces visible. Text in
upper night/ivory negative space.

Logic:
His calm is not detachment. It is love watching her be alive.

## Slide 8 — The Place Not On The Map

Text:

```text
somehow,
they make perfect sense.
```

Scene:
Quiet rooftop/overlook/steps at the end of the route. The city below. Zuv's
map is folded closed. Aachu's keys rest beside it. They sit close, shoulders
almost touching, both soft and settled.

Camera:
Final still frame. Wide enough for quiet. Text in upper sky. More silence than
props.

Logic:
The best part of the plan was the detour they made together.

## Prompt Rule

Each image prompt must include the exact text for that slide and must forbid
readable microtext anywhere else. If model text fails, regenerate the image;
do not switch to a textless visual-only workflow unless the creator approves a
local typography pass first.
