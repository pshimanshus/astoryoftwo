# Cinematic Storyboard V3

status: STORYBOARD_LOCK_BEFORE_IMAGE_GENERATION

This replaces the repeated cafe/tableau direction. The carousel should feel like
a small romantic Indian slice-of-life film: thresholds, glances, movement,
silence, and objects carrying emotion. Use broad modern Indian romance grammar,
not any exact living filmmaker's style.

## Non-Negotiable Frame Rules

- Text is designed into the frame, not added as an afterthought.
- Every slide must have a distinct situation, camera grammar, and emotional
  temperature.
- Both faces are visible unless a deliberate over-shoulder shot is specified.
- No repeated cafe two-shot across multiple slides.
- No zodiac symbols, quote-card design, meme design, or astrology infographic.
- No generated readable microtext on phone, notebooks, menus, mugs, signs, or
  clothes.
- The scene must read before the text.

## Visual Spine

The film is not "Capricorn vs Aries." The film is:

1. He makes life safe enough to begin.
2. She makes life alive enough to matter.
3. Their opposite tempos become chemistry.

Recurring visual motifs:

- His objects: notebook, phone calendar, chai, watch, tidy lines.
- Her objects: keys, red accent, moving hair/dupatta, open hand, doorway/road.
- Their shared motif: a threshold between stillness and motion.

## Slide 1 — The Doorway

Text:

```text
Capricorn man
Aries woman

he plans the future.
she starts it.
```

Scene:
Balcony doorway at blue-grey evening. Zuv sits left at a small round table with
chai, blank notebook/checklist, and a phone calendar with abstract colored grid
marks. Aachu stands right at the open doorway with keys, fully visible, smiling
back at him.

Camera:
Medium-wide 4:5 two-shot. Text in upper third. Zuv low-left, Aachu mid-right,
doorway as the threshold. Her open hand invites him; no wrist grab.

Logic:
He is planning. She is already leaving. He likes being interrupted.

## Slide 2 — The Booking

Text:

```text
he says,
"let's think about it."

she says,
"too late."
```

Scene:
Different cafe framing from the screenshot. Aachu is foreground-right, phone
tilted toward Zuv with only abstract colored blocks. Zuv is background-left,
mid-sentence, one hand paused in the air. The joke is in his interrupted hand
and her already-done smile.

Camera:
Over-table diagonal composition, not symmetrical. Text in upper-left wall
space. Both faces visible. Use shallow cafe depth: lamp, plant, glass, table,
but no readable menus.

Logic:
His sentence is still forming. Her decision has already happened.

## Slide 3 — The Red Mark

Text:

```text
he makes the plan.
she becomes the plot twist.
```

Scene:
Close tabletop planning moment at home. Zuv's neat checklist is in the lower
frame with abstract boxes. Aachu leans in beside him, not hidden behind him,
and places one tiny red heart/arrow sticker on the page.

Camera:
Intimate 4:5 over-table medium shot. Faces in upper-middle, hands and paper in
lower-middle. Text floats above them in clean ivory space.

Logic:
The plan exists. Her one small mark changes its direction. He notices and
smiles.

## Slide 4 — Different Weather

Text:

```text
he worries in silence.
she fights out loud.
```

Scene:
Home corridor or balcony corner after a disagreement. Zuv sits on a step or
leans against a wall, quiet, worried, eyes down then back up. Aachu stands near
him with open hands, expressive but not cruel.

Camera:
Balanced two-shot with a doorway/curtain splitting stillness and motion. Text
in upper-middle wall space. Physical distance is small enough to feel safe.

Logic:
They process fear differently, but they are not enemies.

## Slide 5 — The Sleeve

Text:

```text
he brings stability.
she brings spark.
```

Scene:
Street/cafe exit or balcony doorway. Aachu is mid-laugh, half-turning toward
the road. Zuv gently fixes her sleeve/bracelet/dupatta edge with careful hands.

Camera:
Romantic medium close two-shot. Hands are the emotional center; faces still
visible. Text upper third with breathing room.

Logic:
He grounds her without stopping her. She lights up the frame without becoming
a joke.

## Slide 6 — The Road

Text:

```text
he saves screenshots.
she follows instinct.
```

Scene:
Doorway before leaving. Zuv holds his phone with abstract map cards/screenshots.
Aachu points out toward a blue evening road or lights, face visible, excited.

Camera:
Medium-wide threshold shot. Interior warmth behind them, road/lights ahead.
Text in upper-left negative wall or sky. No readable UI.

Logic:
He prepared the route. She chooses the moment. Both are moving.

## Slide 7 — The Dance

Text:

```text
he acts calm.
but he loves the chaos.
```

Scene:
Balcony night. Aachu dances under string lights, fully visible and inside safe
frame, keys in one hand. Zuv sits with chai and closed notebook, watching her
with a private smile.

Camera:
Medium-wide 4:5 frame. Aachu diagonal motion on right/middle, Zuv stillness on
left. Text upper third. No cropped face or torso-only fragment.

Logic:
His calm is not distance. It is attention.

## Slide 8 — The Notebook Closes

Text:

```text
somehow,
they make perfect sense.
```

Scene:
Quiet balcony floor/steps after the movement. Aachu and Zuv sit close. His
notebook is closed. Her keys rest nearby. Phone face-down. Both faces visible,
soft, settled.

Camera:
Still final frame, medium-wide. Text in upper third. More silence than props.

Logic:
The plan and the spark have become home.

## Batch Rule

Generate only from this storyboard. If a candidate repeats the same cafe
composition, loses the text, crops a face, or turns the action into a generic
couple pose, reject it before packaging.
