## Rejected Direction

The generated V2/V3 direction is abandoned.

Even when the art used warm paper, Aachu/Zuv outfit anchors, minimal labels,
and soft @a.storyof.two styling, it failed the autopsy bar because the frames
became pleasant illustrated couple scenes instead of sharp relationship
receipts.

## Why It Fails

- The viewer notices the illustration before the relationship truth.
- The visuals are warm but not instantly taggable as "this is us."
- The labels carry too much of the joke.
- The scenes are soft tableaus rather than concrete behavior proof.
- Zuv often reads as calm/present instead of visually active in a specific
  funny way.
- The batch solves text accuracy through local overlay, but that does not solve
  the underlying creative problem.

## New Visual Law

Every future visual must pass the Carousel Performance Autopsy before image
generation:

- public contradiction any stranger understands;
- at least three concrete behavior receipts across the deck;
- one physically funny or visually surprising receipt;
- Zuv's love/action visible, not just patient;
- visuals prove behavior, not mood;
- final line/frame creates a tag/send reflex.

## Do Not Reuse

Do not use the V2/V3 generated images, contact sheets, prompt pack, or local
text-overlay workflow as visual references for the next attempt.
