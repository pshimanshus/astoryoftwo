# Saath rehte hain. Phir bhi tumhari yaad aati hai.

Nine-slide copy and proposed visual setup. Scenes are written for this concept,
not presented as a documented incident from your life.

## Slide 1

**Copy**

Saath rehte hain.
Phir bhi tumhari yaad aati hai.

**Visual setup**

Wide view through the living-room doorway. Aachu has just set a grocery bag on
the kitchen counter and turns toward Zuv; he is carrying a laundry basket into
the living room, looking toward the sofa where he will put it. They are only a
few steps apart, but their attention is travelling in different directions.
Keep their faces relaxed and occupied. The doorway divides the frame without
making them look estranged. Leave the upper wall clear for the cover.

## Slide 2

**Copy**

We talk all evening.

“Where does this go?”
“What should we eat?”
“Do we need anything for tomorrow?”

**Visual setup**

Closer view from inside the kitchen. Zuv has left the laundry basket on the
sofa and joined Aachu to unpack. He passes her a milk carton; she holds the
fridge door open and reaches to receive it. Their eyes follow the handoff.
The emptied grocery bag starts to fold flat on the counter. This is practiced
teamwork, with no person doing all the work or ignoring the other.

## Slide 3

**Copy**

Then you start telling me something.

“One second. Let me finish this.”

**Visual setup**

From beside Zuv's shoulder, see Aachu aligning the corners of a large bedsheet
on the sofa. He sits on the arm of the sofa, halfway through speaking, one
hand still raised in a small conversational gesture. She lifts a finger to ask
for a moment while keeping her eyes on the sheet. The basket from slide 1 is
now half-empty. She means to listen once she finishes.

## Slide 4

**Copy**

At dinner, it's my turn.

“Listen, today…”
“Tell me. I'm listening.”

But you're already on your way to the kitchen.

**Visual setup**

Side view along the dinner table. Dinner is over. Aachu has turned her chair
toward Zuv to begin her story. He rises with his own empty plate, head turned
back toward her but body moving toward the sink. Her plate stays on the table.
His response is sincere; the split between his gaze and movement shows why the
conversation is difficult. The newly folded sheet is visible on the sofa in
the background, establishing elapsed time and continuity.

## Slide 5

**Copy**

I almost say,
“Nothing. I'll tell you tomorrow.”

The little things feel harder to tell
once I've waited all evening.

**Visual setup**

Three-quarter view from just behind and to the side of Aachu, including her
profile and looking past her toward the sink. Zuv has set his plate down and
is reaching for the tap; his shoulders show ordinary end-of-day tiredness. In the foreground,
her hand has closed around her own empty plate, ready to carry it over and end
the evening too. Her interrupted attempt is visible through her lowered chin
and paused movement, without tears or a dramatic argument.

## Slide 6

**Copy**

“Can we leave these for a bit?
I miss you.”

“I've been waiting to sit with you too.”

**Visual setup**

Medium-wide diagonal from beside the table. Aachu has set her plate back down
and pulls the adjacent chair out for him. Zuv withdraws his hand before opening
the tap and turns back toward her. Her invitation interrupts the shared habit
of postponing time together. Both choose the pause; she makes the space and
he returns to it. His plate remains at the sink, hers on the table.

## Slide 7

**Copy**

I finally get to the bit
I wanted to tell you.

You laugh so hard
I have to stop talking.

**Visual setup**

Over Aachu's shoulder toward Zuv, now seated in the chair she pulled out.
She is midway through acting out her story with an exaggerated expression
and a small hand gesture. He bends forward laughing, eyes creased, one hand
resting on the table. She breaks into a smile at his reaction. The emphasis
is on a response actually happening, rather than a generic attentive pose.
Do not invent a third person or illustrate an unconfirmed anecdote.

## Slide 8

**Copy**

Then you pick up the story
you started on the sofa.

I'd almost forgotten.
You hadn't.

**Visual setup**

Reverse to Aachu's face from behind Zuv. He resumes the same small hand
gesture from slide 3, now completing his thought. She leans toward him with
her forearms resting on the table, eyes fully on him, an amused recognition
in her expression. Her hands have left the plate alone. This pays back the
conversation she postponed and gives him his own need and response.

## Slide 9

**Copy**

We went to bed later than we meant to.

I felt like I'd finally
spent the evening with you.

**Visual setup**

High three-quarter view of the bedroom. Both are in bed facing each other;
Aachu rests her hand on Zuv's forearm while he reaches with his other hand
to switch off his bedside lamp. They share a small, sleepy smile. Their bodies
and attention now point toward each other, answering the divided attention
of the cover. The lamp is still on in the pictured instant; the room is not
already dark while he switches it off.

## Visual continuity

- Proposed sequence: one evening, from groceries and laundry through dinner,
  conversation, and bed. Keep the same home clothes through slides 1-8; bedtime
  clothes may change on slide 9. Confirm wardrobe against actual identity
  references before any image generation.
- Use recognizable Aachu/Zuv likenesses from the actual identity bundle.
  Default canvas: 1080x1440. Keep the tiny @a.storyof.two top-right signature.
- Use the active cinematic observational watercolor-and-ink style, neutral
  off-white paper, and restrained denim, sage and terracotta accents.
- Evening light comes from the living-room lamp at frame left and the kitchen
  task light over the counter/sink. Bedroom light comes from the bedside lamp.
  Keep warmth local to lit surfaces rather than washing the paper yellow.
- Early compositions follow their separate tasks; later shots follow the
  other's face and response. Use doorways, shoulders and table edges for depth.
  Leave an uncluttered wall or paper area for copy in each frame.
- Track the basket, folded sheet, and both plates exactly as described. The
  dishes remain unfinished while they talk. No added phones, clocks, thought
  bubbles, symbolic threads, or decorative objects are needed.
