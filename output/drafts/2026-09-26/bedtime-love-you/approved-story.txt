They’re getting into bed. He says, “Goodnight. Love you.”
She answers, “Love you too,” while reaching for the light.
Then she stops. She’s answered him without even looking at him.
That is what she’s been missing.
She leaves the light on, turns toward him, and touches his arm.
“Suno.”
He turns back. “Kya hua?”
She takes a second to look at his face.
“I love you.”
He starts to answer automatically, then catches her expression. His face softens. He turns fully toward her.
“I love you too.”
She smiles, moves closer, and now they switch off the light.
