## Image Generation Guardrails

- Use ordinary domestic behavior, not romance poster posing.
- Keep each slide airy with warm paper and one clear action.
- Nancho is expressive and mischievous, not guilty or villainous.
- Himanshu is sleepy, amused, and actively participating, not passive.
- Cash/wallet is a proof object, not the whole story.
- No luxury interiors, cinematic lighting, perfect couple poses, floating
  hearts, glossy AI skin, or dreamlike romance.
- No theft-coded darkness. The tone is playful and affectionate.
- No crime framing, sneaking fear, angry reaction, helpless husband, or
  "wife stealing" implication. The visual must show shared permission.
- Avoid showing a large cash amount. Keep the amount small and comic.
- If using UPI/payment visuals later, avoid readable private numbers, account
  details, QR codes, or transaction IDs.
- Text must be integrated into the generated image.
- Generate native `4:5` Instagram post images and separate native `9:16`
  Reels/Stories images. Do not crop or resize one format into the other.
