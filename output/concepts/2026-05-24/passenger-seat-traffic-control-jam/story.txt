Passenger Seat Traffic Control

Approved carousel concept for @a.storyof.two.

Public hook:
He didn't marry a quiet passenger. He married mission control.

Story engine:
Some relationships are not one calm driver and one silent passenger. They are
one steering wheel and two opinions. She becomes the passenger-seat supervisor:
Maps says left in 300 meters, she says LEFT-LEFT-LEFT now. Her foot finds the
invisible brake. The AC has a committee. The playlist gets vetoed. He does not
make her the problem. He stays amused, drives safely, and asks, "Captain,
next?" The tenderness is that he turns the commentary into their shared
co-pilot language.

Locked slide copy:
1. He didn't marry a quiet passenger. He married mission control.
2. Maps said left in 300 meters.
3. She said, "LEFT-LEFT-LEFT now."
4. Her foot found the invisible brake.
5. The AC had a committee.
6. He still asked, "Captain, next?"
7. Maybe love is one steering wheel and two opinions.

Safety:
Do not make this a nagging wife or helpless husband joke. No unsafe driving,
steering-wheel grabbing, panic, anger, readable map UI, number plates, real
routes, or private car details. The point is shared rhythm: she is his favorite
co-pilot, not his problem.
