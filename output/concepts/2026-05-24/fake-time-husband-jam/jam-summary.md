## Emotional Machine

Universal truth:
Some couples do not solve lateness by fighting the clock. They build a shared
system around one person's expressive final five minutes.

Aachu/Nancho proof:
She says "5 minutes," has a tiny getting-ready crisis, and still believes she
is basically ready.

Zuv/Himanshu role:
He pads the timing, holds the missing earring, keeps the mood light, and becomes
the alibi instead of the audience.

Payoff:
Love is not just waiting. Love is knowing her time zone.

## Safety Locks

- Do not make it a nagging wife-is-late joke.
- Do not make Himanshu a passive waiter, driver, or saint.
- Show shared permission: she knows he pads the time, he knows she needs the
  buffer.
- Use concrete receipts: earring, keys, cab timing, hallway, final look, tiny
  crisis.
- Keep the route fresh: no money/wallet, no body/food, no private-language
  metaphor, no photo/glow premise.
