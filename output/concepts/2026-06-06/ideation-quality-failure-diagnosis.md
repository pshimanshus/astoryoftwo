## What Actually Failed

The workflow confused a recognizable trope for a strong @a.storyof.two concept.

`Own Plate Theory` was relatable, drawable, and structurally compatible with the
gold carousel machine, but it did not feel fresh, premium, creator-specific, or
worth posting after the amount of strategic analysis available.

The artifacts show score inflation:

- `Own Plate Theory` reached 29/30 after repair even after the creator rejected
  the idea.
- The room checked safety risks, scene clarity, and shareability, but it did not
  run a sufficiently harsh taste/originality screen.
- The selector rewarded "people will recognize this" without asking whether
  the idea was surprising enough to feel like A Story of Two IP.
- The replacement shortlist improved the structure, but still risks becoming a
  format wrapper instead of a lived story engine.

## Root Cause

The concept room is over-indexed on checklist completeness:

```text
public hook
-> Aachu behavior
-> Zuv active role
-> proof object
-> tender thesis
```

That checklist is necessary, but not sufficient. It can produce a concept that
is technically passable and emotionally bland.

The missing discriminator is:

```text
Would this feel fresh, ownable, and a little inevitable only for this couple,
or could any couple account post the same idea tomorrow?
```

If the answer is "any couple account could post this," the idea should not
score above 24/30 no matter how many structural boxes it checks.

## New Required Taste Gate

Before any concept receives 28/30 or creator-facing recommendation, add a
World-Class Taste Gate:

1. Novelty: Does the concept have a non-obvious angle, phrase-world, or staged
   turn beyond a common couple trope?
2. Creator-world specificity: Does it feel generated from Aachu/Zuv's actual
   recurring world, or merely from generic Indian couple relatability?
3. Cold-viewer spark: Can the first second create "I need to send this" rather
   than "yes, this happens"?
4. Proof density: Are there three visible receipts before the thesis, and does
   at least one receipt feel surprising?
5. Anti-template pressure: If the character names and art style were removed,
   would the idea still sound too close to generic relationship content?

Decision rule:

- If novelty or creator-world specificity is weak, cap the concept at 24/30.
- If the idea is mostly a known trope with a tender ending, return REWORK.
- If the selector cannot name what is surprising, ownable, and physically
  staged, return STOP.

## Immediate Operating Correction

For the next carousel jam, do not start from another abstract theme or common
domestic trope. Start from one of these only:

- a raw lived Aachu/Zuv incident;
- a recurring phrase with a specific consequence;
- an object that moves through the scene and changes the relationship state;
- a one-second contradiction with a visible reversal;
- a creator-supplied reference mechanic transformed into an A Story of Two
  stage scene.

Do not offer `Aachu Dictionary` as locked direction without first stress-testing
it against this taste gate. It may be a useful series format, but it is not yet
a strong enough concept by itself.
