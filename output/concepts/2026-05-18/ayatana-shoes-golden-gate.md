## Source Read

Visible beats: Aachu in a green dress/sari on a balcony, barefoot and in
main-character mode; Zuv in a white shirt kneeling to help with her footwear;
then the two standing close. The useful proof is not "shoes are romantic." The
golden-theme proof is: Aachu gets to stay expressive, soft, dressed-up, and
slightly impractical, while Zuv turns a small need into gentle care without
making her feel like a burden.

Primary risk: object-first or princess-treatment framing. "He put on her
shoes" by itself is too thin. The pass needs a universal relationship truth,
Aachu-specific spark, Zuv's active noticing, and a tender thesis.

## Selector Verdict

Winner: **She Was Not High-Maintenance** at **29.5/30**.

Why it beats the runners-up:

- It starts from a stronger universal shame than the shoe object: many people
  have been made to feel "high-maintenance" for tiny needs, emotions, outfits,
  timing, comfort, or impractical softness.
- It gives Aachu a clear expressive role without making her the joke. She is
  not helpless; she is fully herself.
- It gives Zuv an active love role: he notices early and acts gently.
- The shoes stay in their correct place: concrete proof, not the premise.
- The final thesis naturally connects back to the gold carousel: love does not
  require the expressive person to become smaller before they deserve care.

Calm Enough first-principles mapping:

```text
universal anti-ideal:
She was not high-maintenance.

Aachu proof:
Green dress, bare feet, tiny practical chaos, still soft and alive.

Zuv response:
He notices before she asks and kneels into the practical detail.

tender acceptance thesis:
Maybe love is care without making someone shrink.
```

## Public Slide-Copy Direction

Working 5-slide shape:

1. She was not high-maintenance.
2. She was just fully alive.
3. Bare feet. Green dress. Tiny crisis.
4. He noticed before she asked.
5. Maybe love is care without shrinking.

Caption stance:

```text
some people call it high-maintenance.

but sometimes it is just being soft, dressed up, barefoot,
and wanting the moment to stay beautiful for one more second.

and sometimes love is the person who notices the tiny discomfort
before you have to turn it into a request.

maybe love is not asking someone to be easier.
maybe it is caring for them gently, exactly as they are.
```

## Repair Notes For Runners-Up

- If using **Main Character, Little Details**, make sure Zuv is not reduced to
  a prop manager. His role must be emotional safety, not only logistics.
- If using **He Noticed Before She Asked**, sharpen the first slide so it has
  more contradiction. "The safest love notices" is warm but less swipe-stopping.
- Avoid **Princess Treatment**. It turns Zuv's active care into a generic male
  fantasy and weakens Aachu's agency.
- Avoid a shoes-first hook. The shoes should appear around slide 3 as evidence.
