Selected concept from the Golden Theme tournament: She Was Not High-Maintenance.

Aachu is in a green dress on the Ayatana balcony, barefoot and fully alive.
She is not difficult; she is soft, expressive, dressed up, slightly impractical,
and trying to keep the moment beautiful for one more second.

Zuv notices the tiny discomfort before she asks. He kneels to help with her
shoes/sandals without making it dramatic, without making her feel like a
burden, and without asking her to become easier.
