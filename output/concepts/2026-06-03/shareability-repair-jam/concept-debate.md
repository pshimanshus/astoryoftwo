# Shareability Repair Debate

date: 2026-06-03

## Calibration

The previous route failed because it confused recognition with shareability.
The new test is stricter:

> If a viewer sends this to their partner, what are they saying without adding
> a message?

## Selector

Winner: `Mummy Ko Mat Batana`

Why:

- strongest no-context tag behavior;
- desi, specific, and memeable without becoming a generic quote;
- gives both partners agency: one starts the tiny harmless mischief, the other
  becomes the alibi;
- creates physical receipts: bag, bill, tag, family call, deadpan nod;
- avoids the stale perfect-husband/enabler engine if both are visibly guilty.

## Rejected/Lower Routes

`Main Nahi Bol Rahi` is shareable but too close to angry-care/apology lanes.

`Nothing To Wear` is visually strong but can become a tired wife/outfit joke.

`Jo Karna Hai Karo` is highly taggable but has stereotype risk and needs taste
repair before public copy.
