# Roti Side Quest - Scratch Storyboard V3

Status: revised copy-lock draft for creator approval. Do not generate yet.

This is now a 4-screen storyboard. The previous 5-screen version stretched a
tiny table moment into too much narration. This version keeps it quiet and
drawable.

## Screen 01

On-image text:

```text
papa bol rahe the
```

Story job: establish where Zuv's attention actually is.

Visual: wide dining-table shot. Father is visible from side/back/foreground or
partly off-frame, speaking. Zuv is angled toward father, listening. Aachu is at
the same table but not framed as the punchline yet.

Hard fails:

- Zuv looks at Aachu.
- Zuv holds a bite.
- Father conversation is not legible.
- Text is missing or changed.

## Screen 02

On-image text:

```text
roti khul rahi thi
```

Story job: introduce the physical action without making it a feeding pose.

Visual: the roti is opening on/near Zuv's plate. Zuv remains oriented toward
father. His hands are empty, resting, or in a conversational gesture; no ready
bite in hand. Aachu notices the roti quietly.

Hard fails:

- Zuv has a torn bite in hand.
- The frame looks like he is offering food.
- Aachu snatches from his hand.
- Text is missing or changed.

## Screen 03

On-image text:

```text
bite uske paas chala gaya
```

Story job: show the quiet transfer.

Visual: Aachu has the bite and is eating it naturally. Zuv is still engaged
with father, not looking at Aachu. The table/plate shows the roti evidence.

Hard fails:

- Aachu looks guilty, greedy, or mocked.
- Zuv catches her gaze.
- Zuv is holding or offering the bite.
- Text is missing or changed.

## Screen 04

On-image text:

```text
baat phir bhi chalti rahi
```

Story job: land the joke through normalcy, not a thesis.

Visual: wider table composition. Father is still the conversation anchor. Zuv
continues listening or responding to father, with empty hands. Aachu keeps
eating like this is completely normal.

Hard fails:

- The scene becomes a romantic two-shot.
- Father disappears from scene logic.
- Zuv looks at Aachu.
- The copy becomes a moral, quote, or love thesis.
- Text is missing or changed.

## Global Visual Lock

- Exact native `1080x1080` for every proof/concept screen.
- Never textless.
- Warm ivory/off-white paper, not yellow parchment.
- Handwritten charcoal/ink typography in upper-middle negative space.
- Tiny `@a.storyof.two` brandmark bottom-right.
- No extra words, labels, UI, quote-card treatment, or random micro-text.
- Zuv never looks at Aachu in this version.
- Zuv never holds/offers a bite.
- Father-conversation eye-line must be clear.
- Aachu is eating naturally, not performing or being mocked.

## Internal Agent STOP Checks

Before any proof is shown to the creator, agents must answer:

- Is the file exactly `1080x1080`?
- Is the expected slide text present and unchanged?
- Is there no extra text except `@a.storyof.two`?
- Is Zuv looking at father?
- Are Zuv's hands free of any bite?
- Is Aachu eating the taken bite naturally?
- Is father-conversation legible?
- Does the frame avoid preachy/thesis copy?
- Does the scene work if text is hidden?

Any `no` means STOP, reject internally, and regenerate or revise before creator
handoff.
