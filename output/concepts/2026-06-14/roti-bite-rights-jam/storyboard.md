## Slide 1

Recommended on-image text:

```text
bas papa se baat kar raha tha
```

Scene:

A warm home dining-table moment during a family meal, staged as a wider/distant
shot from across or slightly diagonal to the table. Zuv is seated beside Aachu,
but his face and body are angled toward his father. Father is present as the
active conversation partner through a soft back/side/off-frame table presence.
Aachu has already taken the prepared roti bite and is eating it naturally,
almost innocently.

Action:

The bite has already moved from Zuv's side to Aachu. Zuv is still in the
conversation with father. He is not guarding the food, not looking at Aachu,
and not holding a bite. The humor lives in the viewer noticing what happened
while he stayed in the father conversation.

Eye-line:

Zuv looks at his father, not at Aachu. Aachu can look at her food, plate, or
slightly sideways while eating. Father is visually present enough to explain
Zuv's eye-line, but not detailed enough to become the lead character.

Hands:

Zuv's hands must not hold the bite. His hands can rest near his plate, gesture
mid-conversation, or touch his own roti/plate without a ready bite in hand.
Aachu's hand or mouth carries the taken bite. Hands must look natural.

Body distance:

They sit close enough that the private bite rhythm feels normal inside the
family meal. No exaggerated leaning or staged romance pose.

Object movement:

Roti is the moving object, but the transfer has already happened. The image
should show evidence: Aachu eating, a missing/torn piece on Zuv's plate, and
Zuv's empty hand or conversational gesture. Do not show him offering/holding a
bite.

Silence:

No speech bubbles. The father conversation is implied by Zuv's eye-line,
posture, and father-side blocking, while the couple's private side story stays
silent.

Consequence:

The viewer understands: Zuv was busy talking to father, Aachu quietly took the
prepared bite, and the table continued. This is not food theft, not `I'm not
hungry`, and not an extra-order joke.

Composition:

Square 1080x1080 frame. Use a wider/distant table composition, not a romantic
close two-shot. Keep clean upper-middle negative space for text. Couple and
table occupy the lower/middle-lower frame. Table is simple: roti, plates, one
or two food cues, maybe a glass. No crowded thali spread, restaurant menu,
pizza, or decorative clutter.

Identity and wardrobe:

Zuv should use attached references for face, curly hair, brows, nose, stubble,
skin tone, and soft expression. No sunglasses. Home-appropriate off-white or
cream kurta/shirt is acceptable if derived from the identity references.

Aachu should use attached references for face, expressive eyes, brows, nose,
soft cheeks, long dark hair, skin tone, and playful-natural expression.
Lavender top or dark casual layer is acceptable if derived from the identity
references.
