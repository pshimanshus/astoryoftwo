## Selected Direction

**Two Operating Systems**

He builds the runway. She makes the takeoff happen.

The Capricorn/Aries hook stays, but the carousel should not become a zodiac compatibility poster. The story is that they move at different speeds and somehow create one rhythm: he prepares, she begins; he saves proof, she follows instinct; he worries quietly, she feels loudly; he acts calm, but he chooses the spark.
