# Love Stopped Feeling Like A Race

Status: `REPAIRED_AFTER_CREATOR_FEEDBACK`

Creator feedback: the first recommendation was preachy and weaker than the
reference. Repair: preserve the source cadence and only convert the visual
world into @a.storyof.two.

## Recommended 3-Slide Copy

1. I thought I had to do this alone.
2. But you never made me feel behind.
3. Turns out, the view feels better with you by my side.

## Active 6-Slide Copy

Slides 1-5 keep the softness; slide 6 adds the sarcastic/humor button.

1. I thought I had to do this alone.
2. I kept walking like I was fine.
3. You never made me feel behind.
4. You just stayed beside me.
5. Turns out, the view feels better with you by my side.
6. Next time, car se.

## Why This Version

- It keeps the reference's clean emotional progression: alone -> not behind ->
  beside me.
- It removes the preachy "race/pace" language from the slide copy.
- It keeps Zuv's care quiet: he does not rescue, lecture, or explain.
- It keeps Aachu dignified: she is capable, just no longer alone.
- The joke is small and desi: romance happened, but she is still choosing the
  car next time.

## Visual Direction

Illustrate as Observational Intimacy Premium @a.storyof.two watercolor-and-ink:
neutral warm ivory/off-white paper, visible grain, lower/middle-lower couple
placement, clean upper-middle handwritten text, tiny bottom-right
`@a.storyof.two` brandmark, identity-first Aachu/Zuv faces, and no yellow,
parchment, UI, or external-cartoon style.

The core scene is a real Aachu/Zuv path, stairs, or hill moment: she begins
alone, keeps going like she is fine, he comes beside her without making a
scene, they sit together with the view ahead, and the final frame gives the
moment a practical deadpan button.
