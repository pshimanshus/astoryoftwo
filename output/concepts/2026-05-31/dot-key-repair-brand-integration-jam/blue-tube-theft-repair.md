# Blue Tube Theft Repair

date: 2026-05-31
status: repaired_story_spine_for_creator_review
reason_for_repair: Creator said the story is solid but the slide-copy sequence destroys it.

## Diagnosis

The earlier copy sequence failed because it explained the idea instead of
staging the discovery.

Broken pattern:

```text
product missing -> strawberry face -> suspicious Zuv -> accusation -> drawer -> two tubes -> thesis
```

Why it failed:

- Slide 1 revealed the whole premise too early.
- Slide 2 was a label, not an action.
- The search was too short, so suspicion had no buildup.
- The ending sounded like a caption line instead of a visual payoff.

## Repaired Story Logic

The viewer should discover the theft with Aachu:

```text
normal ritual
-> expected object absent
-> tiny search
-> her face reacts
-> Zuv enters with suspicious calm/freshness
-> caught
-> drawer reveal
-> funny tender restock thesis
```

## Recommended Slide Copy

| Slide | On-Image Text | Story Job | Visual Beat |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | She came for her blue tube. | Start inside the ritual | Aachu at the mirror, reaching for her Dot & Key Barrier Repair tube. The product is expected, not announced like an ad. |
| 2 | Drawer empty. Pouch empty. | Missing-object proof | Her hand checks the drawer, then the skincare pouch. The blue tube is absent. |
| 3 | Her cheeks said, bas. | Aachu strawberry-face proof | Soft rosy/flushed strawberry-face irritation; she is not shamed, just visibly done. |
| 4 | He walked in too fresh. | Suspicion | Zuv enters, avoiding eye contact, looking suspiciously calm/fresh. No skin-result claim; the joke is guilt. |
| 5 | Mera wala kyun? | Caught beat | Aachu turns with the empty pouch; Zuv freezes. |
| 6 | He opened another drawer. | Reveal setup | His hand opens a drawer/table box. Keep suspense for one more swipe. |
| 7 | Ek tera. Ek mera. | Product payoff | Two blue Dot & Key Barrier Repair tubes, labels readable. One is hers, one is his. |
| 8 | Love restocks what it steals. | Save/share thesis | Both at mirror, half-annoyed/half-laughing. Product sits naturally as the receipt. |

## Brand-Safe Alternate Ending

If Dot & Key wants a softer brand-safe line:

```text
Love restocks what it borrows.
```

Use `steals` for creator-native humor. Use `borrows` for stricter brand review.

## Why This Works Better

- The product moves: expected -> missing -> searched for -> stolen -> restocked.
- Aachu has an active emotional role: ritual, search, suspicion, confrontation.
- Zuv has an active comedic role: theft, guilt, reveal, restock.
- Dot & Key is not pasted in; the blue tube causes the plot.
- The final line is earned by the drawer reveal, not pasted as a quote.
