# Direct Visual Story

Treat every frame as a directed scene, not decorated copy. Turn the locked
relationship truth into visible action, reaction, object state, blocking,
camera choice, environment, and change across beats. Preserve free creative
invention; use structure to expose weak ideas, not to generate checklist art.

## Hard creative standard

A frame is a visual sentence when it contains:

1. a specific subject;
2. an observable action;
3. a person, object, or space acted upon;
4. a visible reaction, consequence, or changed state.

“Warm couple in a cozy wedding scene” is atmosphere. “She points toward the
exit barefoot while he holds both juttis, already familiar with this ritual” is
story. Richness means more causal evidence, not more decorative detail.

Keep these tests active:

- What should a stranger know from this view?
- What should they feel?
- What becomes visible here that was not visible in the previous frame?
- Why this camera position for this beat?
- What do hands, gaze, feet, distance, posture, and object ownership say?
- What in the setting proves who lives here or what just happened?
- If the exact copy disappears, what relationship truth remains readable?
- Does the image add evidence or subtext instead of paraphrasing the line?

## Directing workflow

### 1. Confirm the lock and preserve facts

Start only after a route is chosen. Record the approved concept, exact facts,
copy status, references, current creator corrections, and what must not change.
Resolve the canvas from the current creator instruction and corrections through
`carousel_format_contract`: `instagram_post` is the default only when no canvas
was specified; `reels_stories` and `square` are explicit-only. If copy or canvas
is not locked, direct a provisional visual spine and let copy respond to it, but
treat that board as advisory: it cannot pass Event A or unlock generation.
Rerun the blind read only after exact copy (or a documented text exception) and
the request-derived format set are both locked. Never rewrite locked copy to
excuse a failed image.

Any creator correction invalidates affected downstream prompts, proofs, and
reviews. Rebuild them and refresh fingerprints before proceeding.

### 2. Write one director card

State, in plain language:

- what physically happens;
- whose emotional point of view owns the beat;
- what each person wants;
- what changes from before to after;
- the spoken idea versus subtext;
- what a stranger should infer without reading the copy.

Do not begin with style adjectives or an image-generation prompt.

### 3. Choose the visual grammar

Decide whether the route is a single decisive image, a causal sequence, a
montage with an arc, or a Reel sequence. For sequences, assign useful narrative
jobs such as establisher, initial pressure, prolongation, peak, and release.
Slides may combine jobs. Adjacent frames must not repeat the same job with only
a pose or outfit change.

Pick one or two visual variables to progress—such as body distance, object
ownership, space, line, tone, movement, or rhythm. Do not escalate everything.

### 4. Build the evidence ledger

For every claim, choose at least one observable carrier:

- action or interrupted action;
- hand gesture, gaze, posture, feet, touch, or body distance;
- object ownership, position, residue, damage, absence, or transfer;
- wardrobe or environmental trace of an earlier event;
- reaction or consequence;
- a deliberate change between frames.

Objects are verbs, not motifs pasted on top. Track their state. A prop that does
not locate the moment, change the action, reveal character, create consequence,
or pay off later is decoration and may be removed.

Do not force an object into every frame. When blocking, spatial change, or a
human gesture carries the beat better, record why no object motif is needed.

### 5. Stage before styling

For each frame, specify:

- subject, action, target/object, and reaction/consequence;
- hands, gaze, feet/posture, body distance, and height relationship;
- sub-location, time, motivated light, material texture, and lived story trace;
- shot size, angle, camera position, focal subject, and the story reason;
- incoming and outgoing continuity state;
- expected people, background people, reflections, and forbidden entities;
- native-format text zone and negative space.

Block the relationship before adding watercolor language. Use wide shots for
geography or isolation, closer views for evidence or intimacy, and reactions
to tell the audience how an event lands—but motivate every choice.

### 6. Author the swipe or cut

Treat a carousel swipe as an edit. Select moments that make the viewer complete
meaning between frames. Omit intermediate action only when the gap creates
clear participation rather than confusion. Build at least one setup/payoff or
changed-meaning link in a sequence.

Use production design as biography: ask who has lived here, what just happened,
what remains unfinished, and what only this couple would leave behind. Sparse,
selected evidence usually beats a crowded frame.

### 7. Run Checker Event A — copy-hidden storyboard read

Use a fresh orchestrated critic run that did not author the route. Persist
auditable `review_provenance`: author and critic task/run IDs, the exact blind
input hash, raw pre-reveal response hash, and structured output hash. Preserve
exactly one verifiable response source: inline `raw_response` or a safe
package-relative `raw_response_artifact` whose current UTF-8 contents
hash-match it.
Free-form reviewer labels are metadata; they do not prove independence or
identity. Give the critic only observable staged cards in order: visible
people, setting, action, hands/contact, gaze, blocking, object state, camera
view, and visible continuity. Hide slide copy, caption, theme, scores,
narrative-job labels, POV labels, story reasons, intended interpretations, and
this skill’s calibration examples.

Ask the critic to report per frame:

- who, where, and visible action;
- relationship state and emotional point of view;
- what probably happened before and what may happen next;
- concrete visual evidence for each inference;
- uncertainty or competing readings;
- the sequence in one sentence.

Compare its inference with the director card. Repair the plan until the core
action and relationship turn are inferred from cited evidence. A pretty but
generic couple pose fails.

After the blind inference, run a copy-visual action reconciliation before PASS.
For every slide, compare the inferred scene against the exact copy's subject,
verb, object, temporal cue, movement path, shared-versus-solo action, and
spatial side. This is not shown to the blind critic; it is the post-reveal logic
check. A scene may be visually coherent and still fail the copy.

For door, lock, threshold, arrival, departure, or "went back" beats, explicitly
lock:

- whether the moment is before departure, during return, or after arrival;
- whether the camera and both people are inside, outside, or crossing;
- whether the door is open, closing, fully closed, or already locked;
- where each person was, what direction they moved, and what "went back" means;
- whether the final action is shared or solo.

If copy says "checked it with him," a frame where she checks alone while he
watches is a contradiction. If the creator says the couple closed the door and
is outside, an interior entryway image is a hard fail even when the anatomy and
mood are attractive.

Store the exact observable-only payload as `blind_cards`, fingerprint it, and
record the raw pre-reveal result under `director_storyboard` inside the existing
`visual-plan-quality.json`; do not create a competing gate artifact. Use the
schema in `references/checker-contract.md`. All author and Event A task/run IDs
must be distinct. This is auditable execution provenance, not cryptographic
proof of human identity. Before the run, the generation-facing
`prompt-pack.json` must already exist as a locked draft, but it is never shown
to the blind critic. Any later prompt-pack edit invalidates Event A and requires
a fresh run. After the record is complete, bind status, locks,
source and blind hashes, current creator-correction state, the complete
generation-facing `prompt-pack.json`, cards, slides, staging, ledgers, critic
output, provenance, and issues into `director_event_fingerprint` using
`director-event/v2`. Record competing readings during review; after repair and
recheck, move resolved ones to `resolved_ambiguities` and leave
`unresolved_ambiguities` empty for PASS.

Run:

```bash
venv/bin/python .agents/skills/a-story-direct-visual-story/scripts/check_visual_story.py \
  --carousel-dir output/carousels/YYYY-MM-DD/slug --phase pre
```

A nonzero exit blocks prompt handoff and image generation.

Compile prompt handoffs as one atomic set. `HANDOFF_READY` must carry a
`compiled-prompt-handoff/v1` record that binds the current format contract,
`prompt-pack.json`, `slides.json`, every package-relative `.prompt.txt` and
handoff `.md` file, their current hashes, and the complete set fingerprint.
Expose `codex-image-prompts/` only after every compiled file passes; a blocked
or partial compile leaves no active set. Before first quarantine, packaging must
reject and remove a missing, changed, extra, external, traversing, or symlinked
handoff. Already-quarantined promotion is governed by exact pixel bindings and
does not depend on keeping old prompt files active.

Immediately before the actual image-generation tool call, rerun this precheck
and the workflow doctor. Use only the active compiled `.prompt.txt` whose hash
is recorded in the handoff. A creator correction invalidates the prior
director event and prompt set even when the requested change appears small.

### 8. Prove the riskiest frame first

Choose the hero receipt or highest-risk frame: the one most likely to fail in
identity, object state, hands, gaze, copy logic, composition, or emotion. Attach
the selected Aachu/Zuv identity and style references. Generate the exact
request-locked canvas: 3:4 for `instagram_post`, 9:16 for explicit
`reels_stories`, and 1:1 for explicit `square`. When several formats are
requested, generate separate native proofs rather than deriving one from
another. Preserve exact integrated text and tiny top-right `@a.storyof.two`.

Inspect the actual pixels. Do not award success from a prompt, filename, or
generator report. If the idea itself does not read, repair the director card,
evidence, staging, or sequence before changing prompt adjectives.

Keep the returned proof internal until it is package-quarantined and has passed
native dimensions, exact text, anatomy/entity/spatial integrity, identity, and
provisional image-first story review. Do not render an unchecked candidate
inline as though it were an approved slide.

### 9. Run Checker Event B — image-first rendered-frame audit

Use another fresh orchestrated critic run. Its task ID and run ID must each be
pairwise distinct from the route author and Event A critic provenance. Persist
exactly one verifiable raw-response source (inline or a safe package-relative
artifact), the exact image-first input manifest, and structured output under
`review_provenance`.
This provenance makes the execution auditable; reviewer names alone do not
prove independence. Ask it to inspect each native image image-first, recording
what the pixels visibly communicate before comparing against the director card
and exact copy.

For each slide in every currently locked/requested native format, require:

- observed action and relationship turn;
- focal hierarchy and readable hands/gaze/object state;
- expected versus observed story;
- copy-visual contradictions;
- unintended second stories, people, reflections, or symbols;
- storyboard match and native-format readability;
- specific pixel evidence and the current image fingerprint.

Record the result as `checks.visual_story_readability` inside the existing
`visual-qa.json`. Bind it to the complete approved Event A through
`source_director_event_fingerprint`. Resolve expected files only through
`expected_frame_bindings`: each frame's package-relative path, slide, format,
dimensions, and current bytes must match the canonical request-derived asset.
Do not infer intent from folders or accept an external, hardlinked,
byte-duplicate, wrong-size, or merely same-named file. The reviewed format set
must exactly match the lock,
including explicit `square`, and must not invent a 9:16 derivative because a
workflow mentions it. Keep face likeness, exact text, anatomy, dimensions,
brandmark, and scene-entity integrity as separate checks.

Run the same audit provisionally after the proof. Claim full PASS only after
every slide and every locked native format exists, then run:

```bash
venv/bin/python .agents/skills/a-story-direct-visual-story/scripts/check_visual_story.py \
  --carousel-dir output/carousels/YYYY-MM-DD/slug --phase post
```

### 10. Repair at the right layer

Classify failure before regenerating:

1. unclear intent → repair the director card;
2. weak evidence → replace/add an action, reaction, object state, gesture, or trace;
3. weak staging → change blocking, eye-line, camera, scale, or focal hierarchy;
4. weak sequence → change beat selection or order;
5. generation drift → repair prompt/reference attachment and regenerate.

Never regenerate an unchanged visual idea. After two semantic failures of the
same route, return to the visual premise and replace it. Do not polish a route
that a blind reader cannot understand.

## Stop conditions

Stop or mark REPAIR when:

- the copy is the only carrier of the story;
- a frame communicates atmosphere but no event;
- adjacent frames repeat camera, action, and narrative job;
- an object is decorative or privately symbolic rather than legible evidence;
- hands, gaze, clothing, object ownership, or chronology contradict the copy;
- relationship motion defaults repeatedly to Zuv managing Aachu;
- an unintended duplicate/reflection/background figure creates another story;
- either checker is missing, stale, self-certified as independent, or lacks
  concrete evidence;
- a legacy package lacks current orchestration provenance, complete director
  fingerprinting, or exact expected-asset binding;
- any locked/requested native format was omitted or an unrequested format was added.

Do not call the package final until this skill’s two events and all existing
production gates pass.

## Research and learning discipline

Use the sources in `references/source-register.md` to understand craft, not to
copy protected frames, pages, transcripts, or another creator’s visual style.
Store short original paraphrases and direct source links. Treat current social
platform guidance as refreshable; do not turn it into timeless story law.

When the creator teaches a durable visual lesson, propose the smallest update
to this skill, its calibration reference, the matching canonical rule, and a
regression test. `memory/working.md` remains pointer-only.
