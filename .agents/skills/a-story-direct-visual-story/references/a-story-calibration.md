## Gold: “He didn’t marry peace” and the juttis

Local sources:

- `corpus/media/DYJpjt9CQYY/contact-sheet.jpg`
- `corpus/media/DYJpjt9CQYY/slide-03.jpg`
- `output/carousels/2026-05-09/he-didnt-marry-peace/slides.json`
- `output/carousels/2026-05-09/he-didnt-marry-peace/storyboard.md`
- `docs/audits/2026-05-16-gold-carousel-package-anatomy.md`
- `output/reports/2026-05-17-he-didnt-marry-peace-viral-theme-analysis.md`

Locked line:

> He married “I’m leaving” with no shoes on.

The visual shows Aachu barefoot and pointing toward an imaginary exit, her
dupatta and body moving away, while Zuv holds both shoes with calm familiarity.
The frame communicates more than footwear:

- the wedding/context is located;
- her declaration and her physical preparedness contradict each other;
- his object ownership proves he anticipated the ritual;
- opposite movement and calm reaction distinguish their energies;
- one object compresses comedy, history, care, and emotional safety;
- the core beat survives with the words hidden.

Transfer the mechanism, not the shoes: find one behavior, gesture, object state,
or spatial relation that compresses character, conflict, history, and love.

## Repeated failures this skill must stop

### Beautiful mood instead of event

Warm, soft, polished relationship posters can be emotionally pleasant yet
unsendable. A frame needs physically readable proof, not “nice art” standing in
for “this is us.” See
`output/reports/2026-05-24-carousel-performance-autopsy.md`.

### Copying the chassis, losing the engine

Old derivatives borrowed the wording/shape of a winner while losing behavioral
receipts, surprise, and consequence. See
`docs/audits/2026-05-16-gold-carousel-package-comparison.md`.

### Prop-first premise

`output/concepts/2026-05-18/ayatana-shoes-golden-gate.md` establishes that
“shoes are romantic” is not a story. Shoes work only when their state proves a
specific act such as noticing discomfort, accepting embarrassment, preparing,
or caring.

### Repeated medium couple shots

Background or outfit changes do not create progression. Camera distance,
blocking, action, hands, object state, perspective, emotional beat, and who is
visible must materially change. Follow `config/rules/visual-variety.md`.

### Decorative home shorthand

“Cozy home, plants, books, chai” is not lived production design. Require
sub-location, time, motivated light, camera, blocking, active movement,
materials, object causality, negative space, and continuity callbacks. See
`evals/tasks/ASTO-016-home-cinematic-visual-evidence/deep-spec.md`.

### Copy-visual contradiction

A beautiful image can disprove its line:

- The 2026-05-30 phone-prank slide showed Zuv already wearing pants while the
  line required socks before pants. See `config/rules/identity.md`.
- The roti-bite storyboard requires Zuv to look at his father while Aachu is
  already eating; changing gaze or object state changes the story. See
  `output/concepts/2026-06-14/roti-bite-rights-jam/scratch-storyboard.md`.

### Accidental secondary story

Extra figures, reflections, silhouettes, or duplicated couples can imply a
second timeline or relationship. Use `config/rules/scene-entity-integrity.md`
and the per-frame entity contract.

### Caretaker monotony

Love must not always mean Zuv managing Aachu. Motion can begin with either
person, both, the environment, a shared ritual, or an object changing hands.
Use `config/rules/relationship-motion.md`.

### Mechanical PASS theater

Scores and booleans have approved creator-rejected or stale work before. The
deterministic checker proves that evidence exists and is current; only a fresh
critic can judge whether the story reads. Never collapse those into one score.

## Calibration comparison

Weak:

> Couple stands in a warm wedding setting near some shoes.

Strong:

> She crosses toward the exit barefoot, one hand declaring she is done. He
> remains grounded and holds both juttis, unsurprised; her feet, his hands, and
> their opposing movement make the familiar ritual legible.

The strong version specifies action, object state, ownership, blocking,
reaction, contrast, and inference. It does not need more décor.
