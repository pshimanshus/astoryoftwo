# A Story Caption CTA Engine

## Overview

Treat the caption is the second post. It should reopen the relationship truth, add receipts the slides did not have room for, and create a natural send/save/comment reason without hard CTA pressure.

## Load

- `config/rules/voice.md`
- `memory/semantic/a-story-audience-profile.md`
- `config/skills/winning-carousel-research-runtime-context.md`

## Caption Shape

```text
Line 1: reopen the universal relationship truth
Middle: add 1-2 specific receipts
Optional: one natural spoken/Hinglish line
Ending: earned send/save/comment reason
CTA: soft, only if the post already deserves it
```

## Output

```text
Caption option A:
Caption option B:
Best caption:
CTA/comment prompt:
Why it is earned:
Risk notes:
```

## CTA Rules

- Prefer native asks: "which one of you is this?", "send this to your calm one", or a comment trigger only when it fits the line.
- Avoid "follow for more" and funnel-coach phrasing.
- Do not use a CTA to manufacture shareability from weak copy.

## Hard Fails

- Caption repeats the slides without adding value.
- Caption explains the art instead of the relationship.
- CTA sounds transactional or creator-growth-coded.
