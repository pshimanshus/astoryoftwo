# A Story Hook Generator

## Overview

Generate hooks that open a public relationship doorway before the carousel asks for private context. A good ASOT hook names a recognizable couple pattern and leaves space for Aachu/Zuv proof.

## Load

- `memory/semantic/a-story-audience-profile.md`
- `config/skills/winning-carousel-research-runtime-context.md`
- `config/rules/voice.md`
- `config/rules/golden-theme.md`

## Hook Lanes

- public relationship contradiction.
- Pain mirror in audience words.
- Desire unlock without self-help tone.
- Relatable negative state plus open loop.
- Unspoken emotion stated as fact.
- Minimal identity statement.
- Hinglish/domestic receipt when it sharpens recognition.

## Process

1. State the viewer mirror and the exact send/save reason.
2. Generate 12 hooks across at least four lanes.
3. Reject hooks that sound like generic viral, advice, creator-growth, or couple-coach copy.
4. Pick 3 finalists and explain the swipe reason for each.
5. Select one winner only if a cold viewer understands it in under two seconds.

## Output

```text
Audience mirror:
Concept proof:
Hook options:
Rejected hooks:
Selected hook:
Why it wins:
Slide 2 promise:
```

## Hard Fails

- "How to", "5 things", "here is why", and generic viral prompt energy.
- Object/place/outfit-first hooks unless they name a relationship behavior.
- Hooks that require the caption or private backstory to understand.
