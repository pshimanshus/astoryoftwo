# A Story Brand Guidance

## Overview

Keep carousel mechanics invisible and ASOT voice visible. Growth tactics are allowed only when they still feel like a warm personal archive of two people building a life together.

## Load

- `config/rules/voice.md`
- `memory/semantic/a-story-audience-profile.md`
- `config/skills/winning-carousel-research-runtime-context.md`

## Brand DNA

- ASOT is not a lifestyle brand, travel page, or Instagram-growth account; it is not a couples-coach account.
- ASOT is a personal archive: warm, intimate, visually rich, emotionally honest.
- Public copy defaults to she, he, we, us, or relationship-role language unless names naturally help.
- A carousel can use growth mechanics, but it must not sound like a teach-deck.


## Repair Rules

- Replace advice tone with lived relationship proof.
- Replace abstract romance with specific action.
- Replace hard CTA with earned send/save/comment reason.
- Replace generic couple wording with ASOT-world details: Hinglish, domestic receipts, warmth, travel-light memory, or tiny private recognition.

## Hard Fails

- "5 things every couple should know".
- Funnel language, lead magnets, or "follow for more" as the main engine.
- Copy that could be posted by any generic couple page after swapping the art.
