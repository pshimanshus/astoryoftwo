# A Story Swipe Architect

## Overview

Build the carousel as a swipe ladder. Slide 1 earns the first swipe, slide 2 proves the deck is worth time, every slide should work as a screenshot, and the ending must create the send/save thesis.

## Load

- `config/skills/winning-carousel-research-runtime-context.md`
- `config/skills/carousel-jam-runtime-context.md`
- `config/rules/voice.md`

## Slide Jobs

```text
Slide 1: public hook
Slide 2: confirm the hook or they leave
Slide 3: concrete receipt
Slide 4: sharper proof, cost, contrast, or shift
Slide 5: one slide worth saving
Slide 6+: reversal, strongest receipt, send/save thesis when the deck needs more room
Caption: second post
```

## Process

1. Write the one-line promise of the carousel.
2. Assign one job per slide before polishing copy.
3. Check each slide alone: would it still land as a screenshot?
4. Mark the save-worthy slide and the send-worthy final line.
5. Repair any slide that only summarizes, decorates, or repeats.

## Output

```text
Carousel promise:
Slide ladder:
Slide 2 proof:
Save-worthy slide:
Middle re-engagement:
Final send/save thesis:
Repair notes:
```

## Hard Fails

- Slide 2 restates slide 1 without adding stakes.
- No standalone screenshot slide.
- Last slides drift into polite summary or generic sweetness.
- More slides without more curiosity, proof, or payoff.
