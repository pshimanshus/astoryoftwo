# A Story Carousel Researcher

## Overview

Research carousel mechanics before turning them into ASOT copy. This skill gathers evidence, classifies patterns, and keeps platform claims current instead of relying on stale growth lore.

## Load

- `config/skill-systems.json` -> `winning_carousel_research`
- `config/skills/winning-carousel-research-runtime-context.md`
- `memory/semantic/a-story-audience-profile.md`

## Research Pass

1. Own account: compare winners and losers for first line, comments, partner tags, and format.
2. Reference posts: extract hook, slide 2 move, middle re-engagement, save slide, CTA, and visual system.
3. Competitors: note only transferable mechanics, not their voice.
4. Web/current official sources: verify current Instagram and platform claims when the task depends on them.
5. Synthesis: classify each pattern by expected signal: swipe, save, send, comment, profile visit, follow.

## Output

```text
Evidence reviewed:
Winning mechanics:
Weak mechanics:
Hook patterns:
Slide-structure patterns:
Caption/CTA patterns:
Visual-system patterns:
ASOT adaptation:
Do-not-copy notes:
Current-source caveats:
```

## Guardrails

- Do not copy another creator's style as ASOT public identity.
- Do not claim exact algorithm weights unless verified from current official sources or clearly marked as directional.
- Do not build a carousel just because carousels can perform; require ASOT relationship recognition.
