# Negative Regression Examples

These are not banned ideas. They are **banned execution patterns.** Compare every
new concept against this list before approving.

## Regression 001 — Food stealing became a cringe carousel

Rejected copy:
- nahi yaar, mujhe kuch nahi chahiye
- phir mere plate pe ye haath kiska hai
- naraaz hone ka natak karta hoon roz
- isiliye ek extra pehle hi mangwa leta hoon

Why rejected: forced Hinglish · staged dialogue · meme-admin energy · explains the
joke · weak emotional aftertaste · carousel used to over-explain a single visual
truth · cleverness without intimacy.

Future rule: any food-stealing concept must focus on **anticipation, not
stealing.** The emotional proof is: *He already planned for what she would deny.*
Source: `runs/2026-06-30_food-stealing-love/evals/creative_failure_postmortem.md`.

## Regression 002 — Clever title replaces feeling

Bad: "The Pre-Order"

Why rejected: sounds like a campaign idea, not a relationship moment.

Future rule: if the title is the best part, the concept is not ready.

## Regression 003 — NEEDS_HUMAN hides weak work

Bad: work gets parked at NEEDS_HUMAN even though copy/concept is weak.

Future rule: **NEEDS_HUMAN means strong** but awaiting final human judgment. Weak
work must be `REPAIR_REQUIRED` or `FAILED_CREATIVE_QUALITY`.

## Regression 004 — Completeness mistaken for quality

Bad: run has all files, all scores, state update, and still feels off.

Future rule: artifact completeness is never enough. **Taste outranks structure.**
