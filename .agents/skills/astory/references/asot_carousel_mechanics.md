# ASOT Carousel Mechanics (source-backed)

Carousel craft mechanics extracted **only** from real repo evidence (see
`carousel_research_inventory.md`). Every canonical card cites a path. Metrics for
ASOT's own posts are real (shares/1k + saves from `data/instagram/` +
`references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md`); external winner metrics are
likes/comments/format-tag proxies.

> These are calibration mechanics, **not** a mandate to make carousels. The first
> mechanic is the format gate: most ASOT winners are single images. A carousel is
> earned, never defaulted (`creative_taste_bible.md`, `story-craft.md`).

## Mechanic 1 — Format is a decision: single hero is the default; a carousel is earned

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/craft/story-craft.md` ("Two shapes"); `references/text-style/instagram-winner-mechanics-2026-06-14.md` (Static/Reel Wrapper Formula); `.agents/skills/astory/references/creative_taste_bible.md`; `references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md`.
- source type: derived craft (ASOT voice) + distilled winner-bank + own metrics.
- metric/evidence: winner-bank format mix = 115 carousels / 40 static / 15 reels (there are strong statics); ASOT's own strongest are single illustrations (`story-craft.md`, "most of your strongest").
- confidence: high.
- quote: "Static/reel text should be short enough to understand in under two seconds. **If it needs an explanation, it wants to be a carousel.**" (`instagram-winner-mechanics`); "Single illustration (most of your strongest): one frame holds the whole arc." (`story-craft.md`).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: choose format by *how many true receipts the idea has* — one instantly recognizable behavior → single hero; multiple escalating receipts → carousel.
- why it matters on Instagram: a carousel spends the viewer's swipe-budget; if there is only one beat, the extra slides dilute and the post reads padded.
- what failure it prevents: `CAROUSEL_UNEARNED` — making a carousel because "carousels perform."

### ASOT translation
- how it applies to Aachu/Zuv: r3 food-stealing is one behavior → single hero (correct). A "he stopped believing her no across a week" escalation could be a carousel.
- what post uses it: any run — this is the first decision.
- what post avoids it: a one-image behavioral truth padded into slides.

### Required artifact
- `planning/format_decision.md` (always).

### Gate question
- "Does this idea have ≥2 fresh escalating receipts (each a new emotional beat), or does it land in one frame? If one frame, why is a carousel rejected?"

## Mechanic 2 — Slide 1 is a first-frame stop (curiosity gap / contradiction / confession)

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/craft/story-craft.md` (Hook craft); `references/text-style/instagram-winner-mechanics-2026-06-14.md` (#16 First-Slide Contradiction); `references/text-style/illusion-of-novelty-storytelling-2026-06-18.md`; `.agents/skills/astory/references/taste_benchmark.md` (first-frame stop); `data/instagram/carousel_posts/DYJpjt9CQYY/carousel_export.json`.
- source type: own carousel + distilled winners.
- metric/evidence: DYJpjt9CQYY "He didn't marry peace" = **40.15 shares/1k, reach 531,805** (#1 own send-winner).
- confidence: high.
- quote: "Slide 1 = hook: a curiosity gap or a claim that demands the next slide." (`story-craft.md`); "strong hooks often create a small contradiction: mad but caring…" (`instagram-winner-mechanics` #16).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: slide 1 must earn the second by opening a gap (contradiction/curiosity/confession), not by stating the whole idea.
- why it matters: no stop → no swipe → the carousel is dead on arrival.
- what failure it prevents: hype hooks, fake secrets, "nobody talks about this," or explaining both sides in text (all banned in `story-craft.md` / winner-mechanics).

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: a familiar couple tension stated as an affectionate contradiction ("she says she's not hungry").
- what post uses it: every carousel; also the single-hero's on-image line.
- what post avoids it: a hook that resolves itself (nothing left to swipe for).

### Required artifact
- `planning/hook_options.md` (carousel) and slide 1 of `planning/carousel_architecture.md`.

### Gate question
- "Does slide 1 open a gap the next slide must close, without fake hype or self-resolving?"

## Mechanic 3 — Slide 2 confirms the hook (the first receipt; the critical slide)

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/text-style/instagram-winner-mechanics-2026-06-14.md` (Carousel Wrapper Formula, slide 2 = "first receipt through an object or habit"); `references/craft/story-craft.md` (middle = escalating specific receipts); `.agents/skills/astory/personas/story-room/swipe-retention-critic.md`; `.agents/skills/astory/templates/agents/story_room_agent_prompt.md` (Retention Pass).
- source type: distilled winner formula + own craft + Story Room retention protocol.
- metric/evidence: strong own carousels run 5–8 slides on escalating receipts (`instagram-winner-mechanics`, `outcome-attribution.md`).
- confidence: high (the "slide 2 = first receipt / reason to continue" role is explicit in the wrapper formula; the sharper "confirms the hook or the viewer leaves" is an operational tightening of that role — see "Needs source confirmation").
- quote: "Slide 2: first receipt through an object or habit." (`instagram-winner-mechanics`, Carousel Wrapper Formula).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: slide 2 pays off slide 1's promise with the first concrete receipt and a "this is about me" jolt; it must NOT restate slide 1 in new words.
- why it matters: slide 2 is where drop-off happens; a filler slide 2 loses the swipe the hook earned.
- what failure it prevents: `SLIDE2_PROOF_MISSING` — a second slide that repeats the hook or stalls.

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: slide 2 shows the first behavioral receipt (an object/habit) that proves the hook is true for *this* couple.
- what post uses it: any carousel.
- what post avoids it: a carousel whose slide 2 is a rephrase of slide 1.

### Required artifact
- `planning/slide2_proof.md` (carousel only).

### Gate question
- "Does slide 2 deliver the first concrete receipt and a 'this is me' turn — or does it restate slide 1? (If restate → fail.)"

## Mechanic 4 — Escalating fresh receipts, never a restated point

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/craft/story-craft.md` ("Save the Cat: every beat raises stakes; no repeats"); `.agents/skills/astory/references/source_backed_winner_patterns.md` (Pattern 1 escalation, DYJpjt9CQYY); `references/text-style/instagram-winner-mechanics-2026-06-14.md` (#15 Repetition Chant = a physical action moves each slide).
- source type: own craft + extracted winner pattern.
- metric/evidence: DYJpjt9CQYY escalation → 40.15 shares/1k; "Three real receipts beat six padded ones" (`story-craft.md`).
- confidence: high.
- quote: "Middle = escalating specific receipts, each a fresh scene/turn, never a restated point." (`story-craft.md`).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: each middle slide adds a *new* receipt/turn (fresh prop/scene), raising the emotional stakes; length is set by how many true receipts exist.
- why it matters: repeated staging goes decorative (run-lessons) and kills swipe.
- what failure it prevents: `CAROUSEL_OVEREXPLAINS_HERO_IDEA` (many slides explaining one beat) and monotony.

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: fresh receipts of the same truth, each a different object/scene in Aachu/Zuv's world.
- what post uses it: receipt-deck / loving-complaint carousels.
- what post avoids it: a single beat stretched across slides.

### Required artifact
- `planning/carousel_architecture.md` (per-slide beat + why-it-escalates).

### Gate question
- "Does every middle slide add a NEW receipt/turn, or is any slide a restated point?"

## Mechanic 5 — Every slide must survive as a screenshot (standalone legibility)

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/text-style/instagram-winner-mechanics-2026-06-14.md` (#7 Book-Page Intimacy "feels authored, intimate, and saved-worthy"; #14 Text Screenshot Energy "look like private evidence"); `references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md` (saves engine); `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (save `send_job`).
- source type: distilled winners + own save metrics.
- metric/evidence: saves-driven posts (DY_t4Dek0pq 16.99 saves/1k) survive as kept/screenshotted objects.
- confidence: **medium** — the repo backs the *save/screenshot/message-native* behavior; the strict "each slide stands alone with no dependency on the previous slide" is an operational craft extension (also logged in "Needs source confirmation").
- quote: "photographed text on pages feels authored, intimate, and saved-worthy" (#7); "message screenshots… look like private evidence" (#14).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: any single slide, screenshotted alone, must carry one clear idea with readable text and enough context to make sense — because people screenshot/send individual slides.
- why it matters: a slide that only makes sense after the previous one cannot be sent or saved on its own, losing the biggest share surface.
- what failure it prevents: `SCREENSHOT_SURVIVAL_FAIL` — clutter, vague pretty filler, or a slide that means nothing alone.

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: each Aachu/Zuv slide = one clear behavior + one readable line.
- what post uses it: all carousels; especially receipt decks and micro-essays.
- what post avoids it: a slide whose meaning is entirely borrowed from its neighbour.

### Required artifact
- `planning/slide_screenshot_survival.md` (carousel only).

### Gate question
- "Screenshot each slide alone: does it carry one clear, readable idea without its neighbour? Any slide that fails → fail."

## Mechanic 6 — One save-worthy slide per carousel

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md` (DY_t4Dek0pq 16.99 saves/1k, best follow-conv 2.92/1k); `.agents/skills/astory/references/source_backed_winner_patterns.md` (Pattern 3 observational-intimacy = highest-save; Pattern 4 mundane-future); `references/text-style/instagram-winner-mechanics-2026-06-14.md` (#7 book-page, #10 nostalgia, #13 "I Notice" — all save-drivers).
- source type: own metrics + extracted patterns.
- metric/evidence: highest-save engine is witnessed specificity (16.99 saves/1k).
- confidence: high.
- quote: "people save posts that articulate a detail they secretly want recognized" (#13 "I Notice").

### What it means
- the carousel principle: at least one slide is engineered to be saved/screenshotted/sent — a relationship receipt, a Hinglish contradiction, a line you send your partner, a tiny love proof, a soft truth.
- why it matters: saves + shares (not likes) are the ranking truth; a carousel with no keepable slide underperforms.
- what failure it prevents: `SAVE_WORTHY_SLIDE_MISSING`.

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: designate one slide (often the payoff) as the sendable/save line.
- what post uses it: every carousel; the single hero's line must itself be save/send-worthy.
- what post avoids it: a carousel that is all setup, no keepable line.

### Required artifact
- `planning/save_worthy_slide.md` (carousel only).

### Gate question
- "Which exact slide is the save/send line, and why would someone keep or forward it (not just like it)?"

## Mechanic 7 — Caption is the second post (caption-as-second-post)

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/text-style/instagram-winner-mechanics-2026-06-14.md` (caption median 39 words; #8 Prompt That Opens A Confession; #20 Public Post As Private Delivery); `data/instagram/carousel_posts/DZNl2zxiRD4/carousel_export.json` (caption + comment behavior); `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (`send_job`).
- source type: distilled winners + own carousel export.
- metric/evidence: winner caption median 39 words (12–62 range); comment-prompt captions drive comments/replies.
- confidence: med-high (the exact "second post" framing is a synthesis of caption-structure + comment-prompt + "who sends this" — see "Needs source confirmation").
- quote: "every concept should answer: 'who sends this, and what are they too shy, angry, proud, or tired to say?'" (#20).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: the caption is not a restatement of the slides — it adds intimacy, a receipt not shown, and a natural comment/send/save prompt.
- why it matters: the caption is a second surface for sends and comments; a repeat-caption wastes it.
- what failure it prevents: `CAPTION_SECOND_POST_MISSING` — afterthought captions and funnel-coach CTAs ("tag your partner if…" used lazily).

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: caption restates the feeling more privately, adds one unseen receipt, and opens a narrow human prompt.
- what post uses it: every post (carousel and hero).
- what post avoids it: caption = the on-image line copy-pasted.

### Required artifact
- `planning/caption_second_post.md` (carousel; recommended for hero too).

### Gate question
- "Does the caption add a new receipt + a genuine send/comment reason, or just repeat the slides? Is any CTA genuinely ASOT, not funnel-coach?"

## Mechanic 8 — A carousel is a brand asset (it strengthens the Aachu/Zuv world)

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/text-style/instagram-winner-mechanics-2026-06-14.md` ("The page identity must stay A Story of Two"); `.agents/skills/astory/references/creative_taste_bible.md` (core principle "same people, same world, new moment"); `.agents/skills/astory/references/face_identity_visual_policy.md` (recurring illustrated characters, continuity anchors).
- source type: distilled winners + own bibles.
- metric/evidence: recurring-world continuity is the brand's moat; source-preserving remix keeps the ASOT identity visible.
- confidence: med-high (grounded in the repo's "same people, same world" core; the exact "brand asset not just a post" phrasing is a synthesis).
- quote: "The page identity must stay A Story of Two. The emotional engine may come directly from a proven source post." (`instagram-winner-mechanics`).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: a carousel must strengthen the Aachu/Zuv world, emotional memory, grid identity, and future repost/clip potential — not just chase one post's reach.
- why it matters: consistency compounds into a recognizable brand; a generic-couple carousel adds reach but erodes identity.
- what failure it prevents: `BRAND_ASSET_WEAK` — a post that could sit on any couple page.

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: preserve faces, continuity anchors (evil-eye pendant/bracelet), voice, and world across slides.
- what post uses it: all.
- what post avoids it: a carousel that weakens identity continuity or reads generic.

### Required artifact
- `planning/format_decision.md` (brand-asset rationale) + `planning/carousel_architecture.md`.

### Gate question
- "Does this carousel strengthen the Aachu/Zuv world and grid identity, or could it belong to any couple page?"

## Mechanic 9 — Name the engine: send / save / comment trigger (rank by shares/1k, not likes)

### Source evidence
- source path: `.agents/skills/astory/references/source_backed_winner_patterns.md` (Pattern 9); `references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md`; `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (`send_job`).
- source type: extracted pattern + own metrics.
- metric/evidence: own winners 25–40 shares/1k; likes diverge from sends (DZNl2zxiRD4 leads likes, not sends).
- confidence: high.
- quote: "rank ideas by shares per 1k reach and follow conversion, not likes" (`outcome-attribution.md`).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: the whole carousel (and its save-worthy slide + caption) must name its primary engine — SHARES (send-to-partner), SAVES (kept), or COMMENTS (narrow prompt) — and be judged on it.
- why it matters: optimizing for likes produces high-like, low-share flops.
- what failure it prevents: like-bait; unclear send/save job.

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: declare the engine in the format decision; every slide serves it.
- what post uses it: all.
- what post avoids it: a "pretty" carousel with no send/save job.

### Required artifact
- `planning/format_decision.md` (declared engine) + `evals/carousel_craft_check.md`.

### Gate question
- "What is the primary engine (share/save/comment), and does the save-worthy slide + caption actually trigger it?"

## Mechanic 10 — Brand DNA / novelty model before generation

### Source evidence
- source path: `references/text-style/illusion-of-novelty-storytelling-2026-06-18.md`; `.agents/skills/astory/SKILL.md` (Brain Recall Gate, source_winner_novelty_model); `.agents/skills/astory/references/creative_taste_bible.md`.
- source type: compiled novelty framework + skill gate.
- confidence: med-high.
- quote: "model the winner: old/familiar topic, new reveal, viewer outcome, contrast frame, bullseye proof, what not to explain." (`illusion-of-novelty…`).

### What it means
- the carousel principle: feed the brand DNA (taste bibles, character world, the winning mechanism/novelty model) *before* asking for a viral carousel — never generate slides cold.
- why it matters: cold generation produces generic content-calendar carousels.
- what failure it prevents: `AI_SLOP_COPY_DRIFT`, generic remix.

### ASOT translation
- how it applies: Brand DNA Guardian pass loads the world + novelty model before Story Room writes slides.
- what post uses it: all.

### Required artifact
- `planning/format_decision.md` + (existing) `planning/memory_recall.md` / `source_winner_novelty_model.json`.

### Gate question
- "Was the ASOT brand DNA + novelty model loaded and cited before slide beats were written?"

## Needs source confirmation (do NOT treat as canonical measured findings)

These are operational syntheses used in the architecture bible; the repo backs the
underlying mechanic but not the exact formulation as a measured ASOT result:

- **"Slide 2 confirms the hook or the viewer leaves."** Repo backs slide 2 = first
  receipt / reason to continue (Carousel Wrapper Formula). The drop-off-precision
  claim is a craft tightening, not a measured ASOT per-slide retention number
  (we do not have per-slide drop-off data in the exports).
- **"Every slide survives as a screenshot."** Repo backs save/screenshot/
  message-native behavior; the strict standalone-legibility rule is a craft
  extension.
- **"Caption is the second post."** Repo backs caption structure + comment-prompt +
  "who sends this"; the "second post" framing is a synthesis.
- **"A carousel is a brand asset."** Grounded in "same people, same world" +
  page-identity; the exact phrasing is a synthesis.
- **Format-family names** (Tiny Love Proof, etc.) are labels over source-backed
  engines; the engines are canonical, the names are not measured categories.

If future work wants these promoted to measured findings, gather per-slide
retention/save data from `data/instagram/` and cite it here.
