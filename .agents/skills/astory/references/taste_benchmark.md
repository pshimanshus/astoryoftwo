# A Story of Two Research-Backed Taste Benchmark

This benchmark is built from actual A Story of Two V2 research and winner-bank
evidence.
Examples are **calibration anchors, not reusable templates.**
Do not copy example lines blindly.
Use the **source-backed winner patterns** to judge taste and then create fresh
work for the current run.

Evidence lives in `research_source_inventory.md` and the pattern cards in
`source_backed_winner_patterns.md`. The prior synthetic
charger/pillow/hoodie/umbrella scale is retired (it was not grounded in V2
winners).

## 1. Evidence standard

A benchmark example is valid only if it points to one of:
- a real source path (`data/instagram/carousel_posts/<shortcode>/carousel_export.json`),
- a winner-bank record (`references/text-style/winner-bank/winner_bank.json`),
- the outcome attribution (`references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md`),
- an engagement eval / run report (`runs/*/evals/idea_engagement_*`),
- the craft taste-corpus (`references/craft/taste-corpus.json`),
- or a brain lesson citing a run/source (`references/brain/pages/run-lessons.md`).

Invented examples are not canonical. Ranking truth = **shares/1k reach + saves,
never likes** (own winners ≈25–40 shares/1k; flops ≈4–6).

## 2. Taste scale based on actual winners

### Level 1 — Known failure / cringe
- Example: food-stealing r1 — "naraaz hone ka natak karta hoon roz."
- Source: `runs/2026-06-30_food-stealing-love/evals/creative_failure_postmortem.md`
- Also: DYkEsrfiRRw "Some days make him want silence… Maybe chaos is also home." (the **AI mood-portrait trap**), `references/craft/taste-corpus.json`, reach 20,959 / shares 92.
- Teaches: performative Hinglish + solo mood-portrait + explained joke = dead.
- Copy lesson: never narrate the feeling. Visual lesson: a solo poetic figure proves nothing. Future agents: route to a behavioral receipt.

### Level 2 — Generic but recognizable
- Example: DYxCm58CaOM "Love is not always 50-50… carries the heavier half."
- Source: `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (verbatim), reach 69,585 / shares 383 (weak).
- Also aesthetic-vague floor: DWRT1AEidUH "we matched / it's us" (reach ~3,504, ~19 shares).
- Teaches: the observation is real, but abstract/literary register = no send.
- Copy lesson: replace the abstract noun with a concrete scene. Visual lesson: needs a hard object, not a mood.

### Level 3 — Clean but ordinary
- Example: food-stealing r2 — "she said she didn't want anything."
- Source: `runs/2026-06-30_food-stealing-love/revisions/r2/planning/copy_direction.md`
- Teaches: the line is clean and human, but without a strong visual reveal it is still familiar.
- Copy lesson: clean is the floor, not the bar. Visual lesson: the image must carry the second-read. Future agents: do not stop at clean — push to a source-backed mechanic. A package that is only clean → `REPAIR_REQUIRED`.

### Level 4 — Source-backed strong
- Example: DY4tGrQCXRA send-spec "Be with someone who listens like it matters."
- Source: `references/craft/taste-corpus.json`; `data/instagram/carousel_posts/DY4tGrQCXRA/carousel_export.json` (reach 568,228 / shares 15,401).
- Also DY_t4Dek0pq observational-intimacy (highest-save engine, saved 1,771).
- Teaches: a real winning mechanic (a personal standard the reader sends as a bar; or witnessed specifics that earn saves).
- Copy lesson: specific enough to feel personal, not advice. Visual lesson: the standard/observation is enacted, not quoted.

### Level 5 — A Story-level target
- Example: DYJpjt9CQYY loving-complaint "He didn't marry peace… and somehow still smiles."
- Source: `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (verbatim); `data/instagram/carousel_posts/DYJpjt9CQYY/carousel_export.json`; `references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md` (top share-winner, 40.15 shares/1k, reach 531,805 / shares 21,374).
- Teaches the full stack: contradiction hook → escalating concrete receipts → reframe; a specific Aachu/Zuv truth; copy quieter than the visual receipts; clear send job.
- Copy lesson: the blank must be true, specific, lovable chaos. Visual lesson: each receipt is a fresh prop/scene, never repeated staging.

A package must land **closer to Level 5 than Level 3** to pass.

## 3. Winner mechanics library

Summarized from `source_backed_winner_patterns.md` (all source-cited):

- **first-frame stop** — contradiction ("He didn't marry peace"), tiny puzzle ("What is 9x0?"), or confession ("I want to know the small things").
- **swipe reason** — escalating concrete receipts; a logic resolving into a truth.
- **payoff/reveal** — reframe (chaos→patience; effort→"zero side = nothing").
- **send trigger (SHARES)** — loving-complaint, send-spec standard, one-line "tag your ___" (DYJpjt9CQYY, DY4tGrQCXRA, DZINgGKCU94).
- **save trigger (SAVES)** — observational-intimacy, mundane-future, fate-timing (DY_t4Dek0pq, DY9SfANiXWc, DZSfrNaCS4Y).
- **comment trigger** — narrow human prompt / pick-your-X (instagram-winner-mechanics #8, #17).
- **copy voice** — plain, witnessed, deadpan; real spoken Hinglish ("Main kar lungi") only when truly spoken.
- **visual proof** — one concrete object/gesture per beat (the behavioral receipt, Pattern 8).
- **A Story wrapper** — preserve the winning mechanism, stage it as a lived Aachu/Zuv scene + a final-slide payoff.

## 6. Food-stealing r3 benchmark

Compare the food-stealing idea against at least two source-backed patterns:
- **Pattern 1 (loving-complaint, DYJpjt9CQYY)** — her "I don't want anything" is the lovable chaos; receipts escalate.
- **Pattern 8 (behavioral receipt) + Pattern 3 (observational-intimacy)** — the love is the *visible* thing he already did, witnessed, not narrated.

Borrow: the **anticipation reframe** (he plans for the answer she won't admit) +
the behavioral-receipt proof. Keep fresh: do not auto-reuse "the second plate was
never extra" — that is a *direction*, not the answer; generate fresh options.
Must not become: generic food-stealing meme, sitcom dialogue, or a solo
mood-portrait.

The r3 benchmark must answer:
- Which real winning post/pattern is this closest to? (cite shortcode + path)
- What landing mechanism are we borrowing?
- What is the Aachu/Zuv behavioral truth?
- What is the visual proof?
- What is the second-read reveal?
- Why would someone send/save/comment? (which engine — shares or saves)
- What would make it a weak copy of the benchmark? (`TEMPLATE_COPYING`)
- What would make it feel like A Story of Two (closer to Level 5)?

r3 must beat: "Aachu steals food, Zuv annoyed, caption explains he ordered extra"
(generic meme) AND the r1 failure (performative Hinglish narration).

## 7. Approval rule

A package cannot PASS or NEEDS_HUMAN unless:
- it cites **at least two** relevant source-backed patterns (with shortcodes/paths),
- it explains what it borrowed,
- it explains what it changed,
- it explains why it is not a generic remix,
- it lands closer to Level 5 than Level 3,
- it writes `evals/taste_benchmark_check.md` during the run.

Verdicts:
- only clean / not source-backed strong → `REPAIR_REQUIRED`.
- cannot connect to the research corpus → `RESEARCH_EVIDENCE_MISSING`.
- copies a benchmark without transformation → `TEMPLATE_COPYING`.
- preserves the source's words but loses its mechanism → `SOURCE_MECHANIC_LOST`.

## What validation proves

`scripts/check_astory_taste_files.py` is installation-only:
**This script only validates that the taste layer is installed.** It does not
validate creative quality. Creative quality is judged per-run via
`.agents/gates/astory_content_gate.md` and `evals/taste_benchmark_check.md`, plus
human creative rejection.
