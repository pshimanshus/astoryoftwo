# ASOT Carousel Architecture Bible

Operational guide for deciding and building A Story of Two carousels. Source-backed
by `asot_carousel_mechanics.md` + `carousel_research_inventory.md`. Read those
before writing slide beats. This bible does **not** override the Creative Director
Persona or the taste bibles — a carousel still has to feel like A Story of Two, not
a content-strategy deck.

## Principle

**A carousel is a brand asset, not just a post.**

It must strengthen:
- the Aachu/Zuv world (recurring characters, continuity anchors, house style);
- emotional memory (a feeling people keep);
- profile-grid identity (it looks like ASOT at a glance);
- save/share behavior (shares/1k + saves, never likes);
- future caption/clip/repost possibility.

If it only chases one post's reach and weakens the world, it fails (`BRAND_ASSET_WEAK`).

## Format decision (single hero is the default)

Before choosing carousel, answer in `planning/format_decision.md`:

- Is the idea too layered for one image?
- Does each slide add a **new** emotional beat (not a restated point)?
- Does slide 2 prove the hook (first concrete receipt), not rephrase slide 1?
- Is there one slide people would **save/screenshot/send**?
- Does the final slide create send/share behavior?
- Does the caption add a **second post** rather than repeat the slides?
- What is the primary engine — share / save / comment?

If the answers are "no," use a **single 1080×1350 hero image**. Most ASOT winners
are single images (`story-craft.md`). Choosing a carousel with only one true beat
is `CAROUSEL_UNEARNED` / `CAROUSEL_OVEREXPLAINS_HERO_IDEA`.

`planning/format_decision.md` is **always required**, for hero runs too. If single
hero is chosen, it must still explain: why one image is stronger, why a carousel
would over-explain or dilute, which carousel-craft rule was considered, and why the
idea still has send/save/share potential without slide-2/save-worthy-slide logic.

## ASOT carousel structure (default; do not force all six)

1. **Slide 1 — public hook / familiar tension** (curiosity gap or contradiction).
2. **Slide 2 — proof the hook is worth the swipe** (first concrete receipt; "this is me").
3. **Slide 3 — specific receipt / behavioral example** (a fresh, more specific beat).
4. **Slide 4 — emotional shift / contradiction** (the unsaid becomes obvious, un-named).
5. **Slide 5 — payoff / sendable line / save-worthy thesis.**
6. **Optional caption — the second post**: context, an unseen receipt, comment/send prompt.

Use fewer slides if the idea lands earlier. Use more (up to ~8; 10 only for 10 real
beats) **only if each added slide earns a new receipt or emotional turn**
(`instagram-winner-mechanics` Carousel Wrapper Formula; `story-craft.md`: "three
real receipts beat six padded ones"). Length is set by true-receipt count.

## Slide 2 rule (slide 2 is a hard gate)

Slide 2 is not filler. **For carousel candidates, slide 2 is a hard gate.** It must:
- confirm the hook (deliver the first concrete receipt);
- make the viewer feel "okay, this is about me";
- deepen the tension / give a reason to continue;
- **not** repeat slide 1 in different words.

If slide 2 is weak or restates slide 1, the carousel fails (`SLIDE2_PROOF_MISSING`).
Record the proof in `planning/slide2_proof.md`.

## Screenshot survival rule

**Every slide must survive as a screenshot.** Screenshot each slide alone; it must
carry:
- one clear idea;
- readable, phone-legible text;
- enough visual/context to be understood without the previous slide;
- no clutter, no vague pretty filler.

A slide that only makes sense after its neighbour fails (`SCREENSHOT_SURVIVAL_FAIL`).
Record the per-slide check in `planning/slide_screenshot_survival.md`.

## Save-worthy slide rule

Every carousel needs **one** slide designed to be saved, screenshotted, or sent —
the ranking truth is saves + shares (`outcome-attribution.md`). For ASOT this may be:
a relationship receipt, a Hinglish contradiction, a line people send their partner,
a tiny love proof, a soft truth, a final tenderness line, or a mini-framework only
if it still feels human. Name the exact slide and its engine in
`planning/save_worthy_slide.md`. Missing it → `SAVE_WORTHY_SLIDE_MISSING`.

## Caption as second post (caption-as-second-post)

The caption is not an afterthought. It should:
- restate the idea in a more intimate way;
- add context or a receipt **not** in the slides;
- create a natural comment/send/save behavior;
- avoid funnel-coach CTAs;
- avoid "tag your partner if…" unless it feels genuinely ASOT.

Record it in `planning/caption_second_post.md`. A caption that just repeats the
slides is `CAPTION_SECOND_POST_MISSING`.

## ASOT content format families

Each family maps to a source-backed engine (`asot_carousel_mechanics.md`). For each:
when to use · slide shape · copy voice · visual pattern · risks · gate question.

### 1. Tiny Love Proof
- when: one small anticipatory receipt (Pattern 8). Usually a **single hero**.
- slide shape: 1 image (or 2–3 if a real second beat).
- copy voice: deadpan, quieter than the visual.
- visual pattern: one concrete object/gesture carries the care.
- risks: `CAROUSEL_UNEARNED` if padded; `CLEAN_BUT_ORDINARY`.
- gate: "Does this land in one frame? Then why a carousel?"

### 2. Hinglish Contradiction
- when: the truest line is genuinely spoken Hinglish + its subtext (Pattern 6).
- slide shape: hero, or 2 slides (line → translation).
- copy voice: real spoken Hinglish only — never transliterated for flavour.
- visual pattern: the spoken moment staged simply.
- risks: `PERFORMATIVE_HINGLISH` (the r1 failure).
- gate: "Would a real person actually say this line aloud?"

### 3. Relationship Receipt Deck
- when: multiple fresh receipts of one loving truth (Pattern 1, DYJpjt9CQYY).
- slide shape: 5–8 slides, escalating receipts, reframe payoff.
- copy voice: dry, affectionate accusation; one clause per slide.
- visual pattern: a **fresh** prop/scene each slide (never repeated staging).
- risks: repeated decorative staging; restated points; `CAROUSEL_OVEREXPLAINS_HERO_IDEA`.
- gate: "Does each slide add a NEW receipt?"

### 4. Couple Ritual Montage
- when: one recurring ritual shown as it repeats/evolves (winner-mech #15; Pattern 4).
- slide shape: 4–6 slides; the same action moves/returns each slide.
- copy voice: minimal; the repetition is the meaning.
- visual pattern: one object/action recurs with small change.
- risks: monotony if nothing changes per slide.
- gate: "Does the ritual change or deepen each slide?"

### 5. Soft Micro-Essay Carousel
- when: witnessed specifics build to a soft truth (Pattern 3, DY_t4Dek0pq saves).
- slide shape: 4–7 slides; list of hyper-specific witnessed details → name/refuse.
- copy voice: tender, plain, first-person; never "little things matter."
- visual pattern: small private observed behaviours, one per beat.
- risks: `EMOTIONALLY_VAGUE` / abstract-line flop (DYxCm58CaOM).
- gate: "Is each detail witnessed and specific, not declared?"

### 6. Visual Photo-Essay / Vlog-in-Illustrations
- when: a feeling that changes through time/place (`story-craft.md` visual storytelling).
- slide shape: sequence of scenes; the frame is the actor.
- copy voice: sparse; the image carries it.
- visual pattern: varied camera/blocking; not the default sideways two-face close-up.
- risks: pretty filler; identity/anatomy risk across many renders.
- gate: "Does each frame advance the feeling, or is it decoration?"

### 7. Philosophical Vignette
- when: a small standard/reframe law (Pattern 2, DY4tGrQCXRA send-spec).
- slide shape: hero or short carousel (setup → reframe).
- copy voice: plain, declarative, personal — not advice/therapy.
- visual pattern: one enacted standard, not a quote card.
- risks: `PREACHY_THERAPY_LANGUAGE`; "choose someone who…" register.
- gate: "Is it a lived scene, or a quote-card lesson?"

### 8. Identity-Led Showcase
- when: the Aachu/Zuv world itself is the draw (brand-asset, M8).
- slide shape: carousel that showcases the recurring world/continuity.
- copy voice: warm, in-world.
- visual pattern: strong continuity anchors, house style.
- risks: `BRAND_ASSET_WEAK` if it becomes generic; identity drift across slides.
- gate: "Does it strengthen the world, or just decorate it?"

## Hard rejects

Reject carousels that:
- exist only because "carousels perform";
- explain one image across many slides (`CAROUSEL_OVEREXPLAINS_HERO_IDEA`);
- repeat the hook on slide 2 (`SLIDE2_PROOF_MISSING`);
- have no save-worthy slide (`SAVE_WORTHY_SLIDE_MISSING`);
- use pretty illustrations as filler / any slide that fails standalone (`SCREENSHOT_SURVIVAL_FAIL`);
- have no caption strategy (`CAPTION_SECOND_POST_MISSING`);
- could be posted by any generic couple page (`BRAND_ASSET_WEAK`);
- weaken Aachu/Zuv identity continuity;
- introduce too many anatomy/face risks without mitigation;
- become quote-card romance;
- cite no source-backed carousel mechanic (`CAROUSEL_SOURCE_EVIDENCE_MISSING`);
- ship without `planning/format_decision.md` (`FORMAT_DECISION_MISSING`).
