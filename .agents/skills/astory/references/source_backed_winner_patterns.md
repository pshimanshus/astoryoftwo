## Pattern 1 — Loving-complaint ("He didn't marry ___")

### Source evidence
- path: `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (DYJpjt9CQYY, DYaCIwAiYX9, DZINgGKCU94); `data/instagram/carousel_posts/DYJpjt9CQYY/carousel_export.json`; `references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md`
- type: own illustrated carousels, verbatim slides read 2026-06-24
- metric signal: DYJpjt9CQYY reach 531,805 / shares 21,374 / saved 6,990 (top share-winner); DYaCIwAiYX9 reach 445,729 / shares 6,787
- id: DYJpjt9CQYY (winner), DYaCIwAiYX9 (solid)
- permission: own work, fully reusable mechanic
- reuse: mechanic reusable; exact lines are NOT (fresh receipts each time)

### What worked
- first-frame stop: a curiosity-gap contradiction ("He didn't marry peace.")
- swipe reason: escalating **staged receipts**, each a fresh prop/scene
- emotional trigger: "being too much is safe with the right person"
- send reason: shares (you send it about your own chaos / your person's)
- payoff: reframe the chaos as patience/choice ("Maybe love is not finding calm")
- why new: a familiar "my partner is dramatic" truth told as affectionate proof, not complaint

### Copy mechanics
- length: short slide lines; one clause each
- voice: dry, affectionate, specific
- shape: hook contradiction → 3–4 escalating receipts → reframe
- type: loving accusation → reveal
- must not polish away: the **specific lovable chaos** in the blank; vague = dead

### Visual mechanics
- scene type: per-slide fresh prop/scene receipt ("no shoes on", "10 moods before breakfast")
- proof: each receipt is a concrete object/behavior, not a stated trait
- composition: carousel, 5–8 slides
- A Story risk: receipts becoming repeated identical staging (run-lessons: staging goes decorative)

### A Story translation
- Aachu's specific lovable chaos as escalating staged receipts; Zuv's steady smile as the reframe
- stays: the "didn't marry ___" engine + escalation + reframe
- changes: fresh receipts/props each run
- not slop: never a generic "she's crazy but I love her" meme
- likely blockers: `CAROUSEL_UNEARNED` if receipts don't escalate; repeated staging

### Anti-copy rule
- do NOT reuse "mujhe kuch nahi hua" or "He didn't marry peace" verbatim
- preserve: the contradiction→receipts→reframe mechanism only

## Pattern 2 — Send-spec standard ("Be with someone who ___" / reframe law)

### What worked
- first-frame: a standard or a tiny puzzle ("Can I ask you a question? What is 9x0?")
- swipe reason: a small logic that resolves into a relationship truth
- trigger: the reader can send it AS a bar/standard for how they want to be loved
- send reason: shares (DZNl2zxiRD4 is the reach champion)
- payoff: reframe ("if one side stays zero, the result is always nothing")
- why new: a math/standard frame makes an old truth (mutual effort) feel proven

### Copy mechanics
- shape: direct-address standard OR puzzle→reveal
- voice: plain, declarative, personal-not-advice
- must not polish away: the standard must be **specific enough to feel personal**, not generic advice ("be with someone who listens like it matters", not "find someone good")

### Visual mechanics
- scene: simple staged dialogue or one clear standard moment
- proof: the reframe lands visually (the zero, the listening posture)

### A Story translation
- Aachu/Zuv enacting the standard as a lived scene, not a quote card
- stays: the standard/puzzle→reveal; changes: the specific bar
- not slop: avoid "choose someone who..." therapy-advice register (anti-slop banned)

## Pattern 3 — Observational-intimacy (highest SAVE engine)

### Source evidence
- path: `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (DY_t4Dek0pq, DZjf5OxCR4Z)
- metric: DY_t4Dek0pq reach 104,806 / saved 1,771 (highest-save engine); DZjf5OxCR4Z shares 4,994 / saved 1,522
- reuse: mechanic only

### What worked
- first-frame: a confession hook ("I want to know the small things you don't think matter.")
- swipe reason: absurdly mundane TRUE details (sings in the shower, checks the door twice, socks before pants, how you eat when comfortable)
- trigger: **saves** — "details so specific they feel witnessed, not written"
- payoff: name the feeling ("observational intimacy"); or refuse the diminutive ("the little things were never little")
- why new: specificity so narrow it feels like being seen

### Copy mechanics
- shape: confession hook → list of hyper-specific mundane details → name/refuse
- voice: tender, plain, first-person
- must not polish away: the **specificity**; generic "little things" kills it

### Visual mechanics
- scene: small private observed behaviors, one per beat
- proof: the behavior is the content (the door checked twice, the shower singing)

### A Story translation
- Zuv noticing the un-performed parts of Aachu (and vice versa)
- stays: hyper-specific witnessed detail; changes: which details
- not slop: never "I love everything about you"; must be witnessed, not declared
- send/save: saves engine — aspirational, kept

## Pattern 6 — Hinglish character-line (the true spoken line)

### Source evidence
- path: `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (DYoEl5jicte, DZINgGKCU94)
- metric: DYoEl5jicte reach 126,124 / saved 1,493 (solid); DZINgGKCU94 reach 70,632
- reuse: mechanic only

### What worked
- the truest line is often **what one of them actually says**, rendered romanized: "Main kar lungi." → "Translation: don't go far."
- trigger: recognition ("tag your ___"); the translation reveals the subtext
- why new: real speech + its hidden meaning, not a written caption

### Copy mechanics
- shape: real romanized spoken line → deadpan "Translation:" of the subtext
- voice: actually spoken, not composed
- must not polish away: it must be a line a real person SAYS; this is the controlled, EARNED Hinglish (contrast the banned performative Hinglish in `negative_regression_examples.md`)

### Visual mechanics: the spoken moment staged simply; the line is the proof.
### A Story translation: Aachu/Zuv's real catchphrases + their tender translation.
### Anti-copy: Hinglish only if genuinely spoken; never transliterated for flavor (`PERFORMATIVE_HINGLISH`).

## Pattern 9 — Send vs Save split (rank by shares/1k, not likes)

### Source evidence
- path: `references/brain/pages/outcome-attribution.md`; `references/brain/reports/outcome_attribution.json`; `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (`send_job` field)
- metric: own winners 25–40 shares/1k (DYJpjt9CQYY 40.15); flops 4–6 shares/1k

### What worked / how to use
- **SHARES engines** (loving-complaint, send-spec, weather-app one-liner): "send this about us / send to him." Pick when the goal is reach/sends.
- **SAVES engines** (observational-intimacy, mundane-future, fate-timing): aspirational, kept. Pick when the goal is saves.
- every concept must name its send/save/comment job (taste-corpus `send_job`); rank by shares/1k + saves, never likes.

### Anti-copy: do not optimize for likes; a high-like, low-share post is a flop (DZNl2zxiRD4-vs-likes note in outcome-attribution).

## Pattern 10 — Known anti-patterns (the real failure register)

### Source evidence
- path: `references/craft/taste-corpus.json` (DYkEsrfiRRw, DYxCm58CaOM, DWRT1AEidUH, DWY9KNmCSbL); `references/brain/pages/run-lessons.md`; `runs/2026-06-30_food-stealing-love/evals/creative_failure_postmortem.md`
- metric: DYkEsrfiRRw reach 20,959 / shares 92 (anti); DYxCm58CaOM shares 383 (weak); aesthetic-vague floor 2–3k reach, ~10 shares

### What failed (do not repeat)
- **AI mood-portrait trap** (DYkEsrfiRRw): "Some days make him want silence… Chai. Drama. One long story… Maybe chaos is also home." — solo figure, vague poetic, no turn, no send. The single worst own-pattern.
- **Abstract line** (DYxCm58CaOM): "Love is not always 50-50… carries the heavier half." — right idea, abstract/literary register, low reach. Needed a hard concrete scene.
- **Aesthetic-vague** (DWRT1AEidUH, DWY9KNmCSbL): "we matched / it's us / forever" — emoji/mood, near-zero shares.
- **Performative meme/sitcom Hinglish** (food-stealing r1 postmortem).

### Anti-copy rule
- if a concept reads as mood-portrait, abstract lesson, aesthetic-vague, or
  performative Hinglish → it sits with the flops, not the winners. Repair to a
  concrete behavioral receipt (Pattern 8) before proceeding.
