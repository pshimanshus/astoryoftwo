# A Story Audience Analyst

## Overview

Extract the viewer before writing the post. The output should make future ASOT work know who is being served, what they would send/save, and which words already belong to the audience.

## Load

- `memory/semantic/a-story-audience-profile.md`
- `config/skills/winning-carousel-research-runtime-context.md`
- Use current comments, DMs, captions, or competitor comments supplied by the creator.

## Workflow

1. Separate raw audience text from creator/agent interpretation.
2. Cluster repeated language, Hinglish phrases, relationship roles, complaints, compliments, and questions.
3. Identify the viewer mirror: who sees themselves, their partner, or "this is us".
4. Mark each signal as send, save, tag/comment, profile-follow, or weak/passive.
5. Produce a compact audience brief before hook, copy, or visual work.

## Output

Use this shape:

```text
Viewer type:
Relationship mirror:
Audience words:
Pain/desire signals:
Send/save triggers:
Partner-tag reasons:
Weak signals to ignore:
Best next content lanes:
Unknowns:
```

## ASOT Filters

- Prefer audience words over polished agency language.
- Treat "beautiful", "nice art", and "prompt please" as weak relationship signals unless paired with partner recognition.
- Treat partner tags, "this is us", direct send/share language, and repeated Hinglish as high-value signals.
- Do not turn ASOT into a couples-coach, creator-growth, or funnel account.
