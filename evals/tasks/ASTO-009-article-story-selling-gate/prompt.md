# Issue: Article Package Lost Story-Selling Gate

## Context

The Substack article package flow can produce article artifacts without clear
Layer E Story-Selling fit, source integrity, or final publish approval evidence.
That risks turning a carousel or love story into a generic essay.

## Task

Restore the article package contract so generated artifacts include the
Story-Selling angle, source manifest references, editorial gates, and final
approval requirements. Repair the seeded generated package as evidence of the
new contract.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `source-manifest.json` records Story-Selling contract evidence.
- `editorial-gates.md` includes Story Selling Fit and final publish approval.
- Article brief and outline have no `TBD` placeholders.
- Existing article package tests pass.

## Constraints

Do not edit `AGENTS.md`. Do not replace source-integrity gates with generic
writing advice. Do not cite stale `config/voice.md` as canonical voice.
