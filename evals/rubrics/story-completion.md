# Final story draft completion

Use for a requested final carousel draft after a concept has been selected.
This is a semantic review of the delivered copy and scene sequence, not an
image QA check, reach prediction, or requirement to turn every joke into seven
slides. Apply the current creator-selected architecture. For a fresh story
without an alternative, use the storytelling hook's default or reflective
route. Phases may span slides; slides must earn their place.

Read the request, surviving concept/copy locks, rejected routes, and complete
candidate before scoring. Cite exact lines, slide positions, and actions.
Privately map what the phases actually do; headings cannot prove development.
Do not infer an unwritten response from a direction such as "she starts
answering" when the answer itself is needed to resolve the opening need.

## Dimensions

- **Lock continuity, 0–2:** 0 replaces the approved premise or violates an exact
  lock; 1 retains the topic but drops its central promise; 2 preserves the
  surviving premise, emotional pressure, and exact locked wording. Liking a
  concept is not blanket approval of all provisional wording.
- **Story architecture, 0–3:** 0 no coherent selected structure; 1 an exchange
  or labelled outline substitutes for developed story jobs; 2 the selected
  phases are present, ordered and functional; 3 each phase contributes and the
  cover promise governs the ending. Do not score by counting slides or labels.
- **Causal development, 0–3:** 0 disconnected claims; 1 recognition and an
  immediate repair skip pressure or choice; 2 distinct receipts develop the
  need and cause the turn; 3 both partners' positions and responses produce an
  earned emotional progression. Conflict need not be an argument.
- **Earned payoff, 0–3:** 0 no answer to the opening; 1 only an invitation,
  proposed repair, generic moral, or missing response; 2 the ending makes the
  relevant changed state or meaning legible; 3 the specific ending gathers
  earlier evidence and naturally carries the partner-send reason. An invitation
  can be a complete payoff if being invited was the opening need; a wordless
  response can complete a story. Judge the actual causal chain.
- **Human voice, 0–1:** 0 generic advice, framework language or manufactured
  dialogue; 1 spoken or reflective copy belongs to these scenes.

Pass: at least 10/12; lock continuity must be nonzero; architecture, causal
development, and payoff must each reach 2. Strong prose cannot compensate for a
missing turn or ending. The review must be from a different author/reviewer,
bind the requested draft's current SHA-256, and include evidence for every
dimension. No review means PENDING. Mechanical checks validate these review
conditions; they do not establish the truth of a subjective score.

## Calibration failure

recognizable feeling but omitted the proposed public cover, compressed the
middle to household requests, and stopped at "Achha batao. Kaisa tha aaj?"
with the instruction "End here, with their conversation beginning."
The creator rejected its storytelling format. Use it as a negative example of
concept-to-final compression, not a ban on bedside stories, short carousels,
quiet endings, or this approved concept. Adding two filler slides or phase
labels cannot repair it. A corrected draft needs fresh independent judgment.
