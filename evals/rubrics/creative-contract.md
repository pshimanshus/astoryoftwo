## Use

Use this rubric only after mechanical gates pass. It is for creator-facing
artifacts where deterministic checks cannot fully judge whether the output is
alive, specific, and useful.

## Anchored Dimensions

Seed preservation, 0-3:

- 0: replaces or ignores the seed.
- 1: mentions the seed but turns it generic.
- 2: preserves the seed and gives one concrete scene.
- 3: preserves exact emotional pressure, language, and couple-specific proof.

Scene proof, 0-3:

- 0: quote card or moral with no behavior.
- 1: generic couple pose.
- 2: action, object, reaction, or body language proves the line.
- 3: scene still reads when text is hidden.

Format judgment, 0-2:

- 0: no format recommendation.
- 1: format chosen but not justified by the moment.
- 2: format choice follows hook, retention, payoff, and production gates.

Creator-facing taste, 0-2:

- 0: exposes internal framework terms or score tables.
- 1: mostly clean but still process-heavy.
- 2: reads like a useful creative partner, not an audit report.

Relationship motion, 0-2:

- 0: defaults to caretaker trope.
- 1: partially shows mutual or story-specific motion.
- 2: the relationship dynamic is visible, specific, and non-generic.

Pass requires at least 9/12, with no zero in seed preservation or scene proof.
