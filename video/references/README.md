## style-evidence/ — the look, as already shipped

Three published carousel slides in the active illustration profile.

| File | Scene | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| `style-01-crowd-street-duniya-bolegi.png` | Couple walking a crowded street | Sharp couple held against an impressionistic dissolving crowd — the depth trick that makes a flat frame cinematic |
| `style-02-bedroom-absolute-worst.png` | Couple on the floor by an unmade bed | Warm interior light, lived-in mess as emotional texture, tenderness carried in posture rather than facial close-up |
| `style-03-cafe-doorway-mere-husband.png` | Couple stepping out of a café doorway | Daylight exterior, dappled foliage shadow, comic beat played entirely through body language |

Consistent across all three: warm ivory paper ground with visible tooth,
watercolour-and-ink finish, desaturated palette with olive/cream/denim anchors,
handwritten caption type in generous negative space, brandmark top-right.

**Read for video:** every one of these is a held moment with implied motion
already in it — a walk, a lean, a step through a door. They are closer to film
frames than to posters. That is the bridge.
