durable_decisions:
- A story requires meaningful causal or emotional change; polish cannot replace
  a missing before/after movement.
- ERCRT, WHW, and neuroscience language stay private unless the creator asks
  for craft analysis and any scientific claim is separately verified.
- The private Story State Card persists across follow-ups; current creator
  corrections supersede stale routes while untouched facts and locks remain.
- The storytelling hook runs at the start of every @a.storyof.two creative
  session and before every brainstorming, idea-generation, comparison,
  discussion, rejection, revision, critique, or production continuation.
- Preserve exact rejection feedback and scope. Never polish or rename a closed
  route as fresh. Carry durable closed lanes and reusable repair conditions
  through `memory/semantic/carousel-idea-preferences.md`, then recall relevant
  collisions before future concept generation.
- The reflective six-beat carousel architecture `Cover -> Cold Open ->
  Deepening -> Conflict -> Turn -> Payoff` is a first-class alternate, not an
  inferior compression of the default seven-beat architecture. Preserve it
  when the creator supplies, approves, or continues that storytelling style.
- Those six labels are ordered story phases, not a six-slide limit. Deepening,
  Conflict, and Turn may each span multiple slides when every added scene moves
  an honest question-and-answer chain through active characters, rising
  pressure, visible consequence, or an earned reframe. Couple stories keep at
  least two causal characters; add more only when they change the story.
- Detailed craft lives only in the canonical method above. Other skill,
  runtime, and memory surfaces point to it instead of restating it.
