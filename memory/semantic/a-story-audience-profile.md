# A Story Audience Profile

## Core Viewer

The strongest viewer is a warm desi relationship viewer who recognizes a small
couple rhythm and wants to send it to their partner. They are not asking for
relationship advice. They want language for a private dynamic: "this is us",
"this is me", "this is her", "this is him", or "send this to your calm one."

## Strong Signals

- partner tags with little or no explanation;
- comments that say "this is us";
- saves because the final line gives language to a real couple dynamic;
- shares because the post names a feeling they could not say cleanly;
- Hinglish phrases that feel spoken, not translated;
- specific domestic/travel objects acting as relationship proof.

## Weak Signals

- "beautiful" without relationship recognition;
- "nice art" as the main reaction;
- "prompt please";
- generic hearts with no partner tag or story recognition;
- admiration for the illustration before the couple truth.

## Send/Save Triggers

Viewers send/save when a post gives them:

- a public hook they understand instantly;
- a private receipt they can map onto their own relationship;
- one line that says what they mean to their partner;
- a funny surface beat that turns tender without becoming generic;
- a caption that adds one more true receipt instead of repeating the slides.

## Persona Constraints

- Keep ASOT personal, warm, Hinglish-friendly, and relationship-specific.
- Do not write as a couples-coach, Instagram-growth educator, or funnel account.
- Use audience language as seasoning, not as a script to copy.
- Prefer broad couple recognition with ASOT-specific proof over narrow private
  lore that requires backstory.
