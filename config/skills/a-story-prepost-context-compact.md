# A Story Pre-Post Context Compact

Judge the planned Reel as a relationship story before optimizing distribution.
Preserve supplied concept, hook, caption, edit, audio, and cover wording exactly.
Unknown inputs remain unknown and must lower confidence rather than invite guesses.

## Five Facets

- B1 Hook `/10`: visual, spoken, and text channels; hook killers; DM-send signal.
- B2 Edit `/35`: retention `/10`, loop `/5`, audio `/5`, plus actionable
  b-roll, text-overlay, cover, and edit-structure findings.
- B3 Algorithm `/70`: DM-send `/25`, saves `/15`, low-skip `/15`,
  watch-through `/10`, and audio timing `/5`.
- B4 Caption `/35`: voice, hook line, keywords, CTA, length, and rewrites only
  when useful.
- B5 Culture `/50`: channel theme `/10`, Indian authenticity `/10`, Kashmiri
  signal `/10`, differentiation `/10`, and cultural send trigger. A concept
  without a natural Kashmiri angle receives a neutral score, not a forced one.

## Verdict Contract

The composite maximum is 200. Base tiers are POST `160-200`, REVISE `120-159`,
REWORK `80-119`, and KILL below `80`. Missing audio subtracts 10 before tiering.
Hook below 5 caps at REWORK. DM-send below 10 or cultural authenticity below 5
caps at REVISE. Layer E REPAIR, REWORK, and STOP cap at REVISE, REWORK, and
KILL. Scores remain evidence summaries; Layer E story failure cannot be hidden
by distribution mechanics.

Return concrete evidence, uncertainties, and the smallest useful repairs. Do
not promise virality, invent performance evidence, force Hinglish or Kashmiri
identity, or replace the couple story with generic algorithm advice.
