## Purpose

Use this as the compact context for researching and applying winning Instagram
carousel mechanics to @a.storyof.two. It is a research helper, not a public
voice guide and not a replacement for `config/rules/`.

## Reference Learnings

The attached carousel-skill screenshots taught these reusable mechanics:

- slide 2 confirms the hook; if slide 2 does not prove the first swipe was
  worth it, the viewer leaves.
- Every slide should work as a screenshot: one clear idea, enough context, high
  contrast, and no clutter.
- Every carousel needs one slide worth saving: a line, framework, prompt,
  fill-in-the-blank, or emotional thesis sharp enough to stand alone.
- The caption is the second post: it should restate the idea, add context or
  receipts, and create the natural comment/send/save behavior.
- feed brand DNA before asking for a carousel: audience, voice, transformation
  story, content pillars, and forbidden tones must be available before a
  "viral" prompt can work.
- Skill stacks beat one giant prompt: audience analyst, hook generator, swipe
  architect, brand guidance, visual storyteller, caption engine, and repurposer
  each solve a different bottleneck.

## ASOT Translation

Do not copy Instagram-growth account posture. Translate mechanics into ASOT:

```text
public relationship hook
-> slide 2 proof
-> concrete couple receipts
-> visible relationship motion
-> one save-worthy line
-> caption as second post
-> repurpose winner into Reel/Story/article seeds
```

The strongest ASOT signal is not "nice art". It is a viewer thinking "this is
us" and sending, saving, tagging, or commenting with one specific partner in
mind.

## Research Inputs

Use these before making broad claims:

- current ASOT posts and comments when available;
- supplied screenshots or saved Instagram examples;
- competitor posts and comments as mechanics, not voice to copy;
- `wiki/insights/successful-carousel-standard.md`;
- `output/reports/2026-05-24-carousel-performance-autopsy.md`;
- `output/reports/2026-06-06-instagram-page-and-competitor-analysis.md`;
- current official sources for Instagram, Codex, OpenAI, or platform behavior
  when the recommendation depends on what is current.

## Output Standard

A good research synthesis names:

- audience mirror;
- winning hook mechanics;
- slide 2 mechanism;
- save-worthy slide pattern;
- caption/CTA pattern;
- visual proof pattern;
- ASOT adaptation;
- do-not-copy notes;
- current-source caveats.

## Hard Fails

- Treating screenshots as ASOT visual style.
- Turning the page into an Instagram-growth or couples-coach account.
- Claiming exact algorithm weights without current official sources.
- Letting a carousel pass with pretty slides but no send/save reason.
