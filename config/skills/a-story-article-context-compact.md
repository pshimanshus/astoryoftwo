# A Story Article Context Compact

Use quietly for article and Substack requests.

- Keep the piece warm, personal, specific, and emotionally honest.
- Build from a lived scene or carousel spine, not abstract relationship advice.
- Preserve the couple's private texture while making the reader recognize
  something from their own relationship.
- Avoid coach language, listicle framing, clinical analysis, and generic romance
  claims.
- Open `config/rules/voice.md`, `config/rules/golden-theme.md`,
  `config/rules/story-selling.md`, or
  `config/skills/couple-substack-article-framework.md` only when the task needs
  full wording.
