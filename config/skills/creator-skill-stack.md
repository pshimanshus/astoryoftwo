## Purpose

Use this as the compact creator operating hook before @a.storyof.two idea
sessions, carousel jams, Reel routes, caption routes, and format decisions. It
turns broad creator craft into repeatable checks without replacing the free
creative pass.

This file is an execution switch, not public copy. Do not expose the skill
names to the audience.

## Mandatory Storytelling Change Hook

At the start of every @a.storyof.two creative chat, and before every continuing
creative response, invoke
`.agents/skills/a-story-storytelling-hook/SKILL.md`. That skill owns the Story
State Card, change logic, ERCRT/WHW diagnostics, and continuous-chat
corrections. This stack adds only channel-level scroll-stop, recognition,
scene-proof, retention, payoff, format, taste, and DM-send checks. Do not copy
the story engine into this file.

This is mandatory during brainstorming, concept or idea generation, thinking
aloud, comparison, discussion, rejection, revision, critique, copy work,
visual work, and packaging. A creator message does not need to say "story" or
"carousel" again when the active conversation is already creative.

## Session Start Hook

At the start of any @a.storyof.two creative session, define the active creator
skills before suggesting ideas:

- Scroll-Stop Skill: what makes the first frame or first line impossible to
  ignore in under two seconds.
- Recognition Skill: who thinks "this is me", "this is her", or "this is us".
- Story Change Skill: what is different between the before-state and the
  after-state, and what choice or pressure causes it.
- Emotional Contradiction Skill: what tension makes the love feel alive instead
  of decorative.
- Scene-Proof Skill: what visible behavior, object, place, body language, or
  consequence proves the feeling without explanation.
- Retention Ladder Skill: why the viewer needs the next beat.
- Payoff Skill: the final send/save thesis, not a soft moral.
- Format Remix Skill: which proven shell fits best: POV, confession, then/now,
  expectation/reality, label-pair, list, receipt trail, or short scene.
- Audience Mirror Skill: whether the route travels globally, in India, or only
  inside the creator's private context.
- Volume Skill: how the same spine can become Reel, carousel, story prompt,
  caption, comment bait, or article seed.
- Taste Gate Skill: what gets rejected before polish makes it look acceptable.

If a request is tiny, run the hook mentally and answer simply. If it is a jam,
make the hook explicit in the private decision record before concept lock.

## Jam Hook

Before showing creator-facing concept suggestions, every jam must pass this
sequence:

1. Define the audience mirror.
2. Write the scroll-stop claim or first visible contradiction. Treat it as the
   actual cover/slide one, not an extra title card: the strongest public hook,
   close human emotion or unmistakable action, and one instantly legible visual
   contradiction must create a swipe question in under one second. Reject calm
   portraits, evidence flat-lays, decorative prop spreads, disembodied-hands
   layouts, and any cover that repeats the same hook on the next swipe.
3. Lock the change row: before-state, pressure or choice, and after-state.
4. Name the emotional contradiction.
5. State the honest climax hint and reason to continue without spoiling the
   payoff.
6. Stage one concrete scene receipt.
7. Pick the strongest format shell.
8. Check the middle beat for a caused emotional shift, not just sweetness.
9. Check the ending with the DM Send Test.
10. Reject lanes that are generic, stale, unstageable, unchanged, or only
    pretty.

## DM Send Test

A concept passes only if a viewer can send it to one specific person with a
natural reason:

- "this is us"
- "this is you"
- "this is her"
- "we do this"
- "I want this kind of love"
- "I did not know how to say this, but this says it"

If the likely share reason is only "nice line" or "pretty art", return REPAIR.

## Content Engines

Use these engines when jamming beyond tiny things:

| Engine | Use When | Example Signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chosen family | love becomes the official person | emergency contact, forms, hospital chair |
| Witness | love remembers the becoming version | drafts, old plans, failed attempts |
| Dignity | love protects the room during conflict | no public mocking, no cruel exposing |
| Shared adulthood | romance hides inside responsibility | bills, rent, repairs, paperwork |
| Two cultures, one home | love merges worlds without flattening them | Kashmiri/UP language, food, rituals |
| Repair language | apology arrives through action | collar fix, water kept, room softened |
| Private mythology | the couple has its own tiny universe | labels, recurring phrases, alibis |

## Hard Rejects

Return REPAIR before showing an idea if:

- the hook needs private context to make sense;
- the idea is only a quote in prettier clothes;
- the middle slides do not escalate;
- the ending is a moral instead of a send/save thesis;
- the visual proof is two people standing beside text;
- the lane repeats cooled-down surfaces without a new incident;
- the concept could belong to any generic couple account.
