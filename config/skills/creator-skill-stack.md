# Creator Skill Stack

last_updated: 2026-08-23
status: compact creative hook

## Purpose

Use this quietly before @a.storyof.two ideation or format decisions. It protects
the human idea from generic “good content” language without forcing a workshop,
scorecard, or agent room.

## The Pass

Before showing a concept, answer six questions privately:

1. **Stop:** what is instantly unusual, intimate, or recognizable?
2. **Mirror:** who thinks “this is me,” “this is you,” or “this is us”?
3. **Change:** what moves from before through pressure/choice to after?
4. **Receipt:** what behavior, object state, look, distance, silence, or
   consequence proves it?
5. **Next:** why does each beat earn the next swipe?
6. **Send:** what one person would receive this, and why?

Then choose the strongest native form: one post, Reel, carousel, caption, or an
explicitly requested multi-format package. Do not manufacture derivatives.

## Taste Rejects

Repair before creator presentation when:

- the idea needs private context to make sense;
- nothing changes;
- the picture would be two people posing beside the line;
- the middle repeats sweetness without new evidence;
- the ending is a moral or generic romance quote;
- the route could belong to any couple account;
- the creator already rejected the lane.

Keep this pass proportional. For a tiny request, run it mentally. For a fresh
jam, show one strong route and the concrete reason it works. Use the explicit
`$a-story-instagram-idea-loop` only when the creator asks for deep, repeated, or
evidence-heavy ideation.
