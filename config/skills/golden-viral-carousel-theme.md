# Golden Viral Carousel Theme

## Purpose

Use this before planning, writing, reviewing, or generating any illustrated
carousel for @a.storyof.two.

The golden theme is the universal relationship truth that made the strongest
carousel work: Aachu's expressive, dramatic, loving energy meets Zuv's calm,
patient, choosing-her energy. The exact wording can change. The theme grammar
must not.

The goal is not to make every carousel say "He didn't marry..." The goal is to
make every carousel feel instantly recognizable to strangers and still
unmistakably about Aachu and Zuv.

Read the detailed reference when making a new concept or fixing one that feels
too narrow:

`config/references/golden-viral-carousel-theme-reference.md`

## First-Principles Source Gate

Before submitting, recommending, scoring, packaging, or generating any new
carousel idea, read these files and use them as the first-principles source:

- `wiki/insights/successful-carousel-standard.md`
- `wiki/themes/calm-enough-for-chaos.md`
- `output/reports/2026-05-17-he-didnt-marry-peace-viral-theme-analysis.md`

From `wiki/insights/successful-carousel-standard.md`, define what success
looks like before writing or selecting a route: audience success, creative success,
brand success, and production success. If those four success targets are
missing, the carousel room has not completed the source-memory gate.

The key learning is that the gold carousel worked because Aachu's expressive,
funny, desi emotional chaos was made safe by Zuv's active patience. The
copyable unit is not the literal "He didn't marry..." wording. The copyable
unit is the emotional machine:

```text
universal anti-ideal
-> concrete Aachu proof
-> Zuv steady response
-> tender acceptance thesis
```

Do not submit an idea until you can say how it preserves that machine or why it
is intentionally departing from it.

## Persistent Idea Memory Gate

Before submitting, recommending, scoring, packaging, or generating any new
carousel idea, read:

- `memory/semantic/carousel-idea-preferences.md`

This file is the persistent creator preference ledger. It records concepts and
lanes that were recommended, rejected, packaged, or cooled down in prior
sessions. Treat it as memory that survives the current chat.

Rules:

- Do not present a listed concept or close emotional lane as "fresh" unless the
  creator explicitly asks to revisit it.
- Exclude rejected lanes from the tournament unless you can clearly name the
  repair condition and show how the new version avoids the rejected surface.
- If a concept was only recommended but not selected, avoid recycling it in the
  next jam unless it is materially transformed.
- After the creator accepts, rejects, or cools down a recommendation, update
  the ledger immediately with date, concept, lane, status, reason, and
  confidence.
- Keep `memory/working.md` as a short pointer only; the semantic ledger is the
  durable source for idea preferences.

## Story Director Persona Gate

After the first-principles memory and persistent idea memory are loaded, but
before writing hooks, slide copy, captions, visual directions, prompt packs, or
image-generation instructions, load:

- `config/skills/carousel-story-director-persona.md`
- `config/skills/continuous-carousel-agent-room.md`

This persona must stay active through final native image sets and QA. It checks
that every direction has a real hook, setup, proof, escalation, bridge, active
Zuv role, earned ending, and send/save reason. If a concept only has a good
line without story structure, mark it REPAIR.

For serious carousel concepts, the continuous agent room is a hard gate. It
must generate multiple routes, debate why each can or cannot work, repair the
top 2-3 candidates, and return a selector verdict before final copy or image
generation. Do not collapse the room into debating only the first favorite.

## Non-Negotiables

1. Start with a universal relationship truth.
   - The first idea must be understandable without seeing the original photo.
   - A follower should think, "This is us," or "I know this couple."

2. Use Aachu and Zuv as the specific proof.
   - Aachu brings the plot: emotion, chaos, hunger, overthinking, drama,
     softness, main-character energy, sudden mood shifts, or tiny rituals.
   - Zuv brings the safety: patience, snacks, tissues, chai, silence, calm
     posture, amused smile, or quietly choosing her again.
   - This is a creative/visual requirement, not a mandate to name Aachu and
     Zuv in every visible slide or caption. Public copy should usually stay
     universal unless names are emotionally necessary.

3. Treat photos, places, outfits, and objects as evidence, not the premise.
   - A red bag, waterfall, shoes, food, flowers, or a city view can prove the
     theme.
   - They cannot be the whole theme by themselves.

4. Build a shareable arc.
   - Hook: universal tension or relationship recognition.
   - Reveal: why this is specifically Aachu and Zuv.
   - Proof: one concrete visual behavior.
   - Role: show what the other partner does emotionally.
   - Payoff: a save/share line about love, not just the object or trip.

5. Keep the humor affectionate.
   - Never make Aachu look cruel, foolish, or too much in a mean way.
   - Never make Zuv look passive or lifeless. His calm is active love.

6. Write captions from the same universal theme.
   - First line should reopen the universal truth.
   - Middle lines can show Aachu/Zuv specifics through behavior, care, posture,
     objects, and relationship rhythm. Name them only when it feels natural.
   - Ending should be a send/save/commentable relationship line.

## Golden Filter

Before approving any carousel idea, answer these:

- Can someone share this with their partner even if they do not know Aachu and
  Zuv?
- Does the idea still work if the object/place/outfit is removed?
- Does Aachu have a clear expressive role and Zuv a clear steady role?
- Does the public copy avoid unnecessary private-name repetition?
- Are the proof beats drawable as simple scenes?
- Does the final line say something true about love, patience, chaos, home,
  care, or being chosen?

If any answer is no, revise the concept before writing slide copy.

## Low-Text Poster Rule

The illustration should carry most of the story. Slide copy is a poster line,
not a caption.

- Default to one short sentence or fragment per slide.
- Aim for 3-8 words per slide.
- Do not use two explanatory sentences when one visual beat can carry the
  context.
- Put nuance in the caption, storyboard, visual prompt, or facial expressions,
  not in dense on-slide text.
- If the Golden Theme only works when the slide has heavy explanatory copy,
  the visual beat is not strong enough yet.

## Variant Gate

Before proceeding with any carousel concept, create 5-10 distinct variations
from different creative lenses. Treat these as independent agent passes, not
tiny wording tweaks.

This is a hard gate. Do not give the creator a final recommendation, proceed to
carousel packaging, or generate images from a single hand-picked idea. If the
creator asks "what should we do next?", first run the variant tournament and
then report the winner with evidence.

Each variation must include:

- universal relationship truth;
- Aachu-specific spark;
- concrete proof beat;
- Zuv's active love, care, patience, or choosing-her role;
- save/share thesis;
- a 30-point Golden Theme score.

Score every variation using the review rubric below. The final selector must
compare all scored options and choose the highest-rated concept. Do not proceed
with a carousel direction unless the selected variation scores 28/30 or higher.

If no variation reaches 28/30:

1. identify the weakest scoring dimension;
2. repair the top 2-3 candidates;
3. rescore them;
4. only then choose a winner.

When choosing a winner, pick the highest-scoring concept. If scores tie, prefer
the one with the strongest universal hook, clearest Zuv emotional role, and
most drawable proof beat.

## Stage-Scene Gate

The tournament is not complete when a candidate has good slide copy. After
Layer E and before recommending a winner, convert each serious finalist into a
storyboard-first staged sequence. The deck must feel like a tiny stage scene or
short film: action, reaction, eye-line, hands, distance, object movement,
silence, consequence, reversal, and payoff.

Hard rule: text completes the scene; text must not carry the scene. If the
route only works because the poster line explains it, return REPAIR even when
the numeric Golden Theme score is 28/30+. Do not approve quote-card visuals,
generic couple poses, "Aachu/Zuv standing beside the line," or concept tables
as carousel direction.

The winning concept must carry the Calm Enough For Your Chaos learning into the
visual screen: every slide should prove universal truth -> Aachu/Zuv proof ->
Zuv active care -> tender thesis through visible behavior, not decoration. If a
planned slide cannot show that emotional machine, or if a lower-scored/risky
visual option leaks into the final plan, repair before packaging or generation.

Required tournament record:

- candidate table with 5-10 options and scores;
- selector verdict explaining why the winner beat the runners-up;
- explicit note showing how the winner uses the Calm Enough For Your Chaos
  first-principles analysis;
- explicit note showing which memory-ledger lanes were excluded or avoided;
- explicit note showing the story-director hook/bridge/ending check passed;
- explicit GO / REPAIR / STOP decision;
- if GO, the final public slide-copy direction;
- if REPAIR or STOP, do not create the carousel package yet.

After the creator confirms that final copy is approved or closed, the next
mandatory step is not prompt writing or image generation. Enter the Post-Copy
Visual Creative Room from
`agents/carousel-post-copy-visual-room-orchestrator.md`, write
`post-copy-visual-room.json`, and require GO before visual-debate,
visual-plan-quality, prompt-pack, or image handoff can pass.

## Creator Jam Response Contract

When the creator asks to "jam", "brainstorm", "ideate", "pick today's
carousel", "what should we do next", or otherwise discuss a next illustrated
carousel idea, start directly from this golden-theme workflow.

Do not offer the generic visual companion, browser mockup, design-doc flow, or
spec approval gate for carousel ideation. Those are not the creator-facing
answer. The response should be compact and useful:

1. acknowledge the jam;
2. use the Calm Enough For Your Chaos theme, full viral-theme analysis, and
   `memory/semantic/carousel-idea-preferences.md` as source memory;
3. exclude recently recommended, rejected, packaged, and cooled-down lanes
   unless the creator explicitly asks to revisit them;
4. ask one practical context question only if the moment/photo/constraint is
   missing;
5. run the golden-theme variant tournament when enough context exists;
6. name the winner, score, selector rationale, avoided lanes, and GO / REPAIR
   / STOP decision;
7. update the persistent idea ledger after the creator accepts, rejects, or
   cools down a concept.

The goal is momentum: give the creator the strongest idea surface first, then
package or generate only after a 28/30+ concept is selected.

## Hard Fails

Reject or rewrite the idea if:

- The hook is mostly about an object: "the red bag was never just a bag."
- The caption only describes the photo, place, outfit, or aesthetic.
- The hook is mostly about taking photos, looking beautiful, glowing, or
  waiting for one more picture without a deeper relationship tension.
- The carousel needs private context before the viewer understands it.
- Aachu/Zuv appear only as models inside a pretty travel frame.
- One partner's role is only "she looks good" and the other's role is only
  "he waits/holds things"; this is too aesthetic-first for the golden theme.
- The final slide says the same thing as the first slide with softer wording.
- The emotional payoff is generic romance: "you are my home" without a specific
  Aachu/Zuv proof trail.

## Better Rewrite Pattern

When an idea is too object-first, convert it like this:

```text
Object-first: The red bag was never just a bag.

Universal theme: Some people bring the main-character energy.
Specific proof: Aachu brings color, drama, and one more photo.
Zuv role: Zuv quietly matches her pace and makes it feel safe.
Payoff: Maybe love is not matching outfits. Maybe it is matching someone's
energy without asking them to shrink it.
```

This does not mean using that exact copy. It means rescuing the concept by
moving from object to relationship truth.

## Five-Slide Map

The current C-layer supports 4 or 5 slides. For the default 5-slide format,
compress the golden seven-beat structure into this map:

1. Universal hook: one swipe-stopping relationship truth.
2. Aachu-specific reveal: her version of the truth.
3. Concrete proof: a drawable behavior, object, or ritual.
4. Zuv role: what his calm does for the relationship.
5. Save/share thesis: the tender truth underneath the joke.

For 4 slides, merge slides 2 and 3. Never remove the Zuv role or the final
thesis.

## Caption Formula

Use this shape:

```text
universal relationship truth.

aachu-specific proof.
zuv-specific response.

tiny emotional turn.

send/save-worthy thesis.
```

Keep it short, native, and conversational. Hinglish belongs where it makes the
relationship feel more real, not as decoration.
