# @a.storyof.two — Voice, Aesthetic & Content Guide
# This file is passed to every analysis and writing agent as the channel reference.

## Who created this channel
Anchal Raina Sharma.
Documenting the shared story of Himanshu + Anchal — their life together,
travels, milestones, everyday moments, and the texture of their relationship.

## What this channel is about
Not a lifestyle brand. Not a travel page. A personal archive.
A.storyof.two is about **two people building a life together** — captured authentically.
The content is warm, intimate, visually rich, and emotionally honest.

## Aesthetic signature
- Warm color tones — golden hour, natural light, soft filters
- Couple-centric framing — togetherness is the visual theme
- Mix of candid and composed — real moments alongside intentional shots
- Travel as a recurring backdrop — new places as the setting for their story
- Everyday beauty — ordinary moments made to feel significant

## Content pillars
1. **Travel moments** — trips, new cities, experiences together
2. **Relationship milestones** — anniversaries, firsts, celebrations
3. **Daily life** — ordinary days, home, routines that feel extraordinary
4. **Shared experiences** — food, activities, things they do together
5. **Reflections** — captions that articulate what a moment meant

## Caption style
- Personal and warm — speaks directly from Anchal's perspective
- Storytelling over descriptions — captions explain the *feeling*, not just the scene
- Often poetic or lyrical — short lines, white space, emotional weight
- Hashtags: relationship-focused, travel tags, couple content communities

## Public naming rule
- Do not force Aachu/Zuv, Anchal/Himanshu, or other private-name mentions into
  every carousel slide or caption.
- Names are useful as internal identity anchors for likeness, role, and prompt
  consistency. Public copy should default to "she", "he", "we", or relationship
  roles unless the story naturally needs names.
- Golden-theme proof can come from behavior, posture, care, expression, and
  objects. It does not require naming both people in the visible copy.
- When the creator gives a new copy/voice preference in chat, save it to the
  relevant config or memory file instead of leaving it only in conversation.

## What to look for in analysis
- Which content types get the most engagement?
- What emotional tones perform best?
- Which travel destinations or themes resonate most?
- Are there content gaps — things Anchal + Himanshu do that aren't captured?
- What's the posting cadence and does it affect reach?
- Which captions get the most saves/shares (emotional resonance signal)?

## Tone for analysis outputs
When writing reports or insights about this channel:
- Respectful and warm — this is personal content about real people
- Specific — cite actual posts, captions, patterns observed
- Forward-looking — insights should suggest what to explore next, not just what happened
- Never clinical — this is a love story, treat it as such
