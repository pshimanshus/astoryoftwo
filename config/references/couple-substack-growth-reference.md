# Couple Substack Growth Reference

Use this reference when packaging @a.storyof.two love/couple essays for
Substack.

## Source Notes

- Substack can publish text, audio, and podcast posts. Podcast episodes can have
  corresponding Substack posts, and creators can upload/record audio, add show
  notes, and generate transcripts. Source:
  https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037462092-How-do-I-create-and-publish-a-podcast-on-Substack
- Substack voiceover lets subscribers read and listen in one post; one
  voiceover appears at the top of the post. Source:
  https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/7265784112916-How-do-I-add-a-voiceover-to-my-Substack-post
- Substack feature guidance emphasizes clear topical depth, regular posting,
  and reader engagement. Source:
  https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037455112-How-do-I-get-featured-on-Substack
- Substack SEO guidance emphasizes strengthening search presence and inbound
  links. Source:
  https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407702258836-How-can-I-optimize-my-Substack-publication-for-SEO
- Pew's 2026 newsletter report found that newsletters are a niche but familiar
  pathway and many readers do not read most newsletters they receive. Treat the
  title, preview, and first screen as critical. Source:
  https://www.pewresearch.org/journalism/2026/02/19/email-newsletters-as-a-source-of-news/

## Growth Pattern

1. **Inbox first.** The title and preview must be understandable without opening
   the post.
2. **First screen pays off.** The first screen should contain the hook, emotional
   thesis, and hero image.
3. **Images are story proof.** Use carousel images to deepen the feeling, not to
   decorate a block of text.
4. **Readable on email.** Prefer short sections, whitespace, and clean paragraph
   rhythm.
5. **One emotional promise.** A reader should know what feeling they will get:
   recognition, softness, laughter, ache, memory, or reassurance.
6. **One comment prompt.** End with a question readers can answer from their own
   relationship.
7. **One Notes excerpt.** Extract a short line that can travel on Substack Notes
   without context.

## Title Rules

Strong title patterns:

- Exact emotional hook: `He Didn't Marry Peace`
- Relationship thesis: `Calm Enough For My Chaos`
- Concrete object as love proof: `The Kind Of Love That Holds Your Shoes`

Avoid:

- vague poetic titles with no relationship signal;
- titles that sound like advice columns;
- titles that center the production process.

## Preview Text

Use one sentence that extends the title.

Examples:

- `A small essay on dramatic love, quiet care, and the person who learns your weather.`
- `On crying while saying nothing happened, leaving without shoes, and being loved anyway.`

## Article Body Rules

- Start with a sentence that can stand alone.
- Move from funny recognition to emotional safety.
- Use private details only when they make the universal truth stronger.
- Prefer "this is what love felt like" over "this is what love means."
- Keep sections skimmable but not listicle-like.
- Do not over-explain the joke.

## Image Placement Rules

- Hero image should appear before or immediately after the first short opening.
- Proof images should follow the section they prove.
- Final image should appear near the final thesis, not buried in the middle.
- Every image must have alt text.
- Do not use all carousel images automatically; use the images that serve the
  article's rhythm.

## Reader Prompt Patterns

- `Which one are you in love: the chaos, the calm, or somehow both?`
- `Who in your life learned your weather?`
- `Send this to the person who knows where the shoes are.`

## Final Audit

Before publishing, answer:

- Would a stranger understand the relationship truth from the title and preview?
- Would a couple want to send this to each other?
- Does the first image make the article feel alive?
- Is the ending more specific than "love is home"?
- Is there a reason to comment, restack, or save?
