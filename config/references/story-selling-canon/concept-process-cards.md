# Concept Process Cards

## Purpose

Use one card as the operating process for a carousel concept, article angle, or
story repair. Do not stack many cards at once. Pick the one that best matches
the supplied moment, then score the result with `rubric.md`.

Each card is a derived pattern. It is not copied from a source text.

## Card 01 - Obstacle To Care

- best_for: carousel, article, reel script
- source_patterns: `writers-digest-romance-conflict`,
  `storygrid-love-genre`, `gutenberg-austen-pride-prejudice`
- confidence: 0.86
- process:
  1. Name the tiny emotional obstacle.
  2. Show why it matters to the reader.
  3. Let Aachu reveal the feeling through behavior.
  4. Let Zuv answer with care, not a speech.
  5. End on what love did with the obstacle.
- a_story_of_two_filter: Zuv must make the moment easier without making Aachu
  smaller.

## Card 02 - Misread To Tender Truth

- best_for: carousel, Substack essay
- source_patterns: `gutenberg-austen-pride-prejudice`,
  `pixar-storytelling`
- confidence: 0.84
- process:
  1. Open with what the moment looked like.
  2. Reveal what it actually meant emotionally.
  3. Add one specific proof beat.
  4. Reverse the reader's first assumption.
  5. Close with the truth the couple earned.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The reversal must make both Aachu and Zuv more lovable.

## Card 03 - Dramatic Want, Quiet Need

- best_for: funny-to-soft carousel
- source_patterns: `pixar-storytelling`, `save-the-cat-beats`
- confidence: 0.82
- process:
  1. Let Aachu want something visible and specific.
  2. Find the hidden need underneath it.
  3. Put a small obstacle between want and need.
  4. Show Zuv noticing the need.
  5. End with the need being met, not merely the want.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The want can be comic; the need must be emotionally
  respectful.

## Card 04 - Public Scene, Private Meaning

- best_for: travel photos, wedding scenes, restaurant moments
- source_patterns: `loc-public-domain-films`, `film-scene-engine`,
  `animalz-quality-content`
- confidence: 0.78
- process:
  1. Start with the public image everyone can see.
  2. Name the private feeling only the couple knows.
  3. Use one object, gesture, or glance as proof.
  4. Make Zuv's role visible in the frame.
  5. End with the private meaning becoming universal.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The photo aesthetic must become evidence for love,
  not the main claim.

## Card 05 - Banter To Belonging

- best_for: Hinglish captions, playful carousels, reels
- source_patterns: `copyblogger-story-that-sells`,
  `golden-viral-carousel-theme`, `reedsy-romance-guide`
- confidence: 0.85
- process:
  1. Open with a sendable joke about the couple dynamic.
  2. Escalate with one Aachu-specific behavior.
  3. Let the joke expose a real need.
  4. Give Zuv an active emotional response.
  5. Close with belonging, not punchline.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The humor must never make Aachu the problem.

## Card 06 - Delay The Confession

- best_for: romantic or reflective essays, proposal stories
- source_patterns: `jane-friedman-love-scene`,
  `storygrid-love-genre`, `gutenberg-bronte-jane-eyre`
- confidence: 0.8
- process:
  1. Identify the feeling that cannot be said directly.
  2. Show the feeling leaking through action.
  3. Add delay, distance, interruption, or shyness.
  4. Let one partner make the feeling safer.
  5. Earn the final line through release.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The ending must come from scene evidence, not a loose
  quote.

## Card 07 - Anti-Ideal To Real Love

- best_for: golden-theme carousel concepts
- source_patterns: `golden-viral-carousel-theme`,
  `storybrand-framework`, `animalz-quality-content`
- confidence: 0.9
- process:
  1. Reject the polished ideal of romance.
  2. Name the real relationship pattern.
  3. Prove it with one Aachu behavior.
  4. Prove Zuv's active steadiness.
  5. Land a tender acceptance thesis.
- a_story_of_two_filter: This is the default rescue card when an idea is too
  aesthetic-first.

## Card 08 - Small Ritual, Large Promise

- best_for: food, chai, packing, travel, daily-life details
- source_patterns: `reedsy-romance-guide`, `copyblogger-story-that-sells`
- confidence: 0.78
- process:
  1. Pick one repeatable ritual.
  2. Name the emotional promise inside it.
  3. Show the ritual under slight pressure.
  4. Let Zuv protect or participate in the promise.
  5. End with why small rituals become home.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The object is proof only when tied to emotional labor.

## Card 09 - Choice Under Pressure

- best_for: articles, serious carousels, milestone stories
- source_patterns: `storygrid-love-genre`, `save-the-cat-beats`
- confidence: 0.81
- process:
  1. State what would be easier to avoid.
  2. Show why the moment asks for a choice.
  3. Give Aachu a vulnerable desire or fear.
  4. Give Zuv a visible choice toward her.
  5. End with the cost and reward of choosing.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The choice must be specific enough to film or draw.

## Card 10 - Reader As Third Witness

- best_for: Substack, captions, Notes promo
- source_patterns: `storybrand-framework`, `animalz-quality-content`,
  `copyblogger-story-that-sells`
- confidence: 0.83
- process:
  1. Make the reader feel seen before describing the couple.
  2. Name the relationship desire or anxiety.
  3. Bring in Aachu/Zuv as proof.
  4. Translate the proof into a usable insight.
  5. Close with a question or line the reader wants to share.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The couple is evidence; the reader is the doorway.

## Card 11 - Visual Reversal

- best_for: image-led carousel concepts
- source_patterns: `loc-public-domain-films`, `pixar-storytelling`
- confidence: 0.77
- process:
  1. Choose a frame that seems to mean one thing.
  2. Add context that flips its emotional meaning.
  3. Show the hidden feeling in posture or spacing.
  4. Give Zuv a clear action inside the frame.
  5. End with the new meaning.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The reversal must be visible without dense slide copy.

## Card 12 - The Thing She Brings

- best_for: Aachu-led concepts
- source_patterns: `golden-viral-carousel-theme`, `reedsy-romance-guide`
- confidence: 0.86
- process:
  1. Name the energy Aachu brings into the relationship.
  2. Show why that energy could be misunderstood.
  3. Show the exact proof of its beauty.
  4. Show Zuv receiving it as love.
  5. End with acceptance, not correction.
- a_story_of_two_filter: Aachu's intensity must become value, not burden.

## Card 13 - The Way He Stays

- best_for: Zuv-led concepts
- source_patterns: `storygrid-love-genre`,
  `golden-viral-carousel-theme`
- confidence: 0.84
- process:
  1. Name a moment where staying would be invisible.
  2. Give the reader a reason to care.
  3. Show Aachu's emotion honestly.
  4. Show Zuv's steadiness as a concrete action.
  5. End with the kind of love that stays awake.
- a_story_of_two_filter: Zuv must act; "he waits" is not enough by itself.

## Card 14 - Want Versus Need

- best_for: concept repair
- source_patterns: `pixar-storytelling`, `save-the-cat-beats`
- confidence: 0.79
- process:
  1. Write the visible want.
  2. Write the emotional need underneath.
  3. Remove any detail that does not prove the need.
  4. Build scenes around the need being noticed.
  5. Pay off the need in the final beat.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The hidden need must be affectionate and specific to
  the couple.

## Card 15 - Proof Before Poetry

- best_for: weak final lines, generic captions
- source_patterns: `animalz-quality-content`, `copyblogger-story-that-sells`
- confidence: 0.82
- process:
  1. Delete the abstract romantic line.
  2. List the three most concrete behaviors.
  3. Pick the one behavior with emotional cost.
  4. Build the payoff from that behavior.
  5. Reintroduce poetry only after proof.
- a_story_of_two_filter: No final quote unless the scene has earned it.

## Card 16 - Scene Before Summary

- best_for: article openings and carousel hooks
- source_patterns: `copyblogger-story-that-sells`,
  `jane-friedman-love-scene`
- confidence: 0.78
- process:
  1. Start inside the moment.
  2. Make the emotional friction legible.
  3. Delay explanation until after the reader feels it.
  4. Add one line of interpretation.
  5. Move quickly to proof.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The scene must still point to a universal love truth.

## Card 17 - The Object Is A Receipt

- best_for: object-heavy photos
- source_patterns: `golden-viral-carousel-theme`,
  `animalz-quality-content`
- confidence: 0.88
- process:
  1. Identify the object the idea is leaning on.
  2. Ask what relationship truth the object proves.
  3. Move that truth to the hook.
  4. Use the object as one receipt.
  5. End on the relationship, not the object.
- a_story_of_two_filter: Bags, shoes, flowers, food, and views are receipts
  only.

## Card 18 - Softness After Performance

- best_for: wedding, glam, travel, photo-session moments
- source_patterns: `loc-public-domain-films`, `jane-friedman-love-scene`
- confidence: 0.76
- process:
  1. Show the performative surface.
  2. Reveal the private softness after it.
  3. Add a detail only care would notice.
  4. Let Zuv guard the softness.
  5. Close with love after the pose.
- a_story_of_two_filter: Beauty is allowed, but the private care must be the
  point.

## Card 19 - Almost Too Much, Exactly Enough

- best_for: expressive Aachu stories
- source_patterns: `golden-viral-carousel-theme`,
  `reedsy-romance-guide`
- confidence: 0.87
- process:
  1. Name the trait that might be called "too much."
  2. Show the trait as life, color, or courage.
  3. Add the moment it needs safety.
  4. Show Zuv making room for all of it.
  5. End with love as enough-space.
- a_story_of_two_filter: Never frame her as an inconvenience.

## Card 20 - Saveable Lesson From One Scene

- best_for: Substack, captions, high-share carousels
- source_patterns: `storybrand-framework`,
  `copyblogger-story-that-sells`, `animalz-quality-content`
- confidence: 0.81
- process:
  1. Pick one scene with friction.
  2. Extract one relationship lesson.
  3. Show the proof before naming the lesson.
  4. Make the lesson useful to the reader.
  5. End with a comment or send prompt.
- a_story_of_two_filter: The lesson must feel discovered through Aachu/Zuv, not
  pasted onto them.
