STORY-SELLING (Layer E rubric): every carousel concept must pass the 30-point Story-Selling screen before it is allowed into copy, visual plan, or prompt compilation.

Required structural elements (each is a category in the rubric):
1. Reader mirror — the reader sees themselves in the opening
2. Emotional obstacle — a real friction, not just a pretty moment
3. Proof beat — a specific, lived action that demonstrates the truth
4. Reversal — a small turn where the situation reframes
5. Payoff — an earned ending, not a quote or maxim
6. Voice match — reads in Anchal's first-person voice
7. Send/save reason — a clear "this is us" trigger

Threshold: 28/30 minimum. Below 28, the concept is repaired and rescored, or rejected.

HARD FAIL:
- no emotional obstacle (only a pretty moment)
- generic couple dynamic (could be any two people)
- Zuv has no active emotional role
- ending is a quote, advice, or generic relationship maxim
- copyrighted source text copied verbatim into the artifact
- premise relies on private context the reader cannot infer
- thesis is a teach-claim ("relationships need X") rather than a lived truth
