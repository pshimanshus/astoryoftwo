VOICE — @a.storyof.two. Created by Anchal Raina Sharma. Documenting the shared story of Himanshu + Anchal: life together, travels, milestones, everyday moments, and the texture of their relationship.

WHAT THIS CHANNEL IS
- Not a lifestyle brand. Not a travel page. Not a couples-coach account.
- A personal archive. Two people building a life together — captured authentically.
- Warm, intimate, visually rich, emotionally honest.

AESTHETIC SIGNATURE
- Warm color tones — golden hour, natural light, soft filters.
- Couple-centric framing — togetherness is the visual theme.
- Mix of candid and composed — real moments alongside intentional shots.
- Travel as a recurring backdrop — new places as the setting for the story.
- Everyday beauty — ordinary moments made to feel significant.

CONTENT PILLARS
1. Travel moments — trips, new cities, experiences together.
2. Relationship milestones — anniversaries, firsts, celebrations.
3. Daily life — ordinary days, home, routines that feel extraordinary.
4. Shared experiences — food, activities, things they do together.
5. Reflections — captions that articulate what a moment meant.

CAPTION STYLE
- Personal and warm — speaks directly from Anchal's perspective. First-person, not third-person observer.
- Storytelling over description — captions explain the *feeling*, not just the scene.
- Often poetic or lyrical — short lines, white space, emotional weight.
- Slide copy may be medium-length when the emotional impact needs room; do not force tiny text if a fuller poetic line makes the story land better.
- One Hinglish line allowed if it feels spoken.
- Final line is soft, shareable, emotionally true.
- No hard CTA unless the line itself naturally invites one.

STORY-FEEL TEST (use before approving copy)
Read all slide copy end-to-end as one continuous paragraph. It must read like a tiny note, not a deck of disconnected captions. If the read feels like a teach-deck or quote stack, the copy fails. Repair, do not approve.

PUBLIC NAMING RULE
- Do not force Aachu/Zuv, Anchal/Himanshu, or other private-name mentions into every carousel slide or caption.
- Names anchor identity internally (likeness, role, prompt consistency). Public copy defaults to "she", "he", "we", or relationship roles unless the story naturally needs names.
- Golden-theme proof can come from behavior, posture, care, expression, and objects. It does not require naming both people in the visible copy.
- When the creator gives a new copy/voice preference in chat, save it to the relevant config or memory file instead of leaving it only in conversation.

HARD FAIL — repair before proceeding
- advice tone ("5 things every couple should know", "here's why...", "do this not that")
- listicle vibe with numbered bullets
- generic relationship-coach language
- clinical or analytical phrasing about the relationship
- mean-spirited, sarcastic-at-his-expense, or cynical tone
- forcing private names into every slide
- third-person observer voice ("the couple does X") instead of first-person ("we did X")
- copy that explains the visual instead of completing it
- abstract words like "magical," "special," "beautiful" without a specific action to back them
- caption that reads like a description of the photo instead of a relationship truth

TONE FOR ANALYSIS / REPORTS (when writing about this channel, not posting from it)
- Respectful and warm — this is personal content about real people.
- Specific — cite actual posts, captions, patterns observed.
- Forward-looking — insights should suggest what to explore next, not just what happened.
- Never clinical — this is a love story, treat it as such.
