RELATIONSHIP MOTION — carousel stories need visible relationship movement, not a
permanent Zuv-saves-Aachu pattern.

CREATOR CORRECTION
- Do not build every carousel as "Aachu is the problem and Zuv admires,
  handles, saves, understands, or patiently absorbs her."
- Zuv should do something only when the story is genuinely his beat.
- The gate is not "did Zuv actively save/handle/understand Aachu?"
- The gate is: does the post create a public relationship mirror with enough
  behavior, receipts, contradiction, rhythm, or situation to feel real?

VALID MOTION SOURCES
- Aachu doing something and the story revealing her love.
- Zuv doing something when it is genuinely his beat.
- Both doing the same stupid, sweet, guilty, chaotic, or tender thing together.
- A generic couple pattern where exact Aachu/Zuv identity matters less than an
  instantly sendable "this is us" mirror.
- No heroic actor at all: the win can be the shared rhythm, repeated phrase,
  contradiction, misunderstanding, object movement, or situation.

APPLICATION
- Keep the gold-carousel bar: public contradiction, concrete receipts,
  emotional turn, and send/save/tag behavior.
- Replace forced "active Zuv role" checks with "relationship motion" or
  "relevant partner role" checks.
- Do not force Aachu and Zuv into every beat if the scene works better as one
  person, both people, partial hands/objects, or a generic couple mirror.
- Avoid score inflation for concepts that mechanically add Aachu proof plus Zuv
  care. A generic or mutual route may pass when it is more sendable, more
  physical, and more publicly recognizable.

HARD FAIL
- Any concept that depends on Zuv as the default handler/rescuer/caretaker.
- Any concept that makes Aachu the permanent problem to be endured.
- Any scoring rubric that fails a route only because Zuv is not the active
  caretaker, when relationship motion is otherwise clear.
