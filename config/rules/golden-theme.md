GOLDEN THEME (Calm Enough For Your Chaos machine): every carousel must follow the four-beat spine.

1. UNIVERSAL RELATIONSHIP TRUTH — a feeling many couples recognize (not a couple-coach claim).
2. AACHU/ZUV PROOF — that universal truth proven by a specific lived moment between the two of them. Photos, places, outfits, and objects are evidence, not the premise.
3. ZUV ACTIVE CARE — Zuv must have an active emotional role in the scene. He notices, acts, comforts, or chooses something. Not passive backdrop.
4. TENDER THESIS — the carousel lands on an earned tender line, not a quote, not advice.

Scoring: 30-point Golden Theme rubric. Threshold is 28/30. Below 28, the concept is repaired and rescored or rejected.

HARD FAIL:
- no universal truth — concept reads as a private inside joke only
- only a pretty moment without an emotional turn
- generic couple dynamic with no Aachu/Zuv specificity
- Zuv passive, decorative, or absent from the emotional turn
- ending is a quote, a generic relationship maxim, or a piece of advice
- text spine carries the meaning while the visuals are interchangeable poster art
- visuals only work because the text says so (storyboard-first means the visual action must prove the truth even if the text is hidden)
